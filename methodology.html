The Blunt Diagnosis

Why heuristic toy search fails

Failure Mode 1

Small Circuit Illusions

Searching N≤3 qubit circuits and arbitrary oracle puzzles consistently rediscovers trivial parity tricks (like Bernstein-Vazirani) or overfits to custom bitwise secret-finding oracles that lack a natural scalable problem family.

Resolution Require scalable algebraic or combinatorial problem families with known classical hardness barriers.
Failure Mode 2

Vanity Benchmarking

Treating success_rate ≥ 1.0 on tiny instances as "solved novel algorithms" creates the illusion of progress without testing true asymptotic complexity or classical resistance.

Resolution Record finite experiments as negative results until explicit asymptotic proof obligations are satisfied.
Failure Mode 3

Standalone Unitary Synthesis

State preparation and unitary synthesis are useful subroutines only when tied to a larger algorithmic mechanism; as standalone discovery targets they have a low ceiling.

Resolution Anchor search in representation-theoretic reductions (Racah coefficients, commutants, Fourier sampling).

Defense In Depth

Classical attack matrix

01
Linear Information Set Decoding (ISD)

Attacks code equivalence, permutation invariants, and syndrome spaces using Prange, Stern, and hull projector baselines.

02
Weisfeiler-Leman & Tensor Invariants

Evaluates higher-order graphlet color refinement and individualization tensor observables for isomorphism candidates.

03
Fourier & Low-Degree Polynomial Contractions

Deconstructs phase shifts, boolean hidden shifts, and bounded-degree correlation channels in polynomial classical time.

04
Lattice CVP & BDD Reductions

Applies LLL and BKZ reduction on subset-sum, carry slice lattices, and modular recurrence structures.

Theoretical Grounding

High-upside search agendas

Agenda 1

Nonabelian Coset States

Collective POVMs and commutant moment analysis across multiple coset state copies in symmetric groups S_n and hyperoctahedral groups B_n.

Active Theorem Exact surface reduction and high-multiplicity Racah intertwiners.
Agenda 2

Dihedral HSP & Subset Sum

Testing whether non-trivial correlation between affine carry relations and quantum walk fibers can improve Kuperberg sieve bounds.

Active Theorem Carry ANF degree invariance and 4-block k-sum non-collapse.
Agenda 3

Self-Dual Wreath Codes

Self-dual linear code equivalence under wreath product automorphisms and tetrahedral word map projections.

Active Theorem Orientation retention theorem and sign-syndrome decoupling.

Local Reproduction

Running experiments & audits

  1. Run Dequantization Audit Executes all 1,146 classical attack rows against candidate registries:
    python3 qsearch.py dequantize
  2. Validate Full Registry Verifies consistency across all 659 experiments, results, and proof edges:
    python3 qsearch.py validate
  3. Rebuild Public Progress Snapshot Regenerates the live JSON telemetry powering the dashboard:
    python3 tools/build_progress_snapshot.py
  4. Run Single Experiment Subcommand Executes any individual theorem module (e.g. coset hidden involution):
    python3 qsearch.py coset-hidden-involution-binary-identification-self-reduction
