## A Real Receiver, Not A Chosen Oracle

Original even native level L=2r, q=3^r, supplies independent qutrits

    (|0>+chi_q(<a,s>)|1>+chi_q(<c,s>)|2>)/sqrt3,
    a,c IID uniform in Z_q^n, unknown shared s in Z_q^n.

Embed the three digits as(0,0),(1,0),(0,1) in two r-trit registers.
The embedding is the fixed two-qutrit permutation20<->01 on the occupied
last digits; other new trits start clean. Apply an inverse QFT over Z_q to
each register, then measure EVERY output. No unknown-state inverse, cloning,
postselection, field-shadow source or frequency-fiber erasure is used.

An explicit gate recipe has1+2*(r+r*(r-1)/2+floor(r/2)) one/two-qutrit gates:
local inverse F3 gates, controlled phases with denominators3^k, digit
reversal, and the embedding permutation. Each qutrit can be encoded in two
qubits; these constant-size gates have ordinary precision-dependent gate
synthesis costs. Native hardware/qubit compilation is not performed here.
The r^2 recipe does not enumerate q^2 outcomes. Dense gate replay is strictly
bounded to six qutrits and independently compared with full-root DFTs.
For M readouts, total gate-approximation error must be charged, for example
by summing per-gate errors. Original native-source acquisition remains a
separate cost, including any binary-to-qutrit conversion.

The public classical record is exactly

    y1=<a,s>+e1 modq, y2=<c,s>+e2 modq,
    nu_q(e1,e2)=|1+chi_q(e1)+chi_q(e2)|^2/(3*q^2).

For EACH fixed a,c,s this error law is the same. Fresh qutrits have independent
error PAIRS. Within a pair the errors are NOT independent. This is a quantum
measurement followed by classical processing; sampling nu with a planted
secret for calibration is not a classical simulator of the unknown states.

## Scalar Linear Attacks: Complete Classification

Take a labels-only scalar linear compression of independent original pairs,
E=sum_i(w_i1*e_i1+w_i2*e_i2) modq. Its Fourier coefficient at t is

    product_i nu_hat(t*w_i1,t*w_i2).

This gives a compact complete classification:

- All weights zero: identically zero, with zero linear mean.
- All weights nonunits: uniform on the image subgroup generated by them.
  The linear mean lies in the same subgroup; this scalar carries no secret
  information. Its nonzero subgroup characters are not a recovery signal.
- At least one unit coordinate: normalize all pairs by one such unit kappa.
  If every nonzero normalized pair lies in Delta, only dual frequencies
  +/-kappa^(-1) survive, with bias3^(-w), w=active ORIGINAL qutrits.
- Otherwise the scalar error is exactly uniform modulo q.

Generic modular Gaussian elimination uses coefficients outside these common
root directions and can destroy its scalar signal completely. Signed sums
preserve a mode, but its amplitude is multiplied; estimating a fixed
character to signal-scale accuracy by independent bounded observations has
the usual inverse-square-bias sampling scale9^w. This is a moment-estimation
cost, NOT a general sample lower bound for all nonlinear decoders.

Weights must be independent of outcomes. Label-only BKW bucketing is legal;
outcome-dependent selection needs a different conditional-source analysis.
When an original record is reused, count its NET paired weights, not an
imaginary fresh error at every intermediate merge.

For M IID marginal labels, an exact collision in k coordinates has probability
at most min(1,binom(M,2)/q^k). Matching only low digits is not full-modulus
elimination. Disjoint leaves are needed to multiply independent noise laws.
The ledger charges both collision supply and surviving Fourier amplitude.

## Constructive Classical Gaussian Collimation

The same low-field kernel idea can reduce an ALREADY MEASURED marginal.
Given n+1 independent tagged equations at q>=9, GF3 elimination supplies a
nonzero kernel word. Represent its coefficients by integer0,+1,-1, NOT
arbitrary modular inverses. Then

    A=sum_i w_i*a_i modq is componentwise divisible by3,
    Y=sum_i w_i*y_i modq, c=Y mod3,
    new a=A/3, new y=floor(Y/3), new modulus=q/3.

Each input noise has first-character amplitude alpha_i and known phase phi_i.
Their signed convolution has amplitude alpha=product_(w_i!=0) alpha_i and
phase phi=sum_i w_i*phi_i. Its residue modulo3 is uniform, independently
of the secret, because the linear mean is divisible by3. CONDITIONAL on c,
the new noise law has the same first-character form with

    new amplitude=alpha, new phase=phi-2*pi*c/q.

Thus ALL three residues are retained as exact rational phase tags. There is
no1/3 postselection loss. The output label is uniform at the smaller modulus
conditional on low parent labels: at least one selected integer coefficient
is a unit, and untouched high label lifts are uniform. Fresh disjoint ancestor
trees are essential; this is not a multiple-output independence theorem.

`collimate_tagged` implements this reduction, tracks original record IDs,
charges even inactive parent inputs, and rejects shared ancestor batches.
Visibility MULTIPLIES instead of staying at unit quantum phase visibility.
There is no claim that every branch has support>=2: low-zero columns can
give weight-one merges, whose occurrence/acquisition costs must be charged.

The live q81,n2 tree charges27 original qutrits through81->27->9->3.
It ends with first-character amplitude1/6561, moment-estimation scale43,046,721,
and, in this preselected calibration, ZERO output frequency. That branch
contains no secret signal at all; a nonzero Fourier bias by itself is not
algorithmic progress. This concerns the returned compressed equation, not
all retained ancestor records, tags, nonlinear inference or coherent inputs.

## An Actual Decoder Under A Stronger Access Model

Subtract the known lower-digit phase from the empirical mean. Higher digits

This is an implemented polynomial estimator, not all-secret ML enumeration.

## Native Held-Out Verifier, Not A Search Algorithm

For a candidate t, average the per-qutrit score

    cos(2*pi*(y1-a.t)/q) + cos(2*pi*(y2-c.t)/q)
      + cos(2*pi*((y1-y2)-(a-c).t)/q).

It lies in[-3/2,3]. Its native IID expectation is1 at the true secret and0
at ANY fixed wrong secret, including nonprimitive differences. Threshold1/2
has each error bounded by exp(-2*M/81). T preselected wrong candidates have
false-acceptance union bound min(1,T*exp(-2*M/81)). These bounds require
candidates independent of the HELD-OUT labels and outcomes; training/selection
on the same batch is not certified. The three character terms are correlated
features of one sample, not independent tests.

This verifies a shortlist cheaply in O(M*n) modular operations plus root
precision costs; it does NOT generate that shortlist. With1,024 native
records, live n2/r8,n4/r16,n8/r32 controls give true scores approximately
1.1025,0.9998,0.9975 and fixed wrong scores-0.0103,-0.0465,0.0379.
Simulated seeds do not prove the theorem or supply a native recovery result.

There is NO asymptotic sample obstacle for THIS receiver if a polynomial
surplus is allowed: union over true rejection and all q^n-1 wrong trials
gives failure<=q^n*exp(-2*M/81). The conservative integer choice
M=41*(2*n*r+kappa) makes the threshold uniquely select the true secret with
probability>=1-2^(-kappa). This is only an identifiability theorem with an
exponential reference search, not an implemented decoder. It changes the
research target: computational candidate generation is the unresolved issue,
not squeezing measurements to entropy-width sample counts. Prior near-entropy
screens do not rule out a polynomial-sample local-readout algorithm.

## Published Techniques And Transfer Boundaries

[Brakerski, Kirshanova, Stehle and Wen, Section4/Theorem4](https://www.iacr.org/archive/pkc2018/10770194/10770194.pdf)
already use Fourier readout to turn Gaussian EDCP into Gaussian-noisy linear
samples. Here the finite three-point envelope gives a non-Gaussian paired
q-wide law. Their Gaussian parameter reduction and worst-case implications
do NOT automatically apply to this law. No novelty claim for Fourier readout.

[Akavia, Sections3-4/Algorithm5/Theorem15](https://people.csail.mit.edu/akavia/FQSFT.pdf)
supplies significant-Fourier recovery under function-evaluation access with
structured query sets. A one-sparse conditional expectation does not give
those evaluations from IID native records. Neither that theorem nor
Goldreich--Levin supplies free chosen labels; the missing-access cost is a
research obligation rather than a dequantization verdict.

## Research Decision And Falsifiers

- Native random-label candidate generation remains the bottleneck. Focus
  on the joint paired law, structured dual coefficients or a coherent source-
  valid compiler for informative frequency schedules, not a generic scalar
  Gaussian solver or more final-field shadow tests.
- An actual gate replay or original-ring Born distribution disagreeing with
  nu falsifies the receiver. Root precision and all outcomes must be kept.
- A scalar classification disagreement falsifies its exact support proof.
  The invertible joint countercontrol prevents overclaiming a classical no-go.
- A tagged collimation disagreement, missing residue, reused ancestor or
  apparent unit visibility after noisy merging falsifies the source ledger.
- Native chosen-label compilation costs, not conditional decoder success,
  determine whether the positive radix control could become an algorithm.
- An informative shortlist must survive independent held-out data; a good
  planted score, uncharged label selection or exhaustive ML is not discovery.
- Full original quantum states, alternative receivers, more samples and
  nonlinear full-transcript classical inference remain open.
