# Soft Frequency Constraints Do Not Make This Local Parent A Fast Eraser

LOCAL DERIVATION / REVIEW PENDING. An actual alternative to the frozen
fiber walk, with a state-specific bound. Not a novelty claim or a lower
bound against all quantum algorithms, Hamiltonians or encodings.

## Constructive Proposal

Use the original native source at q=3^r with public
F(x)=sum_i(0,a_i,c_i)[x_i], x in F3^M. For each occupied full frequency y,
seek coherent preparation of v_y=C_y^(-1/2) sum_(F(x)=y)|x> from the known
uniform word state u=3^(-M/2) sum_x|x>. Its clean inverse could erase the
word register while preserving y. This is one sufficient receiver route,
not a requirement for every decoder.

Allow INTERMEDIATE frequency violations. Give marked words F(x)=y amplitude
weight1 and unmarked words amplitude weight t=exp(-beta/2),0<=t<=1.
The global coherent Gibbs state is

    g_t=(sum_marked|x>+t*sum_unmarked|x>)/sqrt(C_y+(D-C_y)*t^2), D=3^M.

For a k-coordinate block B, hold other coordinates fixed. Among its S=3^k
assignments let r be marked. Define Q_(B,t) to project onto the normalized
conditional vector with amplitudes1 on marked and t on unmarked. There is
ONE such rank-one projection in EACH outside-coordinate class. At t=0 an
entirely unmarked class uses its continuous-limit uniform projector.
H_(B,t)=I-Q_(B,t) is an orthogonal projection. Convex mixtures are PSD,
have norm<=1 and annihilate g_t. At t>0 this is the symmetric parent of the
ordinary conditional heat bath for probabilities proportional to1 or t^2.

Unlike the previous exact-fiber walk, these parents can change F. A cheap
transition exists: enumerate ALL S local assignments, compute full F=y,
prepare their normalized amplitude weights with a finite conditional
rotation program and reverse its arithmetic scratch. Conjugating a known-
zero reflection implements the conditional projector. Costs include S
evaluations and weight preparation precision; no whole-fiber count or
preparation oracle is granted. At k=O(log n), M,q=poly(n), this is polynomial
per local call. A random block does not enumerate the block menu. Hardware
gate export and a global precision budget are not implemented.

## Why A Small Gap Alone Would Be The Wrong Argument

The earlier no-small-collision result makes low-temperature accepted words
nearly isolated. This can yield tiny gap modes contrasting different words
in the same fiber. Such modes might have little coupling to the particular
state path we care about. A gap upper bound alone does NOT prove slow
preparation or justify abandoning an arbitrary diabatic schedule.

Instead compare the ACTUAL evolved state to u.

In one S-word conditional class, exact direct projection gives

    ||H_(B,t) u_class||^2
       = r*(S-r)*(1-t)^2/[D*(r+(S-r)*t^2)].

This is zero for r=0 or r=S, using the stated continuous limit. For1<=r<S
it is at most r*(S-1)/D. Sum over classes: their marked counts total C_y.
Consequently, for EVERY public label matrix and EVERY block,

    ||H_(B,t) u|| <= sqrt(p_y*(S-1)), p_y=C_y/D.

No random-label, collision-distance, connectivity or spectral-gap premise
is needed. A convex mixture of blocks satisfies the same bound by the
triangle inequality. Blocks may depend on all labels and y. Different
temperatures and supports may be used throughout the schedule.

## Arbitrary Parent Schedules: Charge Action, Not Wall Time

Consider word-register unitary evolution starting at u, generated by

    H(time)=sum_l gamma_l(time)*H_(B_l,t_l)+lambda(time)*Pi_y.

Pi_y projects onto the target fiber. Allow signed coefficients, reversed
evolution, reheating, full-label/target-dependent schedules and arbitrary
block policies, all with support<=k. Scalar identity terms are irrelevant.
Define

    A=integral sum_l|gamma_l| dt, L=integral|lambda| dt,
    J=A*sqrt(3^k-1)+L.

The propagator identity against the constant reference u gives

    ||U(T)u-u|| <= integral ||H(time)u|| dt <= sqrt(p_y)*J,
    Pr[F(output)=y] <= min(1,p_y*(1+J)^2).

This bounds real state transfer, not an adiabatic runtime inferred from a
spectator gap. Large Hamiltonian coefficients increase action and do not
remove the resource cost. Random schedules satisfy the same bound per
realization with its charged action; expected action alone does not justify
inserting its mean into the quadratic expression.

Actual gate simulation errors add their WHOLE output trace-distance error
to the raw success bound. No postselected normalization or supplied exact
ground-state reflection is included. A Zeno measurement must charge an
implementation or receive a separate analysis; it is not free evolution.

## The Original Born-Weighted Target Is Still Expensive

The native source contains fiber y with weight p_y, not uniform weight over
the occupied frequency list. Put purity K=sum_y p_y^2. With a uniform
per-instance action cap, the complete source-weighted preparation success
obeys

    sum_y p_y Pr[F(output_y)=y] <= min(1,K*(1+J)^2).

For ALL IID uniform full native rows, every distinct word difference has a
unit coefficient, so Pr[F(x)=F(z)]=1/G, G=q^n. Therefore EXACTLY

    E_labels[K]=1/G+(1-1/G)/D.

Policies may depend on the labels: the pointwise bound is established BEFORE
averaging. For D>=G, k=O(log n) and polynomial A,L, population mean success
is at most poly(n)/q^n. Increasing the polynomial copy batch does not remove
the1/G term. Near-entropy source density is not a warm preparation of v_y.

If a clean inverse eraser has squared source-weighted vector error<=e^2,
its forward preparation has the SAME error by unitarity. Projection outside
the desired fiber has squared norm at most that error. Thus successful
erasure requires mean fiber success>=1-e^2. In this parent architecture,

    e^2 >= 1-min(1,(1+J)^2*(1/G+(1-1/G)/D)).

This is a necessary condition, not a sufficient erasure certificate. Fiber
membership alone does not verify uniform amplitudes, phases or clean work.

## Concrete Replay And A Counterexample To Overgeneralization

Use the SAME native n2/q9/M5/seed89881 labels as the frozen-walk audit.
Pick the largest occupied fiber using PUBLIC counts only in this bounded
reference. Exact rational parents are built for t=1,1/3,1/27,0 at support1,
and t=1/3 at support2. Every conditional class, parent matrix entry, Gibbs
null-vector and uniform-state residual is replayed independently. Capped
complete references are exponential and never labeled scalable compilers.

Finite matrix evolution checks a monotone cooling schedule and a signed,
reheating, support-changing schedule with an added marked projector. These
are NUMERICAL controls, not rigorous floating-point or asymptotic evidence.
A complete census of all81 native n1/q3/M2 label matrices separately checks
the exact expected purity formula. Large n/root action ledgers evaluate
the theorem without enumerating words, fibers or secrets.

A positive full-block control defines the explicit bisector
b=(u+v_y)/||u+v_y||. Its reflection2|b><b|-I maps u to v_y in action pi.
This is a conditional Gibbs projector at
t=sqrt(p_y)/(1+sqrt(p_y)); its whole-word table costs3^M. It proves that
neither short physical time nor a Hamiltonian description by itself is
excluded. The obstacle is the cheap SMALL-BLOCK parent architecture.
A genuine polynomial-description global operation would be a real escape.

## Research Decision And Critique

Do not pursue binary-energy small-block coherent Gibbs cooling as a
polynomial-action native fiber compiler. Allowing soft violations repairs
connectivity but does not create enough amplitude in a rare fiber.

Not covered: multilevel residual/distance energies that change the parent
away from the marked fiber's neighborhoods; structured nonlocal operations;
different encodings; an independently acquired warm start; extra non-parent
controls; arbitrary circuits or collective POVMs. In particular a residual-
aware Hamiltonian could modify essentially every conditional class, so the
small disturbance bound must NOT be imported without a new calculation.
That is the next concrete constructive alternative, not another gap plot.

Known quantum annealing/preparation methods already charge evolution and
gap resources: [Somma et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.1571),
[Boixo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.1652). This local native-state
derivation is not a claim that those papers forbid all annealing algorithms.

Falsifiers: a conditional projection has nonzero residual at r=0; beta=0
does not annihilate u; exact residual exceeds the bound; complete signed
dynamics exceed the bound beyond audited numerical error; IID pair-purity
formula fails; full-block control fails; a different energy or a supplied
reflection inherits this narrow action certificate; a rare accepted branch
is normalized before raw cost/error accounting.

Gemini/Antigravity owns routine CLI/registry integration and full production
validation. Register scoped negative controls, not accepted algorithms.
