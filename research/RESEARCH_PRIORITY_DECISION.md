- **Problem/input:** explicit graphs or code generators, unknown global
  isomorphism/equivalence, and a legally constructed quantum measurement.
- **Mechanism required:** an actual joint receiver or reduction bypassing the
  specific known limitations, not another representation dimension or invariant.
- **Baselines/costs:** graph canonicalization/WL and modern code equivalence
  attacks, coset acquisition, representation transforms and entangled readout.
- **Falsifiers:** strong classical invariant attacks; joint-measurement circuits
  whose synthesis is exponential; a scalar-invariant alias mistaken for recovery.
- **Why ranked lower now:** no concrete efficient receiver is supplied, and
  the [multiregister lower bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511148) must be
  respected in its scope. It is NOT an impossibility result for all GI algorithms.

## Stop And Pivot Rule

After each attempted constructive mechanism, record actual costs, what was
falsified, and the remaining assumptions. Before another local DCP/finite-field
extension, compare these independent tracks AGAIN. A sequence of conditional
identities without a new public algorithm is evidence to stop that sequence,
not evidence that a breakthrough is approaching. No probability increase is
inferred from file count, test count, registry count or the number of negatives.
