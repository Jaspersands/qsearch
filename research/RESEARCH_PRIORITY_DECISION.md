  removes a needless mandatory-noise hurdle, but provides no finder. The
  separate product-group source prototype is UNFINISHED and is not a decoder.
- **Re-entry gate:** a concrete uniform algorithmic rule with a non-circular
  success/cost argument, or a specific falsifier that could decide such a rule.
  Another information-only measurement, dense optimum or assumed beta is not
  sufficient. Do not regenerate adjacent barrier notes to keep the track busy.
- **Likely failure:** the subset-sum arithmetic remains hard; source promises
  fail; favorable labels are rare; a good large gap is classically exploitable;
  a receiver consumes more samples than its input reduction supplies.

[BCDV](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0501044) is prior art for the measurement/
subset-sum connection. The [2026 cyclotomic sieve](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34996v1)
explicitly warns that its sample requirement does not itself give the claimed
standard-LWE consequence. Keep this access bottleneck, not just gate counts.

### 3. Natural Quantum Dynamics: Independent Mechanism Tested

- **Problem/input:** a precisely specified local Hamiltonian or linear dynamics
  family with an efficiently prepared initial state and a scalar observable.
- **Mechanism:** exploit a new physical/invariant structure for evolution or
  readout, not a generic HHL call or a restatement of BQP universality.
- **Upside/costs:** potentially broad new algorithms, but no new end-to-end
  candidate is currently specified. Pay state preparation, time, normalization,
  conditioning, precision and observable sample complexity.
- **Baselines/falsifiers:** tensor networks, stabilizer methods, sparse/local
  propagation, classical Monte Carlo where legal, and problem-specific solvers.
  Easy short-time dynamics or exponential readout overhead can erase the claim.
- **Why not immediately first:** changing subject without a concrete mechanism
  would repeat the same failure mode. Existing broad simulation campaign
  negatives are not a justification for abandoning all quantum dynamics.
- **Actual follow-up:** controlled displacement is now executed with exact-input
  overlap arithmetic, classical Pauli sampling and paid vacuum-cutoff bounds.
  Four physical controls, all two-control Paulis, a large-force precision trap
  and a real noncommuting-hopping scope falsifier are tested. This supports
  neither a new quantum algorithm nor a general interacting-model dequantization.

For example, [Sakamoto and Fujii](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10445) separate
specific short-time geometrically local classical dynamics from harder regimes;
that scoped result is not a general Hamiltonian-simulation dequantization.

### 4. GI / Code Equivalence / General Nonabelian HSP: High Upside, Low Current Tractability

- **Problem/input:** explicit graphs or code generators, unknown global
  isomorphism/equivalence, and a legally constructed quantum measurement.
- **Mechanism required:** an actual joint receiver or reduction bypassing the
  specific known limitations, not another representation dimension or invariant.
- **Baselines/costs:** graph canonicalization/WL and modern code equivalence
  attacks, coset acquisition, representation transforms and entangled readout.
- **Falsifiers:** strong classical invariant attacks; joint-measurement circuits
  whose synthesis is exponential; a scalar-invariant alias mistaken for recovery.
- **Why ranked lower now:** no concrete efficient receiver is supplied, and
  the [multiregister lower bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511148) must be
  respected in its scope. It is NOT an impossibility result for all GI algorithms.

## Stop And Pivot Rule

After each attempted constructive mechanism, record actual costs, what was
falsified, and the remaining assumptions. Before another local DCP/finite-field
extension, compare these independent tracks AGAIN. A sequence of conditional
identities without a new public algorithm is evidence to stop that sequence,
not evidence that a breakthrough is approaching. No probability increase is
inferred from file count, test count, registry count or the number of negatives.
