# DCP Cascaded Multi-Stage Dyadic Adaptive Kernel Routing and Minimum-Weight Pruning Bridge

## 1. Universal All-Level Sign Invariance ($-2^\ell \equiv +2^\ell \pmod{2^{\ell+1}}$)
At any dyadic elimination stage $1 \le \ell < t$, wires satisfying $\tilde{A}^{(\ell)}_j \equiv 0 \pmod{2^\ell}$ have residues in $\{0, 2^\ell\}^n \pmod{2^{\ell+1}}$. Since $-2^\ell \equiv +2^\ell \pmod{2^{\ell+1}}$, coset-representative sign flips $\tilde{A}^{(\ell)}_j \mapsto -\tilde{A}^{(\ell)}_j \pmod{2^t}$ leave $\tilde{A}^{(\ell)}_j \bmod 2^{\ell+1}$ strictly invariant, and Stage-$(\ell+1)$ CNOT combinations are independent of the Stage-$(\ell+1)$ syndrome bits modulo $2^{\ell+1}$.

## 2. Fixed-Instance Residue Dichotomy vs. Upper-Bit Ensemble Weight Recurrence
- **Fixed-Instance Dichotomy ($L=3, \bmod 8$)**: For any odd pair $(a_{2j}, a_{2j+1}) \bmod 8$, the unique Stage-1 syndrome $y_{1,j}^*$ with $\tilde{A}^{(1)}_j(y_{1,j}^*) \equiv 0 \pmod 4$ has a deterministic $\bmod 8$ residue $r_j \in \{0, 4\}$. When all 4 pairs have $r_j = 0 \pmod 8$ (`[1, 7, 3, 5, 1, 7, 3, 5]`), mean pruned weight is $19/8 = 2.375$; when all 4 pairs have $r_j = 4 \pmod 8$ (`[1, 3, 5, 7, 1, 5, 3, 7]`), mean pruned weight is $71/16 = 4.4375$, with $100\%$ $\bmod 8$ elimination in both cases.
- **Upper-Bit Ensemble Recurrence**: Over the uniform upper-bit ensemble $A^{(\mathrm{high})}$ ($r_j \in \{0, 4\}$ IID fair), minimum-Hamming-weight kernel selection at $n=1$ picks a weight-1 wire with probability $3/4$ and a weight-2 CNOT with probability $1/4$ at each stage, yielding exact ensemble mean weight $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{wt}^{(L)}] = 2(5/4)^{L-1}$ ($25/8 = 3.125$ at $L=3$, vs. $2^L = 8$ for unpruned trees) and exact quadratic visibility recurrence $V_\ell = \frac{3}{4}V_{\ell-1} + \frac{1}{4}V_{\ell-1}^2 > \gamma_0^{2^\ell}$.
