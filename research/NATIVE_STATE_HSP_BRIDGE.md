## The Source Is Not The Missing Assumption

For the existing native ternary input let A=(O/(pi^r))^n, pi=zeta-1 and
G=A semidirect C3, with (b,t)(c,u)=(b+zeta^t*c,t+u). The observed frequency
label a is uniform in the dual of A. Keep that CLASSICAL label together
with its actual supplied qutrit:

    psi_a^s = (1/sqrt3) sum_j chi_a(lambda_j*s)|j>,
    lambda_0=0, lambda_1=1, lambda_2=1+zeta.

Their ONE original mixed state is

    rho_s = (1/|A|) sum_a |a><a| tensor |psi_a^s><psi_a^s|.

No coherent superposition of frequency labels, graph-state preparation,
unknown preparation inverse, chosen label or cloning is required. Define
a known induced representation independently of the hidden secret:

    R_a(b,t)=D_a(b) T^t,
    D_a(b)|j>=chi_a(zeta^j*b)|j>,  T|j>=|j-1>.

T D_a(c) T^-1=D_a(zeta*c), so R is a genuine representation of G.
The full R is its direct sum controlled by the recorded label a. Phase
arithmetic costs polynomial in the explicit native coordinates and required
precision; exact ideal controls do not settle aggregate implementation error.

Direct calculation gives

    <psi_a^s|R_a(b,t)|psi_a^s>
      = (1/3) sum_j chi_a(zeta^j*(b+lambda_t*s)).

Averaging UNIFORM a uses additive-character orthogonality. Thus

    Tr(R(b,t)rho_s) = 1[b=-lambda_t*s].

This is the Bose subgroup generated by (-s,1), with ORIGINAL gap1. The sign
is a convention induced by the positive phase in the supplied sample. The
native problem already meets the copy-only StateHSP access/gap promise.
The missing theorem is one for its growing nonabelian group class, not a
new density oracle or a preparation inverse. Nonuniform/noisy/correlated
frequency supplies need separate promises; this bridge does not fix them.

## Where The Current Receiver Applies

At r=2, pi^2=0 and the ring has characteristic3. Write b=x+pi*z with
x,z in F3^n. Then

    ((x,t),z)*((x',t'),z')
      = ((x+x',t+t'), z+z'+t*x').

This is EXACTLY the vector-bilinear input of the implemented receiver:
d=n+1, k=n, B_j((x,t),(x',t'))=t*x'_j. The subgroup projection contains
(-x_s,1); no centre element except identity lies in the hidden subgroup.
The group/action chart is public and secret-independent. Native level2
therefore is a legitimate restricted application, not a fabricated oracle.
It is already within known nil-2 algorithm territory and supplies no new
full-depth or classical-hardness separation. The current pass verifies the
source and chart, NOT an end-to-end hardware/native backend compilation.

## Why Fixed-Class Quotients Cannot Recover Full Precision

The known lower central series is gamma_j(G)=pi^(j-1) A for j>=2, hence
the nilpotency class is r. Any group homomorphism into a group of class<=c
kills gamma_(c+1)(G)=pi^c A. Therefore ALL such homomorphisms, even many
different ones, identify secrets differing by pi^c A. Known precomposition
by group automorphisms does not remove this characteristic kernel.

For unrestricted ring secrets the common fibre has size3^(n*(r-c)) when
c<r. For the ACTUAL integer-embedded source, s in Z_(3^ceil(r/2))^n,
the retained precision is at most ceil(c/2) trits per coordinate. Its fibre
size is

    3^(n*(ceil(r/2)-min(ceil(r/2),ceil(c/2)))).

This distinction matters: do not use the full ring count as a hardness
claim for the restricted integer secret. A class2 quotient retains only
ONE trit per integer coordinate. Repeating the same type of quotient does
not supply the missing higher trits. This is an information ceiling for
that HOMOMORPHIC reduction interface, not a lower bound for all receivers.

Countercontrol: at r=4, integer secrets0 and3 agree modulo pi^2. Their
actual qutrits for label a=1 have fidelity1/3, not1. The quantum input still
contains distinguishable information that the fixed-class quotient discards.
Nonhomomorphic state transformations or a genuinely growing-class receiver
remain possible; the countercontrol explicitly prevents a general no-go.

## Revised Next Mathematical Target

The ideal native source has a STRONGER promise than a generic gapped state:
its expectation is exactly the subgroup indicator. For a designated central
subgroup Z with S intersect Z trivial, its central spectrum is uniform.
The conditional projector identity gives

    Tr(R(g)rho_lambda)=chi_lambda(-z_g) if g*z_g in S,
                      0 otherwise.

There is a unique z_g in Z when it exists. Thus a nonempty heterogeneous
zero-sum block has expectation EXACTLY1 on S*Z and0 elsewhere. Its known
tensor action is a representation of G/Z on the prepared sectors and has
quotient Bose gap1. This removes an apparent gap obstruction for THIS IDEAL
SOURCE; it is not a theorem for arbitrary noisy original Bose gaps. The
same statement with characters annihilating S intersect Z extends to a
nontrivial fixed central subgroup, but that is not needed for native H_s.

Independent fresh W=(n+1)^2-input windows yield IID output sources with
known metadata-controlled actions. Every window gives a nonempty zero sum,
and all unused inputs are charged/discarded. Naively composing r-2 such
reductions before the class2 receiver consumes W^(r-2) originals per final
input, and tensor-register/action sizes grow accordingly. That is
n^O(r), not polynomial jointly in n and r. Recyclers may improve constants
or yield but do NOT get a polynomial-depth guarantee from this argument.

Recovering a prefix of the native secret is not the full answer. Once all
but the top pi-adic digit are known, subtract their known phase on FRESH
original samples: the remaining central secret produces linear trit phases,
so ordinary trit Fourier measurements give linear equations for that digit.
This lift step is cheap in principle; it does not repair the cost of
acquiring the reduced prefix source. Approximate state/action precision and
the actual upstream sample budget remain separate.

The revised high-impact target is therefore AMORTIZED CENTRAL DESCENT:
preserve the exact source/gap and full secret precision while avoiding a
product of window costs or live tensor-register sizes exponential in r.
Adaptively recycled outputs need not be IID, so an attempted improvement
must derive their joint measurement law rather than assert independence.
A polynomial-copy AND polynomial-time recurrence would be substantive;
merely calling the abelian learner or proving the gap again is not.

The [normal-character channel audit](NATIVE_NORMAL_CHARACTER_CHANNEL.md)
now eliminates one shortcut: irreversibly measuring pi^c A in one step
identifies secrets modulo pi^(c+1), even with large batches and recorded
outcomes. Coherent retention escapes that bound, but a public pointer
Fourier measurement/feedforward only restores the original input; it does
not provide the missing cross-character/inter-word decoder.

Falsifiers: a wrong representation sign, a nonuniform source silently
averaged uniformly, a reduced secret confused with the full one, an unknown
inverse granted, a character conditioned on uncharged rare supply, or a
recurrence with exponent proportional to r. An arbitrary state-conversion
proposal is NOT rejected solely because the group-homomorphism ceiling holds.
