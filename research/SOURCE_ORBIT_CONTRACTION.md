## Why Orbit Reduction Is Exact

Fix h=(12)(34)... and let C(h) be its centralizer. The source/Walsh law is
the signed pair contraction

    sum_(u,v) A_u A_v product_i L_i(u,v) / D^2,
    D=n!, A_g=D*a_g.

Each central character coefficient is invariant under conjugation. For
s in C(h), h is fixed and every local factor satisfies

    L_i(sus^-1, svs^-1) = L_i(u,v).

Sum ALL v for one representative u of each C(h)-conjugation orbit O and
multiply the row by |O|. The change v -> svs^-1 is a bijection, so this
reproduces the full contraction exactly. The same orbit decomposition is
valid for the null, although a larger symmetry group is available there.
Using one decomposition for both avoids mismatched weighting conventions.

This is not conjugation of u alone with v fixed. Ordinary S_n conjugacy
classes also do not suffice for the alternative: they can move h. Tests
exhibit two S4 first endpoints in the same S_n class with different fixed-h
local factors. Only simultaneous hidden-preserving conjugation is used.

C(h) is generated by swaps inside each matched pair and interchanges of
adjacent pairs. Breadth-first orbit closure uses those generators, not a
list of all centralizer elements. Every permutation belongs to exactly
one orbit, and its orbit size divides 2^(n/2)(n/2)!.

| Degree | Orbits | Streamed Pairs | Full Pairs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 8 | 192 | 576 |
| 6 | 34 | 24,480 | 518,400 |
| 8 | 182 | 7,338,240 | 1,625,702,400 |

At S8 the 7,338,240 product indices are reused for the null and alternative;
two hypothesis kernels are evaluated on them. This is not a claim of that
many elementary CPU operations. The largest product-index block contains
only D=40320 entries. There is no D-by-D product matrix or physical tensor
density matrix. The evaluator still enumerates n! permutations and is NOT
polynomial in the input degree.

## Exact Implementation

`theorems/coset_source_orbit_contraction.py` computes characters by cycle
type and uses lexicographic Lehmer ranks for vectorized permutation
products. It checks conjugacy-class sizes against the enumerated group,
exact row orthogonality of the character table, and the fixed-point-free
characters against independent two-quotient arithmetic.

For the negative-character reflection, the two source projectors have
integer coefficient numerators

    P_plus(g)  = [D*delta_e(g)+A_g]/2,
    P_minus(g) = [D*delta_e(g)-A_g]/2.

The streamed row uses the same four category/corrected-bit factors as the
full evaluator, including their signs and zero masses. Equal factor tuples
are grouped and exact signed integer weights accumulated. Off-coset
absolute-weight remainders are accumulated separately; they are NOT thrown
away when signed weights cancel.

An independently formed scalar corrected-weight kernel is accumulated in
the same stream. Its alpha and beta must exactly equal the corresponding
category sums. Both the categorical and scalar coset-diagonal mixtures are
positive, normalized comparison distributions, not exact small-k laws.

Integer arithmetic is checked rather than assumed: sum_g A_g^2=D^2 and
(sum_g |A_g|)^2<=D^3, so every grouped pair-weight accumulation fits int64
at the declared degrees. At S8, D^3=65548320768000. Overlap certificates
and all subsequent probability powers use Python integers and Fractions.
Degrees outside 4,6,8 are rejected instead of pretending the implementation
scales to arbitrarily large n.

The S4/S6 audit compares EVERY signed categorical spectrum, positive
mixture, absolute remainder and source prior with full pair enumeration.
It separately compares complete scalar weight certificates and overlap
identities. Agreement is exact, not just matching a few floating metrics.

The raw-input lower bound is the known support-rank comparison:

    T_raw >= max(0,1-M/2^k).

Every fixed-h k-copy alternative has rank (D/2)^k. Their mixture has support
rank at most M(D/2)^k, while the null is maximally mixed. A projector onto
that support accepts the alternative with certainty and the null with
probability at most M/2^k. This argument shows information exists; it does
NOT implement that projector efficiently. The exact difference between
this lower bound and measured transcript distance is a certified lower
bound on information lost by this readout, not a computational speedup.

The full pair-likelihood evaluator is extended to S8 for up to four copies:
at k=4 it has distance 0.093224, versus 0.059287 for the ideal sign/bit table.
Larger-copy full-pair scores are explicitly uncomputed. There is no need to
pay for them to falsify this table where the cheaper pair-event statistic
already wins. Neither pair baseline classically replaces its quantum
measurement front end.

## Falsification and Scope Checks

- Generator closure, exact orbit masses and Lehmer ranks must agree with
  independent permutation enumeration.
- Simultaneous C(h) conjugation must preserve every finite local kernel;
  unrestricted conjugation of just one endpoint must fail its regression.
- Complete S4/S6 signed spectra and remainders must equal full enumeration.
- S8 source and corrected-weight marginals must agree with the separately
  computed source priors and scalar weight kernels, including k=7,12,16.
- The n=8 implementation must reject requests for full-irrep or three-way
  character categories rather than silently substitute phase-sign labels.
- Raw support information is not labeled an implemented measurement, and
  no finite result is promoted to a growing-degree theorem.

Remaining risk: both implementations share some character utilities and
the mathematical source/Walsh model. Exact agreement is strong regression
evidence, not independent theorem verification. The two-quotient character
checks, independent scalar kernel and existing matrix-law controls provide
additional checks, but do not establish novelty or a natural-problem speedup.
