## Full-Ramification Source Coordinates

Let p be a fixed prime, d=p-1, q=p^t, L=dt, pi=zeta_p-1, and
O=Z[zeta_p]. Restrict the secret to the actual INTEGER-EMBEDDED s in Z_q^n.
The native pairing is

```text
B_L(x,y) = Tr(x*y / (p*pi^(L-1))) mod1.
lambda_j = 1+zeta_p+...+zeta_p^(j-1), j=1,...,d.
u_j(y) = q*B_L(lambda_j,y) modq.
```

The lambda_j form an integral basis: their columns in the power basis are
triangular with diagonal1. The codifferent is generated by pi/p up to a unit.
Since (p)=(pi)^d, q/pi^L is also a unit. Therefore q*B_L is a perfect integral
trace-dual pairing. Its frequency matrix M in these two integral bases is
integer unimodular. Native O/(pi^L)=O/(q) labels correspond BIJECTIVELY to
(u_1,...,u_d) in Z_q^d. Apply this coordinatewise for vector labels.

Uniform native labels consequently supply IID uniform frequency vectors. The
post-Fourier native state is exactly

```text
( |0> + sum_(j=1)^d exp(2*pi*i*<s,u_j>/q)|j> ) / sqrt(p).
```

These PUBLIC frequencies do not expose s or give evaluations of its phase.
The source is supplied quantum samples, not an unknown-state preparation
circuit, its inverse, coherent shift evaluator, or free identical-state copies.
The coordinate argument does NOT apply unchanged to arbitrary ring secrets.
Tests retain a ternary ring-secret counterexample.

The code calibrates p=2,3,5,7 and t=1,2,3 with exact rational field matrices.
An independent JS checker evaluates the trace in complex field embeddings and
checks exact integer determinants. This is bounded conformance evidence;
the general statement above is a local mathematical argument awaiting review.

For p3, q=3^ceil(L/2), a label y=(a,b) has frequencies

```text
C1 = U*a+V*b modq
C2 = (U+V)*a-U*b modq
M = [[U,V],[U+V,-U]].
```

At even L=2t, detM=-1 and both frequencies are independent. Intermediate odd
L has detM=-3 and C2=2*C1 mod3. The latter is not two independent Z_q vectors.
It instead gives uniform (C1,(C2-2*C1)/3) in Z_q x Z_(q/3). Six levels are
exhausted, and inverse coordinates are checked at levels7 through20.

## Known, Charged Binary/Cyclotomic Conversion

[Boucher, Fouque and Shen, Proposition5](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34996v1)
already give DCP/CCP conversions preserving the integer secret, with success
p/2^(p-1) in one direction and2/p in the other. Our phase-coordinate realization
checks the SAME sample costs; it is not a newly discovered reduction.

A native p-level phase sample projected onto |0>,|1> gives a binary phase
sample with frequency u_1, success2/p, and expected consumption p/2.
Conversely, take d FRESH independent binary phase samples with the SAME s and
IID labels u_1,...,u_d. Project their product onto the zero and one-hot words.
Success is p/2^d; accepted phases give the native sample with label M^-1*u.
Expected binary consumption is d*2^d/p. Every rejected word is charged.

The paper's coset-register construction uses prefix binary words. Our one-hot
POST-FOURIER construction is not asserted to replay that literal circuit.
It is not an inverse acting on one native sample, and grants no cloning.
Overheads are constant only for FIXED p. At p3 they are3/2 native samples per
binary sample and8/3 binary samples per native sample.

This equivalence does not dequantize DCP. It removes any claim that the raw
cyclotomic encoding itself supplies a free family-specific correlation.
Correlations introduced by collective transformations may still matter.

## Actual Full-Secret Reference Measurement

For m ternary native samples at L=2t, define the public three-choice modular
subset-sum map, with G=Z_q^n, N=|G|, and D=3^m:

```text
F(j) = sum_i (0, u_i, v_i)[j_i] in G.
eta(a) = |F^-1(a)|.
psi_s = D^(-1/2) sum_j exp(2*pi*i*<s,F(j)>/q)|j>.
```

Exact suffix dynamic programming computes counts. Public lexicographic
rank/unrank maps each word to (F(j), within-fiber rank), including duplicate
labels and multiplicities. A known rank-range Householder unitary erases the
uniform rank superposition. The remaining state is

```text
sum_a sqrt(eta(a)/D)*exp(2*pi*i*<s,a>/q)|a>|0>.
```

A Fourier readout over the FULL G recovers the complete s with probability

```text
p_opt = (sum_a sqrt(eta(a)/D))^2/N.
```

Character orthogonality gives the covariant POVM on the fiber span. The
weighted Cauchy-Schwarz dual certificate in `TERNARY_CARRY_PACKETS.md` proves
uniform-prior optimality here too; tests check the actual operators and dual
positive-semidefinite inequalities. This is a known measurement framework,
not a novel PGM theorem. [Bacon, Childs and van Dam](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0501044v2)
link efficient DHSP measurements to subset-sum solution procedures. Their
restricted converse is NOT a universal impossibility theorem for all decoders.

This implementation replays public dense unitary blocks, NOT a uniform
gate-level circuit. Classical DP is explicitly costed and exponential in
n*logq. Coherent lookup, QRAM and table compilation are not free. Arbitrary
input norm preservation and involutivity test the rank erasure independently
of the unknown phase. All nonzero-rank outcomes are counted as failure.

Measuring F before phase readout destroys the useful relative phase: with a
uniform secret prior, that strategy succeeds only1/N. A classical preimage
sampler alone does not automatically supply the coherent fiber transform.
However, a future decoder is NOT required to use this exact transform or
sample fibers uniformly: charged transversal or non-PGM methods remain open.

## Information Versus Computation At Growing Parameters

For distinct ternary words j,k, F(j)-F(k) is uniform on G under IID native
even-level labels. Some differing choice has a unit coefficient on an
independent uniform frequency. Thus Pr[F(j)=F(k)]=1/N, and

```text
E sum_a eta(a)^2 = D + D*(D-1)/N
E chi^2(eta/D || uniform) = (N-1)/D
E p_opt >= D/(D+N-1)
E number_of_nonempty_fibers >= N*D/(D+N-1).
```

The last two bounds follow from Holder/Cauchy-Schwarz and Jensen. In detail,
with P=eta/D and C=sum P^2, (sum sqrt(P))^2>=1/C and support(P)>=1/C;
E[1/C]>=1/E[C]. They do not rely on a fitted exponent or a favorable seed.

At m=n*t+4, D=81N, the mean optimal measurement succeeds at least
81/(82-1/N), approximately.9878. Yet this EXPLICIT DP stores at least
81N/(82-1/N) final count entries ON AVERAGE. Sparse dictionaries do not
eliminate its exponential cost. This is a bound on this reference's table,
NOT a lower bound against every classical or quantum decoder. Information
availability alone is not an algorithm.

The report has exact large-integer ledgers for n=8,16,32,64 and t=4,8,16.
A complete q3,m3 source enumeration checks the collision moment and bounds.
No global average-case NP-hardness or optimal runtime exponent is inferred.

## Failed Transfers And Remaining Obligations

- G=Z_(3^t)^n is not the additive group of F_(3^t)^n when t>1: their group
 exponents differ. A same-cardinality finite-field replacement loses the target.
- [Decker and Wocjan](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0703195v3) give efficient
 univariate quadratic/cubic hidden-polynomial-graph cases over finite fields.
 The stated fixed-variable, fixed-degree setup does not automatically solve
 growing-rank modular subset-sum fibers or varying native carry packets.
- A low-degree public evaluator is not an unknown-phase evaluation oracle.
- Full-source input accounting, all secret digits and original source law must
 survive any next transformation. No free matched packet or repeated copy.
- Next substantial work: a concrete implicit high-rank decoder/transversal,
 an efficiently alignable correlated source, or a genuinely different reduction.
 Challenge it against legal classical measurements AND known quantum sieves
 before proposing a candidate. Review source laws and novelty first.
