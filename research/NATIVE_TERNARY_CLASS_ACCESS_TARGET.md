## The Missing Operation

Do not just cap the unchanged public-index edge Grover sampler at polynomial
time. Its native raw advantage is bounded bylambda*(2T+1)^2/(3P), with all
IID source labels and failures charged. A new mechanism must change the
operator, preparation or access model and prove its charged implementation;
this is not a lower bound against such changed mechanisms.

For public IID native labels, construct a charged coherent transform on the
uniform span of full-frequency preimages that maps |u_(S,h)> to |S,h> with
common clean residual work. Approximate implementations must bound channel
error on the ACTUAL phase-state source, not an arbitrary chosen fiber.
The source already lies in that span, but knows neither counts nor witnesses.

Three-by-three spectral diagonalization is easy once its basis is supplied.
The high-variance research is constructing that basis, or extracting its
trit information without constructing it. The public Fourier-erasure map
has singular values sqrt(c_h/D) and mean raw source success~10/(9D).
Do not silently replace its Kraus operator by its polar isometry.

## Ranked Attempts And How To Kill Them

1. **Multiscale native factorization.** Split the qutrit word and frequency
   digit ranges, build local three-class transforms, then coherently compose
   them across carry constraints. Target a recurrence in n,r rather than in
   number of native words. Likely failure: branch count, collision sparsity,
   or accumulated normalization reintroduces3^M. Falsify any claimed recurrence
   with exact small-source composition INCLUDING rejected/empty fibers and
   every source-weighted normalization. Do not assume retained labels stay IID.
   The [prefix Schmidt audit](TERNARY_PREFIX_SCHMIDT.md) now rules out a
   polynomial MEAN explicit-bond budget for faithful middle-scale state
   approximation on the fixed split and original IID source law. This includes
   label/outcome-dependent bond allocation, not label-adaptive partitions.
   It does NOT invalidate implicit carry/stabilizer, alternate tensor geometry,
   or observable-only approaches; those are now the higher-value targets.
2. **Source-aware frame transform.** Allow bounded source-weighted error,
   rather than a worst-fiber exact inverse. Attempt to discard low-information
   sectors while retaining inverse-polynomial total trit advantage. Likely
   failure: informative sparse cross-class fibers carry exactly the erased
   signal. Falsify with exact source-weighted loss and a lower bound on raw
   correctness after all failures, not success conditional on a good sector.
3. **Implicit subgroup or character transform.** Seek an algebraically
   factored transform of the native three-choice sum map, not generic QSVT
   on a1/sqrt(D)-normalized incidence operator. Likely failure: the ternary
   simplex embedding is not a group homomorphism to Z_(3^r)^n; carries and
   arbitrary IID labels break a convenient Fourier diagonalization. Prove
   the precise intertwiner on actual native labels or produce a counterexample.
4. **Coherent tripartite walk without explicit counts.** Try to implement
   transitions or degree-normalized operators directly from public arithmetic.
   Likely failure: uniform-neighbor preparation is itself a constrained
   native preimage sampler. Charge it. Any argument treating degree as a
   constant supplied register or using free walk powers is rejected.

The simplest character shortcut now has an exact full-root control. On a
single native cycle, the public F increments are a,c-a,-c. Translation
independence is equivalent to c=2a AND3a=0 modq. These conditions on every
wire are necessary and sufficient for F to be a group homomorphism from
F_3^M, and thus for EVERY secret's phase state to be a word-group character.
Checking only c=2a mod3 misses the full-root cycle wrap. IID native rows satisfy
the exact conditions with probability3^(-n*M*(2r-1)), not typically. This does
NOT rule out structured transforms on nonlinear maps, intermediate-state
collimation, useful characters for special secrets, or graph-specific
diagonalization. In particular a zero informative graph is trivially
translation invariant even when its F is not a homomorphism; do not infer
graph noninvariance from this source-character obstruction.

## Promotion Requirements

One source-aware truncation shortcut is now precisely bounded: retaining
only full-frequency classes withc/D>=1/(nr)^2 in the globally normalized
public Fourier-erasure operator keeps mean source mass at most
(D-1)/(G*(ceil(D/(nr)^2)-1)), when that ceiling>=2. This is exponentially
small for growing nr. The proof uses E[c(F(X))-1]=(D-1)/G<1/9, not a random
matrix heuristic. It DOES NOT address a new factored normalization or
information extracted coherently from discarded sectors. Attempt2 must
retain source-weighted trit information without this invalid global cutoff.

Demand native IID source coverage, all-secret correctness, fresh-state cost,
coherent gate/arithmetic/precision cost, clean common workspace, all schedule
aborts and successful-branch probabilities. Compare against classical
chosen-query/known-label attacks and established quantum sieves. A polynomial
claim needs a uniform recurrence/proof, not finite runtime fitting.

A failure may be useful if it isolates a specific normalization or algebraic
obstruction. It is not a general quantum no-go. Do not call a transform
promising solely because its abstract block dimension is three.
