## Decision

The earlier [direct ring route](PRIME_POWER_REES_DESCENT.md) removes each
cyclic factor separately. Its expected predecessor bound for a central
grade with r cyclic factors is `[4*(gcd(d,p-1)+1)]^r`. Can a whole grade
be removed with polynomial rather than exponential dependence on r?

**Yes under an additional polynomial rank-dependent prime guard, and
the same ideal source and Rees assumptions.** A regular vector SDE witness
over the residue field lifts simultaneously through every cyclic factor.
Public pilots give a usable intrinsic module without knowing the hidden
central intersection. Incomplete pilots affect acceptance, not correctness.

The selected symmetric solver preserves whole first-level blocks; this
removes an initially proposed exponential all-minors guard. For fixed d
the resulting conditional expected predecessor bound is
`2*(r+2+M(r)) = O_d(r^2)`, including failed episodes and pilots. This is an
improvement over the earlier sufficient direct-conversion bound in their
overlap, NOT a lower bound comparison or an expansion of the already
proposed fixed-class HSP complexity claim via the outer reduction.

The implemented kernel accepts supplied module coordinates and witnesses.
The pilot-coordinate compiler and complete quantum conversion are NOT
implemented. This distinction is retained in every live report.

## 1. Exact Contract

Let Z be the highest remaining homogeneous central grade of an explicitly
coordinatized finite p-group arising from the Rees Lie construction:

```
Z = direct_sum_(j=1..r) Z/p^a_j,   p odd prime,   1 <= d < p.
```

All unit dilations Delta_u must preserve BOTH the hidden subgroup H and
Z, and restrict on Z to multiplication by u^d. Ambient grading alone does
not imply this for an arbitrary H. Rees homogenization is what supplies
the hidden-subgroup invariance; it is a substantive premise, not inferred
from passing arithmetic tests. The original H can be nonnormal.

Inputs are fresh independent legal ideal coset sources, conditional on
every preceding classical transcript. Fourier measurement on Z has the
exact uniform label law on `C=(H intersect Z)^perp <= dual(Z)`. Classical
coordinates of labels and arithmetic in the explicit finite modules are
public and paid. No hidden-subgroup membership query or opaque chart is
free. The exponents a_j are charged through the full `a_j*log2(p)` bits.

Approximate inputs require a JOINT source error and a complete flagged
channel error budget, including aborted episodes. There is no conclusion
from correct one-label marginals or an accepted-branch simulation alone.

## 2. Pilots Without Guessing A Quotient

Each new episode consumes P=r+2 independent pilot sources, measures their
labels, and discards their remaining registers. Let C' be the subgroup
generated by these labels. Compute its intrinsic invariant-factor
coordinates, an embedding back into dual(Z), and a membership map.

For pilot matrix B and ambient diagonal relation matrix D=diag(p^a_j),
one description is

```
R = {v in Z^P : Bv belongs to D Z^r},   C' = Z^P/R.
```

Full integer relation lattices and Smith/Hermite transformations give
these maps in polynomial bit complexity. An equivalent computation uses
the lattice `D Z^r + B Z^P` between D Z^r and Z^r. The transformation maps,
not merely the invariant-factor orders, must be retained. These are
public integer linear-algebra operations, not hidden-subgroup advice or
a discrete-log oracle. Their implementation is a remaining dependency.

Reduce modulo p only AFTER computing intrinsic coordinates. For example,
`p Z/p^2` has intrinsic cyclic rank one even though every ambient label is
zero modulo p. Naive ambient rank compression can erase a real phase.

Let s be the cyclic rank of C'. Take a NEW bank of M(s) sources. If any
label is outside C', abort the whole episode. Do not quotient by an
estimated H intersect Z. If C'=0, take one fresh source and accept it with
scale one only if its label is zero; otherwise abort. This is M(0)=1.

Conditional on pilots generating all of C, the new bank is IID uniform
in C in its intrinsic coordinates, independently of the pilot outcome.
On incomplete pilots no success-law claim is needed: accepted witnesses
are checked in the ORIGINAL ambient label module and remain correct.

## 3. Field Solver And Regular Lift

Write `C' = direct_sum_(j=1..s) Z/p^e_j`, E=max(e_j), and let lambda_ji
be the measured labels in these coordinates. Let g=gcd(d,p-1). Use

```
M(s) = s+1                         if g=1,
M(s) = (s+1)*((g-1)*s+1)          if g>=2.
```

For g=1, a nonzero linear kernel and inverse exponent suffice. For g>=2
use the [bounded symmetric solver](POWER_SDE_RANDOMNESS_BUDGET.md), under
`p >= 16*g^(2*g+2)`. Its uniform failure bound `2^-b`, b>=4, holds for
each fixed admissible bank, with randomness independent of the sources.
The existing Bezout wrapper converts reduced-degree coefficients to an
ORIGINAL degree-d field witness u. A degree-g witness must not be used
unchanged in degree d.

Check `sum_i lambda_ji*u_i^d = 0 mod p` for every j. With every inactive
variable fixed to EXACT zero, form the active Jacobian

```
J_ji = d*lambda_ji*u_i^(d-1) mod p  if u_i != 0,
J_ji = 0                           if u_i = 0.
```

The inactive rule matters even for d=1. Updating an initially zero
variable by p*t would create a nonzero nonunit, not a legal dilation.
If rank J<s, abort this episode. This is not an unsatisfiability claim;
some singular field witnesses already solve the ring equations.

Otherwise choose s active pivot columns, invert their Jacobian minor,
and freeze all other variables. Take canonical integer coefficient lifts
and solve the stronger system with ALL rows modulo p^E. At precision p^k:

```
F_j(u) = sum_i lambda_ji*u_i^d,
t = -J_pivot^(-1) * (F(u)/p^k) mod p,
u_pivot <- u_pivot + p^k*t.
```

Taylor terms of order at least two vanish modulo p^(k+1). The pivot
Jacobian modulo p is unchanged throughout. Thus induction lifts through
E-1 steps. Each active scale stays a unit; each inactive scale stays exact
zero. The result satisfies every original modulus p^e_j, even when the
rows have different orders. Arbitrary integer row representatives give
the same validity guarantee, though not necessarily the same witness.

This is ordinary multivariate Hensel lifting with a nonsingular minor;
see [Grochow's multivariate Hensel note, Theorem 1](https://home.cs.colorado.edu/~jgrochow/hensel-notes.pdf).
No novelty is claimed for it. Work is polynomial in matrix dimensions,
d, E and log p; no ring, field, support or minor enumeration is performed.
The implementation verifies the final congruences in the supplied mixed
module. A future pilot compiler MUST additionally verify the embedded
equations in the original ambient module before operating on sources.

## 4. Selected-Block Regularity And Paid Episode Cost

If C/pC has rank k<=r, P=r+2 uniform pilots generate C with probability
`product_(i=0..k-1) (1-p^(i-P))`. Consequently

```
Pr[incomplete pilots] < 1/(p^2*(p-1)) <= 1/16.
```

For an IID uniform s-by-M(s) matrix over F_p, a fixed s-column minor is
singular with probability `1-product_(i=1..s)(1-p^-i) < 1/(p-1)`.
Therefore the chance that ANY such minor is singular is at most

```
delta_generic(s) = min(1, binom(M(s),s)/(p-1)).
```

When all minors are nonsingular, every nonzero field SDE witness has
support greater than s: a smaller support would give a forbidden linear
dependence with nonzero coefficients u_i^d. Its active columns span F_p^s,
so its Jacobian has full row rank. This justifies using the field solver's
actual output, without assuming that it samples solutions uniformly.
The operational check is ONE Jacobian rank, not all minors. This generic
bound is valid but much too expensive. The actual solver needs far less.

### The Support-Preservation Improvement

For g>=2 the symmetric solver partitions its M(s) original columns into
`h=(g-1)*s+1` consecutive blocks of s+1 columns. Suppose each individual
block is full spark. Every nonzero linear dependence within it then has
ALL s+1 coefficients nonzero. This property is invariant under the
invertible equation-row compression used by the implementation, which
preserves the original column order.

At the second level, a nonzero dependence selects at least one whole
first-level block. Its selected coefficient is nonzero, so all of that
block's stored roots survive multiplication. The symmetric contraction
has a NONZERO coefficient in EVERY cell and disjoint original supports.
Consequently every original column in that selected block remains active
in the final witness. Those columns span F_p^s. The original-degree
Bezout exponent conversion preserves their nonzero support as well.

This argument works for every successful choice of the solver's random
roots and contraction coefficients; there is no uniform-witness assumption
or conditioning on solver success. It is specific to the nonzero
symmetric contraction. It does NOT apply automatically to a different
SDE solver, early-return shortcut or contraction with zero cell weights.
Define the block partition on the ORIGINAL IID columns, before the
data-dependent invertible row compression, when applying the probability
calculation. The compressed columns need not themselves have an IID law.

Each block has s+1 maximal minors. A union bound across h blocks therefore
uses only `h*(s+1)=M(s)` minors, regardless of dependencies BETWEEN blocks:

```
Pr[selected symmetric witness is singular AND solver succeeds]
    <= delta_block(s) = min(1, M(s)/(p-1)).
```

Do not reinterpret this as the same probability conditional on arithmetic
success. For the episode proof, add arithmetic abort as a separate event.

For g=1 the same statement uses its sole s+1-column linear-kernel block.
The additional ambient-rank guard is now

```
p-1 >= 4*M(r).
```

This is polynomial in r for fixed g, not the discarded exponential guard.
It is still NOT implied by the degree-only solver guard at unbounded r.
For g=1 it is `p-1>=4*(r+1)`; that easy case uses only linear algebra and
is not a new nonlinear SDE result.

Use exactly M(s) bank columns for observed rank s, charging at most M(r).
M(s)<=M(r) directly makes the new bound uniform over observed ranks.
The report retains the generic all-witnesses bound for comparison, but
the episode budget is explicitly tied to the selected symmetric backend.

With b>=4, conditional on any prior transcript, a fresh episode obeys

```
Pr[abort] <= 1/(p^2*(p-1)) + 1/4 + 2^-b <= 3/8,
predecessor outputs consumed per episode <= B = r+2+M(r),
E[predecessor outputs per accepted conversion] <= (8/5)*B <= 2*B.
```

No independence between these three failure events is assumed. The
regularity bound is conditional on complete pilots; the arithmetic bound
is uniform for fixed banks. Fresh pilots are acquired EVERY attempt.
Keeping an incomplete C' and retrying indefinitely could make the cost
arbitrarily large, despite pointwise correctness of accepted outputs.

## 5. Why Acceptance Does Not Bias The Quotient Source

Every accepted label is genuinely in C, whether or not C'=C. Its
normalized source correlation vanishes outside HZ; on h*z it is the
appropriate character value at z. Each active unit dilation preserves
HZ and its complement. Multiplying active correlations gives the phase
corresponding to `sum_i u_i^d*lambda_i`, which is exactly zero in dual(Z).
Thus the controlled-right-action contraction yields the SAME normalized
coset state of HZ/Z for each accepted tuple and valid witness. At least
one scale is active, so the support indicator is retained.

The output is independent of pilot labels, solver choices and retry
records. Membership-based acceptance and incomplete pilots cannot change
that ideal output; they only change its availability. This is a
pointwise channel identity, not an inference from averaged cancellation.
The finite test uses an actual nonabelian graded group, a nonnormal H,
mixed central orders and a nonlinear section, but does not establish the
general source contract or execute a Rees receiver.

## 6. Composition And What It Does Not Prove

For homogeneous grades d=2,...,c, with ambient cyclic ranks r_d>0, assume
the guards hold at every stage. The conditional expected lifted-leaf cost
per terminal source is bounded by

```
Q = product_d 2*(r_d+2+M_d(r_d)).
```

Disjoint fresh conditional sources and transcript-independent accepted
outputs justify multiplying these costs, including all failed episodes.
Empty grades are skipped. At fixed class and degree this is polynomial
in the ranks, unlike the earlier sequential cyclic sufficient bound.

For m requested terminal sources, Markov gives a whole-run abort chance
at most 1/16 at a GLOBAL cap of 16*m*Q lifted leaves; each Rees leaf costs
c original copies/queries. The cap is checked before acquiring another
leaf. This is not a constant local retry cap or a successful-tree count.
A full implementation must also count every pilot measurement, attempted
conversion, classical failure and final readout in its complete-channel
precision and operation budget. This report implements neither the
recursive controller nor an OS wall-clock cap. The old manuscript's
conservative bound is not silently replaced by this new composition.

The existing outer reduction already proposes fixed-class polynomial HSP
without bounded exponent. This direct route avoids its repeated cyclic
conversions only in the stated overlap and does not by itself identify a
hard native application. Standard multivariate lifting and integer module
arithmetic are not novel ingredients. A literature comparison and human
review are still required before making an originality claim.
