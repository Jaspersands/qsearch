# Subsystem 152: Expander Graph Ramanujan Spectral Gap & LDPC Syndrome Peeling Bridge

## 1. Mathematical Formulation
For a $d$-regular Ramanujan graph with $|\lambda_2| \le 2\sqrt{d-1}$, Tanner's eigenvalue bound guarantees vertex expansion:
$$\frac{|N(S)|}{|S|} \ge \frac{d^2}{\lambda_2^2 + (d^2 - \lambda_2^2)\alpha}.$$
At $d=10$, $\lambda_2 = 2\sqrt{9} = 6$ and the spectral gap is $d - \lambda_2 = 4.0$.
