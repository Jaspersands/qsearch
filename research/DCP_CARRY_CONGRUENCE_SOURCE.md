# Higher-Modulus Native Carry Source Compiler

This generalizes the exact q8 conditional source law to all dyadic phase
levels, without enumerating higher-label tables or quantum states. It is a
classical source-law certificate, not a quantum extractor or decoder.
No novelty, hardness, speedup or independently reviewed theorem is claimed.

CLI: `python qsearch.py dcp-carry-congruence-source`.
Artifact: `research/phase_workbench/dcp_carry_congruence_source.json`.
The earlier q8 reference remains in DCP_CARRY_SOURCE_LAW.md.

## Native Conditional Model

Let q=2^t, t>=3 and Q=q/2. Condition IID native labels on their binary low
matrix B, so A=B+2Z and each row of Z is independently uniform modulo Q.
Retained independent binary physical directions u_j and the complementary
measurement chart must be chosen from B and the initial syndrome ONLY.
The caller must establish this lineage; an API string does not verify it.
The flat native packet makes complement-Z outcomes independent of higher
labels and the hidden secret under that lineage. Set epsilon_i=(-1)^x_i
for the observed PHYSICAL background x.

The exact XOR polynomial is

    A(x xor sum_j y_j u_j)-Ax
      = sum_(nonempty S) (-2)^(|S|-1)
          [sum_i epsilon_i A_i product_(j in S) u_ji] product_(j in S) y_j.

Product extraction for EVERY secret requires all mixed coefficients to
vanish modulo q, not just their binary parities. For degree2<=h<=t require
`sum epsilon_i A_i e_S,i = 0 mod 2^(t-h+1)`. First its low B sum must be
even. For h<t this leaves the mixed-modulus linear congruence

    sum_i epsilon_i Z_i e_S,i
       = -[sum_i epsilon_i B_i e_S,i]/2 mod 2^(t-h).

Degree h=t gives only the low-bit test; higher degrees vanish. The linear
output label is `a'_j=sum epsilon_i A_i u_ji/2 mod Q`, an integer because
u_j lies in ker(B). Thus acceptance and output labels are one affine
congruence problem. The raw monomial menu can be exponential, but need not
be expanded. Intersections with identical nonzero physical support have the
SAME weighted A sum; only the SMALLEST mixed degree imposes the strongest
divisibility. The compiler processes fresh directions and retains minimal
generating subsets of each intersection, tracking mixed-degree witnesses
separately from singleton generators. Larger representations of one meet
cannot create a stronger extension. Zero intersections are discarded.

This exact meet-closure compression is output-sensitive, not a general
polynomial-size theorem. Its state/work counts and caps are explicit. An
independent full-subset reference checks small non-disjoint cases; disjoint
large cases are proved by all pairwise intersections being zero.

## Integer Lattices, Not Dense Label Tables

For constraints Rz=c modulo divisors D_i of Q, let Gamma_R be the integer
row lattice generated by the m COLUMN VECTORS of R and the diagonal D rows.
Write delta_R=[Z^c:Gamma_R]. There are prod(D_i)/delta_R reachable constraint
targets. An attainable target therefore has exact acceptance delta_R/prod(D_i).
HNF membership detects inconsistent targets; parity ranks alone do not.

For outputs Jz+o modulo Q, use the joint lattice generated by domain columns
`(R_column,J_column)` and all diagonal periods. Let its index be delta_joint.
Each accepted component is uniform on an affine output coset with support

    Q^d delta_R/delta_joint.

Components remain independent after their separate acceptance constraints,
so joint acceptance multiplies and conditional entropies add. A particular
linear-system solution supplies a COSET REPRESENTATIVE, not preparation of
an unknown quantum state. The compiler never supplies a free conditional
phase-state sampler.

### HNF Ordering Must Match Its Convention

FLINT returns upper-triangular ROW HNF. Place CONSTRAINT coordinates FIRST
and outputs LAST, yielding block form `[[C,B],[0,A]]`. The first block spans
the constraint projection, while the bottom-right A spans the output kernel
lattice, with det(A)=delta_joint/delta_R. Its rows give all permanent output
correlations, not merely an entropy estimate. Single-coordinate projections
use gcds of its columns; full marginals need not imply joint IID labels.

The earlier note's leading-block recipe used the opposite column-HNF
convention. It must not be transplanted to FLINT row HNF. For example,
`2z=0 mod4`, output z mod4 has support{0,2}, not four outputs. In output-first
ROW HNF the leading block is1 and would wrongly predict full support.

Every HNF certificate proves BOTH integer lattice containments with explicit
coefficient matrices. Matching determinants or a floating rational rank alone
does not establish the intended integer lattice.

## All-Background Invariance at Every Phase Level

The q8 background-invariance argument extends without enumeration. For each
component, make the uniform bijective change of variables

    Z'_li = epsilon_i Z_li - x_i B_li modulo Q.

Then `epsilon_i A_li = B_li+2Z'_li mod q`. All constraints and output labels
at background x become exactly their background-zero expressions in Z'.
Therefore the native acceptance and ENTIRE conditional affine output law
are background-invariant, under the declared low-defined selection lineage.
Changing directions after inspecting higher bits is NOT covered by this proof.

The highest bits of Z are absent from every residual constraint (D_i<=Q/2).
The retained direction matrix has full binary row rank, so these free highest
bits map surjectively to all d top output bits. Consequently those bits are
jointly uniform even when lower output coordinates have permanent correlations.
This does not classify an unconditional mixture over different B/selected bases.

## Research Use and Failure Modes

Preserve correlated output laws instead of rejecting everything that fails
an IID interface. They may be useful for a different collective decoder.
Nevertheless, every native acceptance penalty and repeated batch consumption
must be paid. Fixed-B calibration controls do not prove a growing native
family has useful acceptance; an observed B and postselecting a chosen B
have different costs. Neither source entropy nor a known affine subgroup is
an efficient quantum measurement or decoder.

Finite controls compare q8 with the independent older reference and exhaust
q8/q16 higher-label tables using the ORIGINAL phase function, not the compiler's
monomial equations. A q=2^64 control computes exact laws without a label grid.
Resource and selection-lineage caps are not impossibility theorems. Quantum
product-extraction gates, robustness, inter-level composition and hard natural
problem reductions remain separate proof obligations.

Probabilities use exact `{numerator_hex, denominator_binary_exponent}` records;
expected rejection attempts use `{power_of_two}`. Very large integer lattice
cardinalities use hexadecimal JSON strings, with no interpreter-wide digit
limit change. This avoids decimal overflow at large phase levels or component
counts. Original-register cost also includes the physical input width per batch.

A 64-output DISJOINT control at modulus2^1024 avoids expanding its
2^64-65 possible mixed monomials:2016 pair intersections certify that all are
zero. Its output support cardinality2^65472 is retained exactly. This is
fixed-B source algebra, not preparation of that many quantum states or a
typical-family throughput claim. The probability of any specified B under
unconditional IID low bits is recorded, but an observed random B is NOT
automatically an extra rejection filter. Postselecting a prescribed B is.
Reported label entropy is PUBLIC classical source entropy, not information
about the hidden secret or the quantum Hilbert-space dimension.

The large control has t64,n16,m5,d4 and fixed B=0. Its accepted output
law is fully IID modulo2^63, yet native acceptance is2^-992 and expected
supplied attempts2^992. This is a CONDITIONAL fixed-B calibration, not a
claim of a typical native family or a supplied resampling oracle. It
demonstrates why passing an IID-output interface does not pay acquisition.
The expected-attempt figure refers to INDEPENDENT supplied-batch rejection,
not all possible coherent preparation algorithms. A suitable parent/inverse
and amplitude-amplification access are NOT provided by this compiler; they
would require separate query, precision and normalization accounting.
