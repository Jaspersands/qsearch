# Pilot Coordinates With An Original-Module Verification Boundary

29 October 2026. An exact public-label compiler and capped controller for
the [spanning-block descent](SPANNING_BLOCK_REGULAR_SDE.md). This removes
an implementation gap between measured ambient labels and intrinsic SDE
coordinates. It is ordinary integer-module arithmetic, not a new hidden
subgroup algorithm, certified quantum receiver or speedup.

## 1. Scientific Question And Scope

Can the converter obtain useful intrinsic coordinates without knowing
the hidden central intersection, and remain correct when its pilot
samples do not generate the full character support?

**Yes at the public-label arithmetic boundary.** Compute exactly the
subgroup C' generated by the observed pilots, never estimate a quotient
by the hidden subgroup. Test bank membership in C', perform intrinsic
arithmetic, and verify cancellation back in the original ambient module.
Incomplete pilots can cause aborts, but cannot invalidate an accepted
ambient witness. Completeness is a probabilistic source assumption/event,
not a property inferred from a successful certificate.

The intended ambient input is explicitly coordinatized
`A = direct_sum_(j=1..R) Z/p^a_j`, with public odd prime p and 1<=d<p.
Raw labels have canonical integer coordinates. Integer arithmetic and
the entire bit lengths a_j*log2(p) are paid; the interface is not an
unexplained chart, black-box discrete-log oracle, or H-membership oracle.
The source contract for the HSP application remains uniform independent
legal labels from C=(H intersect Z)^perp, conditional on prior transcripts.

The mechanism does not itself give an asymptotic quantum advantage.
It enables the earlier conditional fixed-class source bound using public
coordinates. Its strongest classical baseline is exact integer linear
algebra, which this compiler already uses. A hard native application,
matched classical attacks, legal source production and full receiver
composition remain necessary for any speedup claim.

## 2. Exact Coordinate Construction

Let D=diag(p^a_j), let B have the pilot vectors as columns, and put
`G=[D B]`. The inverse image of C' in Z^R is the full-rank lattice
`L = G Z^(R+P)`, containing D Z^R. Find a column basis H of L and retain
integer matrices F,Q with BOTH containments:

```
G F = H,       H Q = G,       det(H) != 0.
```

These certify L=H Z^R without assuming that equal determinants or matching
invariant factors imply equal embedded subgroups. The full relation matrix
for the quotient in basis H is `T=H^-1 D`, with `H T=D` checked over Z.
Compute a Smith decomposition

```
U T V = S = diag(s_1,...,s_R),
det(U), det(V) in {+1,-1},    s_i>0,    s_i divides s_(i+1).
```

All factors must be powers of p. Remove the factors equal to one; the
remaining factors give intrinsic cyclic exponents e_i. Check each e_i
is at most max(a_j), and `prod(s_i)*abs(det(H))=prod(p^a_j)`.

For a canonical ambient label lambda, compute q=H^-1 lambda over Q.
If q is not integral, lambda is not in C'. Otherwise its coordinates are
`z=U q mod S`, omitting trivial factors. To embed z, pad trivial coordinates
with zero and compute `H U^-1 z mod D`.

**The embedding uses U^-1, not V.** V changes relation generators, not
the coordinates of the ambient free module. Since U T=S V^-1,
changing lambda by D times an integer changes U H^-1 lambda by a vector
in S Z^R. Thus membership and coordinates are independent of the ambient
representative. Conversely, coordinate equivalence modulo S is precisely
equivalence modulo D after embedding. The maps are inverse additive
homomorphisms. They commute with integer scalar multiplication, including
the scalar s^d used by the phase-cancellation equations.

The verifier checks both lattice containments, H T=D, the full Smith
identity, unimodularity, p-primary factors, subgroup order, pilot
roundtrips and the exact orders of embedded cyclic generators. It does
not rerun HNF/SNF or enumerate the group. Certificates keep the complete
maps, not just the list of invariant factors.

Implementation uses the existing FLINT row-HNF convention, transposing
the resulting basis and transformation to obtain column maps. See
[python-flint's HNF transformation interface](https://python-flint.readthedocs.io/en/latest/fmpz_mat.html).
SymPy 1.14.0's installed `smith_normal_decomp` supplies S,U,V; its
documented source identity is S=U*T*V. Every returned transformation is
checked rather than trusted by convention. No new arithmetic dependency
was installed and no normal-form algorithm was hand-written.

## 3. Episode Policy And Accounting

`pilot_module_episode` validates the degree, backend, callback and required
RNG protocol BEFORE acquiring labels. It then requests exactly R+2 new
pilots, compiles C', and records the certificate.

- For positive intrinsic rank r, each raw bank request has r+1 labels.
  Record and charge the WHOLE request, then map every label into C'. A
  nonmember aborts the whole episode. Otherwise the existing capped
  spanning-block policy selects blocks and lifts a witness.
- For rank zero, request ONE additional label and accept with scale one
  only when that label is exactly zero in the ambient module. Zero
  pilots alone never authorize an output.
- Map each selected intrinsic label back to its recorded ambient source
  position, verify exact equality, and then independently check
  `sum_i lambda_i*s_i^d = 0 mod p^a_j` in EVERY original ambient row.
  Check nonzero witness and unit-or-exact-zero scales as well.
- `pilot_module_search` retries only whole episodes, with fresh R+2
  pilots and a new bank each time. The episode count is capped. All failed
  pilots, membership failures and rejected raw blocks remain charged.
- Invalid callbacks, incorrect certificates and backend defects raise
  errors. They are not disguised as ordinary statistical failures.

Incomplete pilots cannot corrupt correctness: all accepted inputs are
members of the exactly known C', the embedding is a homomorphism, and
the final equation is also verified directly in A. This does not infer
that C'=C. In particular the compiler does not estimate H intersect Z.

Conditional on complete pilots, the NEW bank is IID uniform in C in the
computed coordinates, for every fixed valid frame. Therefore the earlier
availability calculation applies: pilot incompleteness<=1/16, block
exhaustion<=2^-b, and field abort<=2^-b for b>=4. With probability at
least 13/16 an episode succeeds. For t fresh episodes the probability
that all abort is at most (3/16)^t, under the conditional source/randomness
promises. There is no requirement that episode outcomes be unconditionally
independent; the conditional failure bound suffices by induction.

Use the ambient R, not observed r, for the uniform cost cap B_R from the
preceding note. Per-episode consumption<=B_R, a t-episode run costs at
most t*B_R, and the unbounded ideal retry expectation is <=16*B_R/13.
Rankzero consumes R+3<=B_R. Actual reports distinguish raw labels consumed
from these conditional predecessor-source bounds.

## 4. Falsifiers And Remaining Assumptions

- **Ambient mod-p collapse:** pZ/p^2 has intrinsic rank one although
  all ambient labels reduce to zero mod p. Reducing before compiling the
  module loses a genuine phase; this case is tested explicitly.
- **Oblique embedding:** in Z/9 + Z/27, pilots (3,3),(0,9) generate a
  cyclic order-nine subgroup, not a coordinate-axis product. Substituting
  the right Smith transform for the left fails the certificate.
- **Order is insufficient:** replacing Smith factors (1,9) by (3,3)
  keeps cardinality nine but changes the group. The full identity rejects it.
- **Missing pilots:** all-zero pilots followed by a nonzero bank label
  must abort, take NEW pilots, and charge both episodes. The live control
  consumes 15 labels across this failure and the successful retry.
- **Incomplete but safe:** pilots generating only 3Z/9 can accept a bank
  in 3Z/9 even if the actual support were larger. The native equations
  still hold; the result never certifies hidden-support completeness.
- **Corrupted intrinsic output:** altered scales or raw labels are caught
  at the independent ambient equation/provenance boundary.
- **Availability is not physics:** callbacks returning chosen or seeded
  labels do not prove source independence, freshness, one-use ownership
  of quantum registers, or a valid Fourier-measurement implementation.
- **Runtime is not formalized:** standard constructive integer normal
  forms provide a polynomial-bit theoretical route. This pass verifies
  exact outputs of installed backends, not their worst-case bit complexity,
  execution clock or a failure-aware quantum circuit implementation.
- **Small-field solver gaps remain:** unsupported regimes fail before
  pilots. The implementation does not silently import the published
  general field solver, even when a trivial frame might later be observed.

No failed classical attack or bounded arithmetic experiment is taken as
hardness evidence. There is no claim of novelty for these coordinate maps.

### Independent Review And Dependency Defect

Independent reviewer Hooke found a P1 availability defect in the initial
implementation. Installed python-flint 0.9.0 returned the NEGATIVE inverse
on the integer-inverse path for `U=[[0,-1],[-1,0]]`, whose determinant
is -1: `U*U.inv(integer=True)=-I`. Exact rational inversion gives I.
This was independently reproduced before editing. The valid pilot inputs
`p3, a=(3,2), B=[(1,4),(1,1)]` and sorted `a=(2,2,3),
B=[(5,3,24),(4,4,1)]` previously raised a roundtrip error. They failed
closed, not with a false witness, but that still invalidated the advertised
success guarantee for the affected implementation.

The fix converts the exact rational inverse to integers and explicitly
checks both inverse products against I before constructing the embedding.
Both frames and a controller-level availability regression now pass.
Tests require the correct result rather than requiring the library defect
to persist. A search found no remaining `inv(integer=True)` call in this
repository's `theorems/` or `tests/`; no dependency or unrelated module was
changed. The pre-fix 577-test green run did NOT expose this defect.

The reviewer supplied additional certificate falsifiers, reproduced here:

- For `D=9,B=3`, an overlarge claimed lattice H=1 can pass the reverse
  containment and pilot checks. The forward integer containment H=G F
  prevents pretending that the pilots generate all of Z/9.
- With `D=diag(9,27)`, `H=[[4,1],[1,1]]`, `U=[[1,0],[1,1]]`,
  `S=diag(3,27)`, the wrong pair of maps U^-1 H^-1 and H U passes both
  pilot roundtrips. It maps label (5,2) to (1,0), falsely suggesting that
  three copies cancel; the actual ambient sum is (6,6). Exact embedded
  generator order and the prescribed Smith orientation reject this.
- A permutation swapping 2 and 4 in Z/9 round-trips every element and
  fixes its generator, but falsely maps labels 2,5 to a zero sum. This
  illustrates why a bijection is not enough: linear certification matters.

The reviewer found the proposed module isomorphism, incomplete-pilot
soundness and pre-pilot probability bounds valid under the stated source
promises. Independent checks included all 729 pilot pairs in C3 times C9
and rejected-block, late-nonmembership, field-abort and retry accounting.
This is automated review, not a human peer review or Lean proof.
