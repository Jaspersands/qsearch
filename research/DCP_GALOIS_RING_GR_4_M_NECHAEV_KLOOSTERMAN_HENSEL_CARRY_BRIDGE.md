# Subsystem 148: Galois Ring $GR(4, m)$ Teichmüller Lift & $\mathbb{Z}_4$ Carry Decomposition Bridge

## 1. Mathematical Formulation
In $GR(4, m) = \mathbb{Z}_4[x]/(h(x))$, every element has a unique 2-adic Teichmüller expansion $u = c_0 + 2c_1$ with $c_0, c_1 \in \mathcal{T}_m$ ($a^{2^m} = a$). Adding $a, b \in \mathcal{T}_m$ yields $a + b = c_0 + 2c_1$ where $c_1^2 = a \cdot b$ in $\mathcal{T}_m$ ($c_1 = \sqrt{\bar{a}\bar{b}}$), proving algebraically why radix-2 carries inject degree-2 bilinear products into $\mathbb{F}_2$ CNOT circuits.
