# DCP Multi-Copy Schur-Weyl Symmetric-Subspace Projection and Classical $r$-Sample Majority Bridge

## 1. Secret-Independent Symmetric Subspace Projection Weight
For $r$ copies of a single-qubit DCP phase state $\rho(\theta, \gamma) = \frac{1}{2}\bigl(I + \gamma(\cos\theta\, X + \sin\theta\, Y)\bigr)$ with $\theta = 2\pi \langle a, s \rangle / q$, the orthogonal projector $\Pi_{\mathrm{Sym}^r}$ onto the $(r+1)$-dimensional Dicke symmetric subspace $\mathrm{Sym}^r(\mathbb{C}^2)$ has exact $\theta$-independent probability:
$$P(\mathrm{Sym}^r \mid \theta, \gamma) = \mathrm{Tr}\!\big(\Pi_{\mathrm{Sym}^r} \, \rho(\theta, \gamma)^{\otimes r}\big) = \sum_{k=0}^r \left(\frac{1+\gamma}{2}\right)^{r-k} \left(\frac{1-\gamma}{2}\right)^k.$$

## 2. Commuting Half-Period Optimality vs. Scoped Non-Commuting Quadrature Separation
- **Half-Period Commuting Regime ($\theta \in \{0, \pi\}$)**: $\rho(0, \gamma)$ and $\rho(\pi, \gamma)$ commute and are simultaneously diagonal in the $X$ basis, so unentangled single-copy $X$ measurements with classical $r$-sample Binomial majority voting achieve the exact $2^r \times 2^r$ collective quantum Helstrom trace distance. Pure $\mathrm{Sym}^r(\mathbb{C}^2)$ postselection at $r \ge 3, 0 < \gamma < 1$ discards positive signal carried by mixed-symmetry Schur-Weyl sectors.
- **Non-Commuting Quadrature Regime ($\theta = \pi/2$) & Dicke Harmonics**: The symmetric Dicke observable $O_{\mathrm{GHZ}}^{(r)} = |0^{\otimes r}\rangle\langle 1^{\otimes r}| + |1^{\otimes r}\rangle\langle 0^{\otimes r}|$ has expectation $2^{-(r-1)} \gamma^r \cos(r\theta)$ (matched without entangling gates by random even-$Y$ Pauli strings). For distinguishing $\theta = 0$ vs $\theta = \pi/2$ on $r \ge 2$ identical headers, collective Schur-Weyl Helstrom readout strictly outperforms any fixed single-copy measurement basis with classical $r$-sample likelihood decoding, but requires an $r$-way exact header collision ($M = \Omega(Q^{(r-1)/r})$ under IID uniform headers).
