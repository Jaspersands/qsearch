# Consecutive-Pole Plane Adapter For The Recovered Curve

Date: 2026-10-26. Parent derivation; independent review pending.
This is a constructive complexity argument with symbolic controls, not a
native genus41 adapter or an actual Jacobian-order computation.

## Claim Under The Graded-Algebra Contract

Let X/F_q be the actual smooth geometrically integral recovered curve,
g>=1, H the recovered degree-ell line bundle with ell=2g+2, and O any
recovered rational point. The marked point reconstructed by the lift is
sufficient; an extra hidden point at infinity is unnecessary. Assume exact
R_j=H^0(jH) multiplication through degree4 over F_q and a certified compact
section-ring representation as in
`../compressed-graded-algebra-2026-10-26/HANDOFF.md` (possibly over a known
finite extension for internal arithmetic). Then a birational plane equation
over ORIGINAL F_q of total degree at most2g+2 can be constructed by
polynomially many finite-field operations. All representation conversions,
base changes and coefficient sizes remain charged.

No generic plane projection, unbounded Groebner solver, zeta input, canonical
divisor, halving operation or torsion basis is used in this adapter.

## 1. Compute The One-Point Spaces By Bounded Products

Set W_j=H^0(2H-jO) subset R_2 for0<=j<=ell. W_0=R_2; W_1 is the
evaluation kernel at the known O. Iteratively compute

```text
U_j=span(W_j W_1) subset R_4,
W_(j+1)={z in R_2 : z R_2 subset U_j},  1<=j<ell.
```

Each factor has residual degree at least ell+1>=2g+3 (for W_j at the
last step) or2ell-1. The mixed multiplication theorem therefore fills
H^0(4H-(j+1)O). Since R_2 is globally generated, division by all of R_2
imposes exactly vanishing along (j+1)O, including multiplicity. This is
the product/colon mechanism already reviewed for the sampler conversion.

Choose any nonzero h in W_ell and write E=div(h), a section divisor of
degree2ell with E>=ell O. For j=2g,2g+1,2g+2 compute

```text
V_j={z in R_2 : z W_j subset h R_2}.
```

The base divisor of W_j is exactly jO because the residual line bundle
2H-jO has degree>=ell>=2g+2 and is globally generated. The containment
thus says div(z)+jO>=E. Consequently

```text
V_j = h L(jO).
```

These are linear containment systems using only degree2 times degree2.
They do not require an a priori function-field model or named local
parameter for computing high-order vanishing. Riemann--Roch gives
dim V_j=j+1-g throughout this range.

Put m=2g+1 and n=m+1=ell. Choose z_x in V_m minus V_(m-1) and z_y
in V_n minus V_m, using F_q-linear elimination. Define

```text
x=z_x/h,  y=z_y/h.
```

These are functions over F_q, with pole divisors EXACTLY mO and nO,
respectively. The internal choices h,z_x,z_y remain explicit degree-two
polynomials in the original recovered section coordinates. They are not
chosen over the extension used for optional compact-module arithmetic.

## 2. Coprime Poles Certify Birationality

Let K=F_q(x,y) inside L=F_q(X), and let pi:X->Y be the corresponding
finite map to the smooth projective model of K. The pole of x on Y has
only O above it, since x has only one pole on X. Its image Q is rational
because O is rational, so its residue degree is1. The fundamental degree
formula gives

```text
[L:K]=e(O/Q),
[L:K] divides ord_O(x)=-m and ord_O(y)=-n.
```

As gcd(m,n)=1, [L:K]=1. This also rules out a nontrivial inseparable
degree. The pair really generates the SAME function field, not merely a
finite quotient whose Jacobian would be different. One may choose the
separating coordinate among x,y using the pole order not divisible by p,
but the degree argument itself does not assume p>2g+2.

## 3. Relation Size And Recovery

Since [L:F_q(x)]=m and L=F_q(x,y), the minimal polynomial of y is

```text
F(U,V)=V^m+sum_(j=0..m-1) a_j(U)V^j.
```

y has no pole above a finite x-value, so it is integral over F_q[x],
and every a_j lies in F_q[U]. There is a unique place of L above the
infinite place of F_q(x). Normalizing that base valuation by v_infty(x)=-1,
every conjugate root, counted with inseparable multiplicity if needed,
has valuation -n/m. The elementary-symmetric coefficient bound therefore
gives

```text
deg a_j <= floor((m-j)n/m).
```

The norm gives deg a_0=n. Thus F has weighted degree mn for weights(m,n),
and total degree at most n. The possible monomials form the set

```text
M={(i,j): 0<=j<=m, 0<=i<=floor((m-j)n/m)}.
```

There are O(g^2) of them, and i+j<=n. Compute the sections

```text
z_x^i z_y^j h^(n-i-j) in R_(2n),  (i,j) in M.
```

The polynomial-kernel relation is unique up to a scalar: the relation ideal
of x,y is generated by their irreducible minimal F, whose weighted degree
is mn; no nonconstant multiple fits this weighted bound. Solve the linear
kernel and normalize the V^m coefficient to1. Require kernel dimension1
and this nonzero leading coefficient. Multiplication into R_(2n) uses the
compact ring representation, not evaluation on the finite native columns.

There is no fixed-degree claim here:2n=4g+4 grows with genus. In the compact
free module, however, R_(2n) has dimension2n*ell+1-g=O(g^2), and matrix
entries in the generator actions have bounded polynomial degree. Computing
the O(g^2) sections and their kernel therefore has polynomial cost. A dense
monomial basis in N=O(g) original variables would NOT have that cost.

If the compact module was built over F_(q^b), use the original F_q sections
h,z_x,z_y in it. Geometric integrality preserves the same one-dimensional
relation kernel on base change. Uniqueness of the monic relation forces
every normalized coefficient to be fixed by q-Frobenius. Check that and
extract the coefficients in the known base-field embedding. We do NOT
count the curve over F_(q^b) and infer its F_q order. The final equation
itself is over F_q. This is the required descent interface.

## 4. Consequence For The Fixed-Characteristic Order Gate

[Lauder--Wan, Corollary3](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book44/files/18lauder.pdf)
computes the Jacobian order of the smooth projective model from a possibly
singular geometrically irreducible plane equation, in time polynomial in
its degree, p and the finite-field extension degree. Its weight-one factor
extraction handles the difference between the affine plane curve and its
smooth projective model. Hence, conditional on the certified adapter above,
the actual fixed p=100003 family has a theoretical polynomial order route.
This is not a practical runtime guarantee or uniform polynomial dependence
on log p when the characteristic grows.

The compiled native adapter and point counter have NOT been run. The
compact-module construction has a separate pending review; its all-degree
certificate is a real dependency. The order route is not a complete native
full-support solver: the effective mod-two divisor search can still cost
2^ceil(r_2/3), and canonical/differential/native normalization for the final
auxiliary solve must be supplied. Actual higher tower inputs are not created
by proving an algorithm that accepts a promised input matrix.

## 5. Falsifiers And Control

Break this reduction by finding a missing base divisor in the colon formula,
an uncharged growing-degree representation, a function-field degree not
dividing both pole orders, a relation outside the weighted bound, or a failed
base-field descent. Mere vanishing on native sample columns cannot certify
the relation or its function field.

The symbolic control uses the elliptic curve eta^2=t^3+2t+2 over F_17.
Take x=eta (pole3 at O), y=t^2 (pole4). Their plane equation is

```text
y(y+2)^2-(x^2-2)^2=0,
```

monic of degree3 in y and total degree4, with rational inverse
t=(x^2-2)/(y+2), eta=x. It is intentionally a possibly singular plane
presentation; checking equality only on rational points would be too weak.
The test uses exact polynomial reduction and section coordinates. It is
a control of this adapter, not a tiny quantum candidate or its algorithm.
