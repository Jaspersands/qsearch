## Qualifications Retained

- Cumulative adjoint closure uses all specified generators. Witnesses are
  constructed separately over each legal prime, not inferred from one
  rational determinant or a few passing examples.
- D is the actual free-Lie dimension in group order and fiber identities.
  D_* may replace it only in conservative bounds and the planner.
- Good pushforward mass is unnormalized; only nonsingular roots in a fixed
  D-variable slice are bounded. Singular fibers may be large.
- The convolution is (T^*T)^m. No positivity of the good-part transform or
  Hermiticity of the original curve mean is assumed.
- Coefficients belong to the prescribed lower-central terms. Arbitrary
  noncommuting coefficients in the same ambient class do not suffice.
- Each packet shares one scale internally. Different packets have fresh
  independent scales and IID native copies. These are different obligations.
- The source is pure, the action linear and coherently uniform, and the
  action precision and acquisition costs are charged. The receiver and
  moment design remain imported obligations.
- No same-gap small-prime extension, growing-class algorithm, mixed-state
  source theorem, natural hard task, novelty or advantage is asserted.
