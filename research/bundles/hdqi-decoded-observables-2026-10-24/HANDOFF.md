# Handoff: Decoded Physical Observable Comparator

The local proof attacks the ACTUAL decoded output, not the pilot in its
place. A physical Pauli observable pulls back to a partial signed permutation
of pilot labels. Conditional signed words estimate its numerator with second
moment <=1; a separate signed denominator estimates 1/C. Median-of-means,
rational square-root weights and analytic C_bar give a fully priced additive
expectation estimator whenever C_bar and decoder runtime are polynomial.

## Integration Tasks For Gemini

1. Integrate only after the main checkout migration is resolved. Preserve
   the explicit physical Pauli list, ordered-product phases, decoder identity
   and failure convention in every artifact. Do not use an unrelated output.
2. Require a reproducible, bounded-runtime decoder implementation. An opaque
   callable is currently the research interface, not proof of its complexity.
3. Keep diagnostic draws visibly uncertified. Full median-of-means costs are
   conservative and explicitly budgeted; insufficient budgets produce no
   certified estimate. Use confidence-aware optimizations only with proofs.
4. Add per-Pauli and energy-density reporting with coefficient L1 normalization
   and requested accuracy. Do not confuse density accuracy with total energy.
5. Do not clip importance weights. Rare large weights are legal and accounted
   for by second moments, not bounded-range concentration.
6. Separate actual-decoder output accuracy from Gibbs-state accuracy. A bad
   decoder is still a valid comparator target but may not prepare a Gibbs state.

## Research Priority

In the polynomial-sign-cost regime, coherent pilot preparation alone does
not support a physical energy-estimation advantage: this is a matched
classical attack. Look beyond additive Pauli readouts, or beyond that regime,
with an explicit decoder and meaningful output. Full physical sampling and
rare conditional events remain open; do not infer them from these estimates.

This is local and unreviewed, with no novelty or universal dequantization
claim. No quantum circuit, extra reviewer, registry admission or Git change.
