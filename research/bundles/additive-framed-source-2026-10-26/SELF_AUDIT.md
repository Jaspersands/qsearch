## What Could Kill It

1. A nonintegral translation that becomes an integral framed isomorphism.
   The proposed converse works in the FULL unknown End(E): separately
   labeled matrix units force two repeated 2-by-2 blocks, the cross equation
   identifies their inverse adjoints, and two pointwise inclusions force
   an integral unipotent shear. Testing only scalar matrices would not check
   noncommutative order mistakes. Exact tests use genuinely noncommuting
   quaternion elements as well as rational nonintegral shears.
2. Torsion markings encode the wrong equivalence relation. Explicit negative
   controls exhibit false coset collisions after removing EITHER inclusion.
   They also show why level two alone misses sign changes. Ordered full
   graphs are too strong: they reject legal shears. Relations must be sets
   of pairs under ONE common automorphism, with a uniform norm-based lift
   bound and consistent field embeddings. No implemented native encoder
   currently guarantees this contract.
3. A principal matrix is not a represented PPAV. Positivity and unimodularity
   do not implement a theta-model converter. The proposed converter uses
   the explicit quadratic identity and existing smooth quotient argument;
   it does not invoke an abstract representation-existence axiom. Native
   small-prime-square kernels, flag refinement, descent, canonicalization
   and marking transport remain unexecuted as a combined source.
4. Canonical labels leak construction choices. Intermediate four-square
   witnesses, kernel generators, flags, random roots and isomorphisms must
   disappear from the final encoding. Canonicalization applies to the
   represented variety and the WHOLE framed object. A source that retains
   any of that auxiliary workspace fails coherent coset invariance.
   The initial four-square matrix T is DIFFERENT: it is part of the framed
   polarization construction and must be fixed once for the entire query
   domain. Two nonproportional choices of equal norm cannot satisfy the
   frame-preserving cross equation, even at the same gamma. An explicit
   equal-norm countercontrol rules out fresh T choices per query. Only
   downstream converter witnesses may vary before canonicalization.
5. Index checks accept a false ring. This happens in an explicit test:
   R=Z+3O has index 27. A spurious order-27 subgroup generated by the scalar
   coordinate passes the lattice determinant check but outputs 1/27.
   Its norm is 1/729, so it is not an endomorphism. Native isogeny division
   of every output basis map is a separate, indispensable certificate.
6. A classical algorithm extracts the same lattice from these labels.
   No native classical lower bound is proved. The competitor receives E,
   every alpha_i, their traces, evaluators and the entire geometric labels.
   Membership-only and opaque-collision lower bounds do not apply to that
   stronger interface. In particular, search for direct use of the PPAV
   automorphism algorithms, lattice saturation and known ideal techniques.
7. The argument is prior art or an insignificant repackaging. Full-suborder
   quantum-polynomial EndRing is already published. No title/keyword search
   proves this construction novel. Its only prospective distinction is a
   direct abelian label with no reference-curve dictionary or powersmooth
   lift. A claim of removing heuristic/GRH assumptions requires a complete
   comparison of every imported subroutine, not an abstract-level contrast.

## Checks That Currently Support The Proposal

- The identities for L, its adjugate, Z, integral inverse and translation
  covariance are exact noncommutative matrix identities. Their proofs do
  not assume knowledge of O. Fixtures use known O only to check arithmetic.
- Positivity follows by congruence for L and its exact Schur complement for
  Z. Testing numeric eigenvalues is unnecessary and weaker.
- The scalar frame centralizer calculation is coefficientwise over any
  quaternion order. A rational finite control has the expected eight
  degrees of freedom; it is not an exhaustive geometric-isomorphism search.
- Full rational rank of the supplied native maps makes Frobenius central.
  This uses the given suborder, not an unavailable maximal-order basis.
- The denominator d need not be coprime to p. Only the auxiliary torsion
  primes must avoid p. A characteristic-divisible arithmetic index control
  is included; inseparable native division itself was not executed here.
- H has order d. Its recovered lattice Gamma has determinant d^3, not d.
  Incomplete exact annihilator samples give a supergroup, hence the wrong
  determinant unless recovery is already complete. This argument does not
  certify outputs after source errors; integrality must still be checked.

## Resource And Value Assessment

There are 128 geometric dimensions, 2048 graph relations, s^256 pairs per
graph at prime s, and 2^256 theta coordinates per point. These are explicit
and fatal to practicality, even though fixed dimension permits a formal
polynomial-time theorem. Do not start implementing a dimension-128 backend
just to produce activity. First validate the source theorem and its novelty.

The input supplies FOUR rationally independent native endomorphisms. It is
not a solution from a bare supersingular curve or one non-scalar map. The
rank-two commuting case cannot generate the missing noncommuting access;
its saturation is closely related to already-known primitivisation. This
input barrier is more important than improving the tiny arithmetic controls.

## Decision And Next Decisive Work

Retain as an unreviewed candidate REDUCTION, not an admitted breakthrough.
The next useful review target is the complete equivalence and native
source-cost composition, including transport of unordered graph relations.
Prioritize a search for a classical extraction algorithm using the same
PPAV primitives and a precise prior-art comparison. Do not generate an
opaque oracle and call recovering its supplied subgroup experimental
evidence of quantum advantage. Do not spend GPT usage wiring UI around it.

An actual flaw in framing, native model construction, fixed-field labeling,
or output verification falsifies the claimed reduction. A matched classical
polynomial algorithm removes its speedup claim but can itself be valuable.
Neither has been excluded by the current checks. Novelty and asymptotic
advantage remain unestablished.
