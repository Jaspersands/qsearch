# Three-Probe Orthogonal Reduction: Predeclared Falsifiers

Input is the standard full-stabilizer HSP in split O+(2n,F_p) or SO+(2n,F_p),
odd p and n>=3. No numeric hidden-subspace label is given to the controller.
The proposed algorithm uses three line-radical abelian HSPs and an exact
membership certificate, not the Rees receiver or an additive ambient oracle.

Test before promoting the written argument:

1. The public E_e(w) matrices are legal, additive in w, and injective.
2. For e outside L their stabilizer kernel is the projection of L intersect
   e-perp, and the recovered neighbor is (L intersect e-perp)+<e>.
3. Three quotient-independent probes reconstruct L through pair intersections.
4. An EXACT certificate accepts no wrong maximal singular subspace, even on
   degenerate probes or inaccurate Fourier output.
5. A dense finite reference uses ONLY opaque oracle labels and explicitly
   charges its exponential classical table, rather than claiming quantum time.
6. The n=2 boundary fails as predicted; it cannot be silently extrapolated.
7. Generic labels expose no vector coordinates. Classical comparison covers
   the full group oracle, not only the selected probes.

The result, if controls survive, is a proposed oracle-problem reduction.
Independent proof/novelty review and any useful non-oracle application remain
open. No synthetic candidate admission or Shor-level claim is authorized.
