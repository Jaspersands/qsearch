parity-conditioned words and performs controlled-bias rejection. It needs
no amplitude oracle. Every probability computation is rational/integer,

better of the mass bound and the new cluster-derived bound in its exact
admissible regime, with C=1 on an edgeless graph. The cluster bound depends
on the previous local unreviewed proof and extends coverage to an explicit
growing-degree family outside both older comparator regimes. No empirical
C estimate is trusted.
