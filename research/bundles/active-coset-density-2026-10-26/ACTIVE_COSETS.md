## 2. Conjugation Is Quadratic In A Hidden Translation

The source congruence for any integral delta in O is

    Z_(beta+d delta) = S_delta^dagger Z_beta S_delta,
    G_(beta+d delta) = S_delta^-1 G_beta S_delta.

Write S_delta=I+N_delta. In each first-half 2x2 block N_delta has only
upper-right entry delta; in each second-half block it has only lower-left
entry -delta^dagger. Thus N_delta is additive, and

    N_delta N_epsilon = 0  for ALL delta,epsilon,
    S_delta^-1 = I-N_delta,
    S_(delta+epsilon) = S_delta S_epsilon.

For a fixed w in G_beta the obstruction to membership in M_g(R) is the
additive-quotient-valued function

    F_w(delta) = w+w N_delta-N_delta w-N_delta w N_delta  mod M_g(R).

It has total degree at most two in any integral basis of O, equivalently
every mixed third finite difference vanishes. The quotient is an ADDITIVE
group; R need not be an ideal. No multiplication on O/R is being assumed.

Because dO is contained in R, changing delta by d eta changes this expression
by a matrix in d M_g(O), hence in M_g(R). Therefore F_w is well-defined on
B=O/dO. Its VALUES need not descend to O/R. Its ZERO EVENT does: for r in R,
S_r and S_r^-1 have entries in R, so conjugation by S_r preserves membership
in M_g(R) in both directions. Here r^dagger is in R since
r^dagger=Trd(r)-r. Thus uniform delta in B gives the same zero-event
probability as uniform delta in O/R, which parametrizes beta+H.

## 3. An Active Coset Has Constant Conditional Success Probability

Call beta+H active if at least one query in it has a useful symmetry.
Let ell_min be the least prime divisor of d. Then EVERY active coset obeys

    Pr[useful query | this coset] >= 1-2/ell_min >= 1/3.

Proof. Choose a witness w from G_beta for which F_w is nonzero somewhere.
Let C be the subgroup generated by its values in M_g(O)/M_g(R). C is a
nonzero finite abelian group of exponent dividing d. Choose a nonzero
homomorphism chi:C -> F_ell for some prime ell dividing |C|. Such a map
exists, ell divides d, and f=chi F_w is not identically zero because the
values generate C. Mixed third differences of f still vanish.

For any h in B, discrete degree two gives

    f(x+ell h) = f(x)+ell Delta_h f(x)+binom(ell,2) Delta_h^2 f(x) = f(x),

since ell is odd. Therefore f factors through B/ell B, isomorphic to
F_ell^4. A function on this vector space with zero mixed third differences
is an ordinary polynomial of total degree at most two: its second
difference b(x,y) is biadditive, and
f(x)-f(0)-b(x,x)/2 is additive. In particular, it is a constant plus a
linear form plus a quadratic form.

The standard polynomial zero bound gives Pr[f=0]<=2/ell. For completeness,
write a nonzero degree-D polynomial as a polynomial of degree k in its
last variable, with nonzero leading coefficient of degree at most D-k.
The leading coefficient vanishes on at most (D-k)/ell of the other inputs
by induction. Else the last-variable polynomial has at most k roots.
This bounds the zero fraction by D/ell. Apply with D<=2. Since f!=0
implies F_w!=0, the useful-query fraction is at least 1-2/ell, hence at
least 1-2/ell_min. The zero-event quotient observation completes the proof.

This is the usual Schwartz--Zippel zero bound, not a new polynomial lemma;
see [CMU Advanced Algorithms, Theorem8.3](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs.cmu.edu/academic/class/15850-f20/www/notes/cmu850-f20.pdf).
The new application here is to the source's hidden shear conjugation.

Let rho be the fraction of ACTIVE cosets among the d^3 cosets. The sampler
under study satisfies the exact sandwich

    (1-2/ell_min)*rho <= p_hit <= rho.

Thus an inverse-polynomial uniform lower bound on rho is equivalent, up
to a constant factor, to the missing uniform hitting theorem. Nothing
here proves such a bound. If only one coset is active, its contribution
can still be of order d^-3, exponential in the bit length of d.

CRITICAL ACCESS LIMIT: delta in O/R is unknown. This is a counting argument,
NOT an algorithm to sample conditional translations or to find an active
coset. Uniform native beta gives a uniform conditional law automatically;
we may not replace it by supplied-O sampling in a native benchmark.

## 4. Pell Frames: The Entire Integral Coset Is Classified

Suppose d^2-2k^2=1 and use roots (2k^2,2k,0,0). The earlier exact scalar

    G_(d delta) not contained in M_g(R)  iff  delta not in R.

The reverse direction of the containment statement is immediate:
G_0 has entries in R because Aut(E) is already in R, and S_delta is then
invertible over R when delta is in R.

For the other direction, suppose G_(d delta) is contained in M_g(R).
Signed permutation matrices are in Mon(Aut(E)). The integer algebra they
generate contains 2 M_g(Z): subtract a coordinate sign flip from I to
get 2e_ii, and multiply by permutations to get every 2e_ij. Conjugating
by the integral unimodular P leaves M_g(Z) unchanged. Thus the algebra
generated by G_(d delta) contains

    2 S_delta^-1 M_g(Z) S_delta.

Choose i at the first coordinate of an upper-triangular shear block.
The corresponding matrix S_delta^-1 e_ii S_delta has entries 1 at (i,i)
and delta at (i,i+1), and zero elsewhere. Therefore 2delta is in R.
Because O/R has odd order d, this forces delta in R. This argument uses
the ENTIRE group/algebra; one need not individually identify a particular
monomial generator to prove existence of a useful returned symmetry.

There are exactly d-1 useful classes among the d queries of H. In particular
the earlier complete zero-query no-growth result is preserved: zero is the
ONLY failing query of this coset, not evidence that the whole coset fails.
This is conditional on a Pell index/frame, not a theorem for all frames.

## 6. Attempts To Break Or Overextend The Argument

- Composite d and a nonideal R: use a prime quotient of the subgroup
  generated by VALUES, not a nonexistent quotient ring or a coefficient
  reduction that could discard every nonzero value.
- Even d: the odd-characteristic polynomial reduction and 2delta inference
  fail. The function x*y on F_2^2 has support1/4, below1/3. Keep 2-saturation.
- Incomplete automorphism information: the upper/lower sandwich is for
  COMPLETE unmarked information. Testing only known generators without
  completeness provenance cannot establish that a coset is blind.
- Changing T during translation: the conjugation identity uses a fixed
  frame. A mixture of frames can be analyzed conditionally per frame, not
  by silently identifying different source families.
- Losing information in the converter: Q must be coprime to d. The older
  exact bad-multiplier counterexample still applies.
- Unknown translations: the polynomial argument supplies no native hitting
  set. Even a small interpolation stencil would require unavailable O
  coordinates unless separately constructed from native data.
- Pell examples: neither the index17 control nor a special Pell index
  proves a distributional claim for arbitrary proper/non-Bass orders.
- Classical advantage: many hits within H are still exponentially rare
  across A and already covered by native division. Do not report them as
  unexplained quantum-source leakage.
