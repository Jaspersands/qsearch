# Matrix-Only One-Point Lift Removes The Quadratic Boundary

## Result And Scope

Using only the original parity matrix, compute an evaluation representation
of O_X(G+P) from that of O_X(G). This is an application of the known Schur
quotient identity, not a new curve-reconstruction theorem. Under the complete
AG-code promise with d>=2g+1 and m>=2d+4, the lifted embedding has degree
at least2g+2 and its ideal is generated by quadrics. Thus the boundary issue
in the preceding MATRIX_GEOMETRY.md can be bypassed without changing the
native input class or solving a different search problem.

The implementation constructs the lifted matrix. It does not construct a
Jacobian backend, a zeta polynomial, an auxiliary divisor, or a native witness.
Dimension checks do not independently recognize a complete AG code.

## Exact Identity

Choose any evaluation point P (a nonzero column). Internally puncture P,
writing D'=D-P and n=m-1. From B compute, by linear algebra,

    V0 = ev_D' L(G),
    Vminus = ev_D' L(G-P),
    W = V0*V0 = ev_D' L(2G).

Vminus is obtained by shortening at P before puncturing. Normal generation
gives the equality for W. The quotient identity in
[Couvreur-Marquez-Corbella-Pellikaan, Proposition7](https://arxiv.org/html/1401.6025v3#S2.SS2)
applies with their E=2G and F=G-P: deg F=d-1>=2g, while
deg E=2d<=n-3 precisely when m>=2d+4. It gives

    Vplus = (Vminus * W^perp)^perp = ev_D' L(G+P).

This uses duality and a product theorem, not the invalid argument that
vanishing of a degree-3G section on only D' forces global vanishing.
It also avoids trying to reconstruct cubics of the original embedding.

Riemann-Roch and injectivity give dimensions k-1,k,k+1 for Vminus,V0,Vplus.
Keep the original punctured B rows as the first k basis vectors of Vplus
and append one new row. In the G+P embedding all original G-sections vanish
at P; the added section has a nonzero leading pole coefficient. Therefore
the recovered projective P is [0:...:0:1]. This inserts P back into the
geometric presentation; the original search matrix B and its syndrome are
not replaced by the lifted matrix.

At n=m-1>2(d+1), the quadratic relations on D' are exactly those on X.
Since d+1>=2g+2, they generate the whole homogeneous ideal. Computing their
basis is another finite-field kernel operation. The point-lift helper
records its dimension but does not emit all ideal generators.

If B has unknown nonzero coordinate multipliers u, the three known spaces
have weights u,u,u^2 and the quotient has weight u. The argument is still
projectively valid. For actual native solving all multipliers, row-basis
changes and local trivializations must be tracked back to B; geometry
reconstruction alone is not a full-support solver.

## Resource And Next-Step Ledger

Schur spans, duals, shortening and quotient are polynomial field-operation
computations in the input matrix size. Fixed-degree pieces through degree7
of the recovered coordinate algebra can in principle be built by linear
algebra modulo the quadratic ideal; the naive polynomial is large.
The original line-bundle class is the recovered hyperplane class minus P.

This removes the need to assume separately supplied curve equations for
this promised input range. It does not automatically discharge representation
conversion, canonical divisors, rational-place arithmetic, zeta computation,
uniform Jacobian sampling, pairings, native evaluation normalization, or
the2^(r_2/2) effective-coset search. In particular do not promote the previous
conditional auxiliary attack to an implemented polynomial matrix-only solver.
