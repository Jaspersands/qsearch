## Next GPT Scientific Question

For fixed q above the cutoff, the remaining norm stage is exact avoidance
of simultaneous zeros of m affine TWO-component linear maps on the actual
kernel of M. Each map has rank two on that kernel in the certified branch,
but this does not imply efficient joint avoidance once m/q^2 is large.
A constructive algorithm for these AG-derived affine systems would matter;
another numerical rank plot, generic source variant or repeated small
witness would not. First inspect known finite-field affine-avoidance
algorithms and retain the actual original B,s reduction and all costs.

The separate inverse-trace repair review proves only a first-move
obstruction for monotone repair over a predeclared polynomial block
catalogue from its constructed one-defect starts. It does not rule out
adaptive new supports, biased proposals, uphill moves or ordinary
random-start witness search. Do not use it as a classical-hardness claim.
