# Growing-Class Rees: A Source-Consumption Obstruction

2026-10-27. A scoped mathematical deduction about the CURRENT destructive
source-tree architecture. Not a lower bound for HSP, a new oracle candidate,
a discovery admission, or a claim of novelty. One completed independent AI
adversarial review accepts the scoped argument, not the full Rees theorem.

## Scientific Decision

Question: would an efficient solver for the actual random diagonal systems
alone make this Rees receiver polynomial for arbitrary growing class?

No, within the prescribed full-grade, fresh-source descent. A family of
actual class c forces at least c! lifted leaves on the generic-label event,
even granting a free perfect diagonal solver. Classical pooling, adaptive
grouping, recycling unused measured targets, and restarts do not remove
this necessary consumption under a polynomial label budget at large p.

This is useful for choosing research, not a reason to build a new workbench.
Do not invest in an average-case SDE solver as though it were the sole
blocker. A growing-class proposal must ALSO change destructive composition,
skip grades, exploit a different reduction, or prove a favorable source
distribution outside the example. The existing fixed-class proposal is
unaffected. No useful hard application is established for it.

## Exact Architecture Contract

The input model is the existing explicit prime-field Lazard model with
ordinary hiding-function access, or fresh ideal mixed coset copies. Charge
public arithmetic, native-copy acquisition and all measurements. This note
grants arbitrary computation on the measured labels, including a perfect
SDE solver, to isolate the quantum consumption issue.

Only the following architecture is covered:

1. The positive lower-central Rees cover is used, including its c-copy
   native lift. Source acquisition may instead cost c ordinary queries.
2. Grades c,c-1,...,2 are removed one at a time, using the established
   central-character instrument. Each incoming exact coset state is
   measured on the entire current top grade. Its label is counted once.
3. A successful merge uses nonzero scales satisfying the exact diagonal
   equation. Its active quantum targets are consumed and not shared with
   a different output. A returned state is exactly the next coset state,
   independent of its measured records and of disjoint unused targets.
4. Labels can be selected adaptively and zero-scale targets kept for later
   attempts. At most N distinct-source labels are inspected at EACH stage
   over the entire run, including restarts. A total
   operation cap N implies this, but a native-copy budget alone does not.
5. Success in this bound means producing a terminal grade-one source by
   this architecture, not guessing or computing the final subgroup by a
   different method. Errorful approximations require a separate complete-
   protocol distance bound; none is silently assumed here.

It does not cover coherent multi-output instruments, quantum target reuse,
cross-grade processing, a different cover, direct subgroup answers, or
freshly acquired lower-stage sources. It is not a quantum query lower bound.

## Family With Full Central-Label Rank

For any c>=2 and odd prime p>c, let L have basis

    x, y_0, ..., y_(c-1),     [x,y_i]=y_(i+1) (i<c-1),

with all other brackets determined by antisymmetry or zero. This is an
abelian ideal acted on by one nilpotent Jordan block, so Jacobi holds.
Its dimension is c+1 and actual class is c. For j>=2,

    gamma_j(L)=span(y_(j-1),...,y_(c-1)),  r_j=c+1-j.

Take h=span(x+sum_i a_i y_i), for any coefficients a_i. The line is a Lie
subalgebra and is nonnormal for c>=2. It meets every gamma_j, j>=2, trivially.
Consequently its Rees lift is exactly t*h, with no higher-grade component.
After any prescribed higher-grade quotients, the hidden group intersects
the current central grade trivially. The established central branch law
therefore gives independent uniform labels in F_p^(r_j) for fresh sources.

This is a structural counterexample family, NOT a newly proposed hard
oracle problem. At fixed c its elementary-abelian-by-cyclic group is already
within known HPGP-based coverage. No hardness is inferred at growing c.

## Adaptive Pool Bound

For k<=r, k IID uniform vectors in F_p^r have dependence probability

    d(p,r,k)=1-product_(i=0..k-1)(1-p^(i-r)).

In particular d(p,r,r)<1/(p-1), by exposing columns successively and a
union bound. If N>=r, every dependent subset of size <=r extends to an
r-subset of the N labels. Hence

    Pr[there exists such a subset] <= min(1, binom(N,r)/(p-1)).

For N<r the event is simply dependence of all N vectors, with probability
d(p,r,N). For N=0 it has probability zero. The implementation uses the
exact probability also at N=r; the simpler displayed bound remains valid.

Adaptivity does not authorize treating SELECTED labels as IID. Instead
couple acquisition at each fixed stage to an upfront IID stream of N
potential labels. The bound is on every small subset of that whole stream,
and therefore contains every choice made from it. Independence of output
from records is essential for coupling later acquisitions; it is supplied
by the exact all-record conversion proof, not assumed from correct
marginals alone. No independence BETWEEN different stages is needed for
the final union bound.

For any exact nonzero scale witness, the active coefficients s_i^j are
nonzero field elements and form a linear dependence among the active
labels. Thus, unless the exceptional event occurs, every merge at stage j
consumes at least r_j+1 targets. Linear dependence is only necessary:
power-residue constraints can make the true cost larger.

## Factorial Consumption And Asymptotic Consequence

Follow the ancestry of ONE terminal source. Active targets are disjoint
across outputs by the architecture contract, so on the complement of the
union of exceptional events its tree contains at least

    product_(j=2..c)(r_j+1)=c!

top-stage lifted sources. Each costs c originals under the specified lift.
If native budget B<c*c!, the probability of producing a terminal source
is at most the sum of the stage exceptional bounds, capped at one.

For supplied primes 2^(c^2)<p<2^(c^2+1), log p=Theta(c^2) and the explicit
structure-constant input has polynomial length in c. Such a prime exists
by Bertrand's postulate; this is an input family, not a claimed generator.
For every fixed polynomial label budget N=poly(c,log p), the exceptional
bound is at most

    (c-1)*max(1,N^(c-1))/(p-1) = 2^(-c^2+O(c log c)).

Meanwhile c*c! is superpolynomial in c. Thus a free SDE solver does not
give polynomial growing-class performance for this architecture. This
does not exclude c growing slowly enough for c! to stay polynomial in
the actual input length; it does not contradict the derived-rank upper
bound. This is not an unconditional factorial EXPECTED-cost lower bound.
Nevertheless, an expected-polynomial total-operation implementation with
success >=2/3 would truncate at six times its expected budget with success
>=1/2 by Markov's inequality, contradicting the polynomial-cap result.

Fixed public subdivisions into blocks b_i need their OWN fresh-source
reset at every block and a union bound over the block-specific exceptional
events. Under those additional premises, product_i(b_i+1)>=1+sum_i b_i
preserves the c! ancestry bound. There are at most c(c-1)/2 blocks, so the
same negligible polynomial-budget scale follows. This is not inferred by
projecting a previously selected full-grade pool. Source-dependent quotient
selection is a different interface and is not covered.

A complete approximate protocol within trace distance epsilon of the
budget-capped ideal protocol (including records and aborts) inherits the
terminal-event upper bound beta+epsilon. This elementary contractivity
statement does not certify such a distance from local marginal checks.
