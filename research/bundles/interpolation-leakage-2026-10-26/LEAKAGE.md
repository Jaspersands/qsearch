## 1. Same Native Problem, Not A New Oracle

Use the preceding native-nonbass-realization bundle. Let phi:E0->E be an
efficiently evaluable isogeny of odd prime degree q, with q != p and p>3.
The full source order O0=End(E0) and bounded-degree evaluators for its basis
are supplied. Pay for the dual, represented models, fields, and evaluator
compression as in that bundle. Put

    O = End(E),  R = Z + phi O0 phi^dagger,  [O:R] = q^3.
    theta(a) = phi a phi^dagger/q.

In SOURCE rational coordinates R0=theta^-1(R)=Z+q O0. The actual order
O1=theta^-1(O) is a q-neighbor of O0, not the abstract center O0.
It agrees with O0 away from q. All maps below are actual morphisms, unless
explicitly identified as proof-only Tate matrices or supplied controls.

First remove cheap cases: if E and E0 are geometrically isomorphic, a
computed isomorphism transports the full source order. Adjoin all cheap
target units. Section 5 also eliminates special-j targets at q>3. None of
these exclusions proves hardness of the residual native input.

## 2. What Algorithm 6 Actually Returns

Choose the charged smooth auxiliary M>q, gcd(M,pq)=1, with
M-q=a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2. The integer four-square matrix

    A4 = [[a,-b,-c,-d], [b,a,d,-c], [c,-d,a,b], [d,c,-b,a]]

satisfies A4 A4^T=(M-q)I. On X=E0^4 x E^4 the proof map is

    Psi = [[A4, phi^dagger I4], [-phi I4, A4^T]],
    Psi^dagger Psi = Psi Psi^dagger = M I.

The algorithm computes v:X->Y from the graph kernel, a polarized
isomorphism delta:Y->X, and searches the finite product automorphisms.
For some a in Aut(X), Psi=a delta v. This is not a promise that an arbitrary
partial CRT quotient is already a product, nor that all of its symmetries
are induced by terminal product symmetries.

## 3. Terminal Closure Proposition

Localize at q. Write A=O0 tensor Z_q. Let S be the subring of O tensor Z_q
generated by Z_q, phi A phi^dagger, and ALL already-computable target units.
The known block category on E0 and E is

    B = [[A,           A phi^dagger S],
         [S phi A,    S             ]].

This is a ring closed under adjoint. Indeed phi^dagger S phi lies in A
because these are actual source endomorphisms and the full source order
is known. The other off-diagonal product lands in S because S contains
phi A phi^dagger. Target unit inverses are their adjoints, so S is
adjoint-closed. Division by an integer coprime to q preserves B as a
Z_q-lattice. Division by q is expressly NOT covered.

For nonisomorphic elliptic factors a polarized product automorphism is
monomial within each isomorphism type: each column has sum of entry
degrees equal to one, hence exactly one degree-one map. Consequently
Aut(X) is contained in the amplified B. This statement includes source
and target automorphisms, not merely coordinate permutations.

Both Psi and Psi^-1=Psi^dagger/M lie in amplified B. Since
delta v=a^-1 Psi, all terminal composed maps lie there too. An automorphism
of Y conjugated through delta is a product automorphism of X; terminal
polarized factor projectors become ordinary product projectors. Sums,
compositions, adjoints and prime-to-q scalar cancellations of these
terminal maps therefore have target corners in S.

For generic E with Aut(E)={+1,-1}, S=R tensor Z_q. In compatible local
coordinates, used ONLY for the proof and controls,

    A=M2(Z_q), Phi=diag(q,1), Phi^dagger=diag(1,q),
    S=<I, q E11, q^2 E12, E21> over Z_q.

Its index in M2(Z_q) is q^3; B has index q^7 in M4(Z_q). Exact controls
check the basis multiplication, adjoints, terminal ambiguities, and
prime-to-q inverses. A local symplectic source/target swap is NOT a native
product automorphism unless the curves are isomorphic.

Scope is deliberately narrow. This does not simulate the geometric
coordinates given to the classical competitor. It does not exclude
native q-divisibility tests, geometric algorithms applied to those
coordinates, intermediate quotient symmetries, or nonzero additive-source
queries. No classical hardness statement follows.

## 4. Cross-Map Completion Theorem

Suppose a genuine efficiently evaluable eta:E0->E is found with

    eta not in phi A  (locally at q).

Then the Z-span of identity and the sixteen actual maps

    u a_i v^dagger,  u,v in {phi,eta},  i=1,...,4

is the FULL target order O. Here a_i is the supplied source basis.

Proof at q: Phi modulo q has image a line L. The condition says eta has
an image vector outside L. T=[Phi eta] therefore has row rank two modulo
q. Its block adjoint T^dagger has column rank two. As X ranges over
M4(F_q), T X T^dagger ranges over all of M2(F_q). The sixteen maps span
that image. Their integral lattice is full at q by Nakayama (equivalently,
an invertible 4x4 minor). Away from q, phi O0 phi^dagger is already full
because phi is a local isomorphism. Equality at every prime proves equality
of the global rank-four lattices. No uncharged saturation remains.

Detection needs neither a target-order oracle nor large-q torsion:

    z = phi^dagger eta in O0,
    eta in phi A iff z in q A.

Recover z's four exact source coordinates by the existing small-prime
trace/CRT protocol. Any coordinate nonzero modulo q is an escape. The
sixteen maps have degrees at most max(q,deg eta)^2 B0, where B0 bounds
source basis degrees. Trace Gram reconstruction and HNF have polynomial
bit cost in this paid degree/evaluator data, as in the existing product-
corner attack. This is a specialization of that rectangular-rank argument,
not a novelty claim.

Coprime degree of eta is sufficient, not necessary: the local pair
Phi=diag(q,1), eta=diag(1,q) both have determinant q and already complete
the target. Conversely a terminal candidate phi u with u in A cannot
trigger this condition.

## 5. One New Endomorphism Leaves At Most Two Orders

Suppose a genuine efficiently evaluable x in O but outside R is supplied.
Then y=theta^-1(x) lies in O1 but not in R0. In a source split at q^2,
there are two cases:

1. y is integral in the center A. Its reduction modulo q is nonscalar,
   otherwise y belongs to Z_q+q A=R0_q. A preserved neighbor is specified
   by a projective line fixed by this nonscalar 2x2 matrix. There are at
   most two such lines. Its characteristic quadratic finds them in
   randomized polynomial bit time without enumerating q+1 neighbors.
2. y is nonintegral in A. Then qy is integral; the nonzero residue of qy
   is rank-one nilpotent. Its image is the UNIQUE possible neighbor line.
   The leading pole alone is not enough: check preservation of that full
   neighbor lattice. The code explicitly rejects counterexamples that
   have the correct nilpotent pole but do not preserve the lattice.

Reconstruct the at most two global neighbor orders with the existing
annihilator/right-order routine. They already match O0 away from q. Use
native isogeny division to certify their bases, NOT just discriminants or
integral quaternion norm. For a proposed a in q^-1 O0,

    theta(a) = phi (q a) phi^dagger / q^2.

At most eight basis divisions certify the two candidates; the preceding
wrong-maximal-order control explains why they cannot be omitted. Charge
the published division interface, coefficient heights, evaluator lengths
and fields. A basis entirely integral in O must be O by maximality.

The split at q^2 suffices: replacing y by y+q A changes membership in none
of these neighbor orders. Native detection of x outside R is also cheap
given its actual evaluator. In a source basis (1,a2,a3,a4), compute
z=phi^dagger x phi in O0. Then

    x in R iff z_1 is divisible by q AND z_2,z_3,z_4 by q^2.

Membership is global here because R and O differ only at q. The input
must be a verified native x in O; arbitrary rational matrices are not
accepted as geometric witnesses.

Cheap target units sharpen the baseline. If q>3 and j(E)=0 or 1728, a
standard non-scalar unit has reduced discriminant -3 or -4, respectively.
It cannot lie in R: an element of Z_q+q A has discriminant divisible by
q^2. Thus these targets already give the at-most-two classical selection
route, with the bounded unit-definition extension charged. Tiny q can be
enumerated directly. This is NOT an arbitrary-curve OneEnd oracle claim:
the required map is outside this SPECIFIC full-rank R, not just nonscalar.

## 6. The Remaining Native Experiment

For a partial CRT quotient v_D:X->Y_D with polarized multiplier D|M and
a genuinely computed b in Aut(Y_D), form the native map

    L = v_D^dagger b v_D,  L^dagger L = D^2 I.

Every elliptic corner has degree at most D^2. A target corner outside R
invokes Section 5; a source-to-target corner outside phi A invokes
Section 4. Both are tested through known-source trace coordinates. No
unknown target basis is used by either gate.

Do not apply the terminal closure proof to Y_D. The remaining quotient
kernel need not be stable under b, and Y_D need not be a product. Likewise
do not invent arbitrary local symplectic b and count it as a geometric
automorphism. Very small D only produces bounded-degree maps available
to a standard low-degree classical search. Large prefixes need separate
cost and useful-automorphism probability analysis.

Decisive outcomes are an ACTUAL escape map, a rigorous obstruction for
the actual partial quotients, or a proved useful-symmetry distribution.
None has been obtained. No timing, success rate, native dequantization,
or hardness is inferred from the supplied local controls. The next pass
must attack this geometric question, not add larger opaque-label samples.

## 7. Implementation And Falsifiers

interpolation_closure.py implements exact local closure, cross-map rank,
source-coordinate gates and the at-most-two selector. Sampling exhaustion
propagates as UNKNOWN, not a failed promise or negative result. Tests use
three supplied quaternion neighbor fixtures and primes up to 2^61-1 for
the quadratic selector. Large primes test arithmetic scaling only.

Explicit falsifiers include division by q breaking terminal closure,
nongeometric source/target swaps, source-coordinate gate denominators,
old scalar elements yielding NO_NEW_LOCAL_INFORMATION, wrong fractional
pole lower terms, and two independent degree-q cross maps completing the
ring. Full q+1 enumeration appears only in tiny exhaustive controls, not
the selector or proposed native algorithm.
