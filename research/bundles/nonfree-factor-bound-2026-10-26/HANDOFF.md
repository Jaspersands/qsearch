# Handoff: Close The Nonfree-Factor And Binary-Automorphism Loopholes

The complete argument is in NONFREE_FACTOR_BOUND.md. It is a new,
unreviewed deduction, not a native experiment or a new quantum algorithm.
Keep this bundle isolated until the main repository's migration is verified.

## What Changes In The Research Decision

The previous polarization-completion note's small-a certificate covered
only free factors isomorphic to E. Now ALL elliptic factors are covered.
For H=[[a,beta],[beta^dagger,d]], beta in Q(alpha), determinant one:

- A primitive factor outside the known quadratic algebra implies an
  endomorphism w not commuting with alpha, deg(w)<=floor(min(a,d)^2/4).
- Every automorphism outside that algebra, including on powers of H,
  implies a noncommuting endomorphism of degree<=min(a,d)^2.
- Both bounds apply after any supplied legal K-basis change with integral
  inverse. Large unreduced coefficients are not a good search objective.

Do NOT spend Gemini implementation effort extending the old a<=8 or a<=16
form box to nonfree factors. A complete native low-degree search is already
the matched comparator. Do not use a general generic-moduli automorphism
theorem as a probability bound for this project's special query families.

   from this bound. Polynomial-in-value enumeration does not settle large

## Next Decision

A useful continuation needs an explicit native family whose reduced
diagonals grow beyond the low-degree competitor, with a mathematical reason
for informative factors/automorphisms and an honest comparison to the
existing one-endomorphism/class-group algorithms. No such family has been
provided. Until then deprioritize this completion route; adding more small
Hermitian examples is not research progress.

The separate rank-four additive source still has possible theorem-level
value, but cannot be advertised as solving the one-endomorphism input.
Its independent review and full prior-art comparison remain separate. No
reviewer authorization was reused, and no new reviewer was spawned.
