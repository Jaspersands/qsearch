## Representation

Let R be the section ring of the recovered line bundle H, with
ell=deg(H)>=2g+2 and N=dim R_1=ell+1-g. The ideal is generated by I_2.
Choose independent linear forms u,v with no common zero on the curve.
Write S=F_q[u,v]. Then

```text
R = S*1 direct_sum S(-1)^(N-2) direct_sum S(-2)^g.       (A)
```

This is standard graded Noether-normalization structure in this setting,
not a new algorithmic discovery. Here is the argument needed for this use.
The complete normally generated section ring of a smooth curve is normal;
its dimension is2, so Serre's S2 condition makes it Cohen--Macaulay. A
basepoint-free pencil u,v is a homogeneous system of parameters, hence a
regular sequence. Riemann--Roch gives

```text
Hilb_R(t) = (1+(N-2)t+g t^2)/(1-t)^2.
```

The quotient A=R/(u,v) has dimensions1,N-2,g,0,0,... . Lift bases of A_0,
A_1,A_2 to1,beta_i,gamma_a in R. Graded induction gives a surjection from
the free module in(A) onto R. Equality of Hilbert series proves injectivity.
This also explains why just an arbitrary finite module, without the
regular-sequence hypotheses, would not justify a free representation.

For the actual ell84,g41,N44 case the free rank is84, with degree shifts
one0,42ones,41twos. Its degree7 piece has dimension548. This is a module
over a TWO-VARIABLE polynomial ring, not a claim that R itself has vector
space dimension84 or that the curve admits a degree-one map to a line.

### Choosing And Certifying The Pencil

For any nonzero u, its zero divisor has degree ell. A random v over a field
of size Q vanishes at one of its geometric support points with probability
at most ell/Q: each point imposes a nonzero linear condition, possibly more
than one condition over that field. Thus Q>=2ell gives success probability
at least1/2. The actual q100003 fixture meets this without an extension.
For growing genus at fixed q, use an explicitly represented extension of
degree ceil(log_q(2ell)) if necessary; do not assume the base field stays
large enough. Descent of bases and multiplication maps then costs additional
linear/semilinear algebra and must preserve the native field and frames.

A finite certificate is A_3=0, along with the expected dimensions of A_1
and A_2. As A is generated in degree1, A_3=0 implies all higher pieces are
zero and the pencil has no projective base point. Reject/retry bad pencils.
Do not test absence of base points only on the supplied rational columns.

## Build Multiplication From Degrees2 And3 Only

1. Export the true I_2 kernel from the lifted matrix, with its monomial order.
   Choose u,v and extend them to the degree-one basis u,v,beta_1,...,beta_b,
   b=N-2. Keep the invertible basis change and original section inclusion.
2. Compute the R_2 normal form in the basis
   u^2,uv,v^2,u beta_i,v beta_i,gamma_a. It has3+2b+g elements. Require
   this basis to have the known dimension; gamma_a must be stored as
   actual quadratic polynomial representatives, not abstract labels.
3. The only missing generator actions are X_(i,a)=beta_i gamma_a. All
   products beta_i beta_j are already known from R_2 normal forms.
4. Expand the cubic associativity identities

```text
beta_i (beta_j beta_k) - beta_j (beta_i beta_k) = 0.
```

   Every term containing u or v reduces using the known quadratic table.
   The remaining unknowns are the b*g elements X_(i,a). Their coefficients
   are constants in F_q. Their right sides are known elements of the free
   degree-three space, with basis size4+3b+2g.
5. The coefficient map is

```text
Lambda^2(A_1) tensor A_1 -> A_1 tensor A_2,
(beta_i wedge beta_j) tensor beta_k
  |-> beta_i tensor (beta_j beta_k) - beta_j tensor (beta_i beta_k).
```

   Its cokernel is A_3. To see this directly, start with tensor words of
   length3, quotient the last two slots by commutativity and I_2, then impose
   swapping the first two slots. These are exactly all degree-three
   commutativity and quadratic-ideal relations. Thus A_3=0 means the
   constant coefficient matrix has full column rank b*g. Solve it with
   all4+3b+2g right sides, and CHECK every omitted/redundant equation.
6. Record the actions of every beta_i on the free basis1,beta_j,gamma_a
   as ell-by-ell matrices over F_q[u,v]. The u,v actions are scalar.
   All entries have degree<=3, with the exact shifts enforced.

The cubic system is an implementation proposal derived here. Its relation
map and consistency must be checked independently before relying on a
native genus41 artifact. This document does not assert that somebody has
already run that construction on B.

## Strong Certificate, Not Just Dimension Matching

The resulting module operations must pass ALL these exact tests:

- Each matrix entry has degree1+shift(column)-shift(row), or is zero.
- Every pair of generator-action matrices commutes.
- Every exported quadratic relation vanishes as a matrix polynomial.
- Applying each gamma's stored quadratic representative to1 returns its
  designated free basis vector; likewise for every beta.
- The cubic coefficient map is surjective, all right sides consistent,
  and R_2 agrees with the initial exact normal forms.

These conditions certify a cyclic S[x]-module realizing the same quadratic
presentation: A_3=0 gives spanning over S by the lifted basis; the commuting
actions and quadratic relations define a map back to a FREE module sending
that spanning set to its basis. The two maps compose to the identity on
the free basis, so no extra hidden module relation was introduced. This
provides an algebra certificate in addition to the geometric input promise.
It is not a recognition algorithm for smooth complete AG codes.

For fixed truncation h, obtain R_j from the free shifted monomials and
multiply using these matrices. There is no higher-degree monomial explosion.
Return actual structure constants and reduction maps, not just dimensions.

Coarse setup budget: O(N^3) cubic equations, O(Ng) unknowns and O(N+g)
right-side coordinates; naive elimination is O(N^5 g^2) field operations,
polynomial but not a performance promise. Stream equations or store sparse
rows. For N44,g41: b*g=1722 unknowns and212 right-side coordinates, rather
than a99-million-column degree7 matrix. Arithmetic and memory counts should
be measured in the implementation, not extrapolated from this control.
