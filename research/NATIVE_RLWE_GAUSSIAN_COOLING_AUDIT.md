# Native Homogeneous Gaussians: Warm-Start Cooling Still Needs New Moves

Date: 2026-10-04. LOCAL DERIVATION / REVIEW PENDING.

Follow-up to NATIVE_RLWE_HOMOGENEOUS_DGS_REDUCTION. No novelty, independent
verification, general quantum-sampling lower bound or algorithm result.
This tests native-basis Gibbs-parent evolution, including state-specific
warm starts, rather than inferring runtime solely from a small global gap.
Routine implementation belongs to Gemini.

## 1. Decision

Centered sampling removes coset representatives and phase consistency.
It does not repair long native moves. On natural labels the native basis
of EVERY coordinate-neutral K_j has long columns with high probability.
Their Metropolis Gibbs parents barely couple the whole Gaussian-relevant
ball. Polynomial-action evolution cannot materially change its position
distribution, even from a warm Gaussian start and with diagonal controls.

This is NOT a bound for arbitrary quantum Hamiltonians, every discrete-time
walk, nonlocal moves, a reduced basis, or a supplied target-state reflection.
Direct postselected cooling is separately audited: its acceptance cost
cannot be ignored, including at constant output TV error.

## 2. Native Coordinate-Neutral Bases Really Are Long

Write F=[F_0,F_1], F_l=C_(a_l)^T. Condition only a_1 to be a unit;
a_2 remains uniform. Let V=F_0^(-1), v=V*e_j and choose ANY h with v_h!=0
using a_1 alone. Let p be all first-block coordinates except h and
T=V*F_1. With coordinate order p,h,second-block, a natural basis of K_j is

    B_j=[q*I_(d-1) -M_0 -M_1],
        [     0        I_(d+1)],
    M_0=-v_p/v_h mod q,
    M_1=T_p-(v_p/v_h)*T_h mod q.                           (1)

Use centered integer representatives in M. This follows by writing
x=-T*y+t*v, then eliminating t using x_h. The basis determinant is q^(d-1).
Adaptive h avoids an unnecessary nonzero-coordinate rejection.

Set r=d-1 and

    V_r=pi^(r/2)/Gamma(r/2+1)*(1+sqrt(r)/(2*q))^r.          (2)

A uniform centered vector of Z_q^r has probability <=V_r of norm <q:
cover its integer points by disjoint unit cubes inside a ball of radius
q+sqrt(r)/2. Values above one are vacuous bounds.

For each fixed h, v is a uniform UNIT ring vector (a conjugate/monomial
image of a_1^(-1)). Before unit conditioning, v_p/v_h given v_h!=0 is
uniform in Z_q^r. Union over the possible adaptive h therefore bounds
the first free-column exception by d*(1-1/q)*V_r/p_U.
Conditional on a_1,h, every individual column of M_1 is uniform in Z_q^r:
a_2 -> F_1*e_l is an invertible signed coefficient permutation, followed
by a public full-row-rank map. The columns are NOT independent; a union
bound gives d*V_r for their exceptions. Here p_U=(1-q^(-d/2))^2.

Thus conditional on first-label unit,

    Pr[some column of B_j has norm <q]<=2*d*V_r/p_U,
    Pr[any of the d bases fails]<=2*d^2*V_r/p_U.            (3)

The q*e_i columns already have norm q. Log10 upper references in (3)'s
all-coordinate bound at the d^4 rows are -15.092283,-146.028012,-904.572710,
with the exact positive p_U divisor retained. Unit selection is separately
charged. This does not model dependent ring entries as an iid matrix.

## 3. The Whole Low-Energy Ball Has Tiny Quantum Coupling

Fix a certified long-column basis and let B={c in K_j:||c||<=q/4}.
For every native signed increment w and c in B,

    ||c+w||^2-||c||^2>=||w||^2-(q/2)*||w||>=q^2/2.         (4)

There are NO native transitions between two distinct points of B.
Let pi_s(c) proportional to exp(-pi*||c||^2/s^2), where s is a PROBABILITY
width. Take any symmetric native-move proposal Q_s(x,y)=Q_s(y,x), with
row sums <=1, and its Metropolis chain P_s. With D_s=diag(sqrt(pi_s)),

    H_s=I-D_s*P_s*D_s^(-1), ||H_s||<=2,
    (H_s)_(x,y)=-Q_s(x,y)*exp[-pi*abs(||y||^2-||x||^2)/(2*s^2)]
                                                     for x!=y.

Equation (4) bounds every B-to-outside amplitude. Row and column sums of
the cross block are at most exp(-pi*q^2/(4*s^2)), since Q_s is symmetric
and substochastic. The Schur bound gives

    ||Pi_B*H_s*(I-Pi_B)||<=exp(-pi*q^2/(4*s^2)).            (5)

The B block is diagonal. Rejected cutoff-boundary moves do not invalidate
(4)-(5); finite-source normalizers/tails still need their own ledger.

## 4. A State-Specific Bound, Not Just A Gap Complaint

Consider time-dependent evolution generated by

    H(t)=sum_l lambda_l(t)*H_(s_l(t))+D(t), s_l(t)<=s_max,

with arbitrary bounded diagonal controls D(t). Define action
A=integral sum_l |lambda_l(t)| dt. Allow noncommuting schedules and signed
coefficients. Remove only B/outside couplings to form block-diagonal H_0(t).
The propagator identity/Duhamel estimate gives

    ||U-U_0||<=Lambda=A*exp(-pi*q^2/(4*s_max^2)).           (6)

Inside B, H_0 is diagonal, so every position probability stays unchanged.
For any initial pure/mixed state with outside probability epsilon_0,

    TV[position law after U, initial position law]<=epsilon_0+Lambda. (7)

The reference outside block can change at most its total epsilon_0 mass;
unitary perturbation adds Lambda. This explicitly covers warm starts and
does not assert that a small spectator eigenvalue necessarily slows every
state. Increasing Hamiltonian coefficients increases A; rescaling time
does not erase the action cost. This is not an all-query-model lower bound.

### Warm Gaussian Cooling At The Existing References

Take s_0=s_max=q/(16*sqrt(d)) and target s_f=sigma/sqrt(2), with sigma
from the d^4 L2 table. A centered lattice Gaussian, on ANY full-rank K,
has rho_(sqrt(2)*s)(K)/rho_s(K)<=2^d by Poisson summation. Hence

    Pr_(pi_s0)[outside B]<=2^d*exp(-pi*q^2/(32*s_0^2))
                          =exp[-(8*pi-log(2))*d],
    exp(-pi*q^2/(4*s_max^2))=exp(-64*pi*d).                (8)

The corresponding log10 bounds are -679.294773,-2717.179094,-10868.716375
for outside mass, and -5588.485545,-22353.942181,-89415.768725 for coupling.

The initial and target laws are NOT already close. Their exact classical
Bhattacharyya coefficient is

    BC=Z(s_eff)/sqrt(Z(s_0)*Z(s_f)),
    s_eff^2=2/(s_0^(-2)+s_f^(-2)), Z(s)=rho_s(K_j).         (9)

When the full-syndrome relative-flatness certificates hold at all THREE
widths, Z(s) lies in (1+-eta)*Theta(s)^(2*d)/q^(d-1). If
R=s_0/s_f and delta_theta=2*exp(-pi*s_f^2)/(1-exp(-3*pi*s_f^2)), then

    BC<=(1+eta)/(1-eta)*(1+delta_theta)^(2*d)
          *[2*R/(1+R^2)]^d,
    TV(pi_s0,pi_sf)>=1-BC.                                (10)

Retain the tiny positive theta term, not an underflowed exact zero. These
are three flatness events; union their own B(width)/(eta*p_U) bounds,
rather than assuming the entire continuum from one endpoint certificate.
Their widths exceed the terminal width and satisfy the earlier mass-bound
premises. At eta=.01, log10 BC upper references, apart from the explicitly
negligible theta factor, are -67.476338,-500.908621,-2928.411324.

To reach target TV error epsilon, (7)-(10) require

    Lambda>=1-BC-epsilon_0-epsilon.                        (11)

Thus polynomial-action cold Gibbs-parent evolution cannot do this cooling,
even if the broad centered Gaussian is GIVEN as a warm start. An initial
position-law approximation delta adds at most 2*delta conservatively to
the right-side loss. Source/implementation approximations must be charged.
Hotter excursions, basis changes and non-parent hopping are outside scope.

## 5. Filtering Is A Different Route, But Not A Free One

The exact diagonal cooling filter has acceptance

    p=Z(s_f)/Z(s_0) approximately R^(-2*d).                (12)

Zero attains the maximum target/source likelihood ratio, so this p is
optimal for exact one-copy position-preserving rejection. Generic quantum
amplification still pays the small-success burden; log2 normalizer proxies
for its square-root overhead are 288.000237,1920.000005,10752.000000.

Constant approximation error does not provide polynomial acceptance.
Take target ball A_f={||c||<=s_f*sqrt(d)}. Its centered-Gaussian tail is
delta_f<=exp[-(pi/2-log(2))*d]. A Chernoff tilt gives

    pi_s0(A_f)<=exp[pi*d*(1-R^(-2))]*Z(s_f)/Z(s_0).         (13)

For ANY successful position-preserving filter with output TV error epsilon,
mass domination p*pi_output(A_f)<=pi_s0(A_f) implies

    p<=exp[pi*d*(1-R^(-2))]*Z(s_f)/Z(s_0)
                                      /(1-epsilon-delta_f). (14)

At epsilon=.01 and eta=.01, log10 upper references are -86.230829,
-806.672448,-5076.215256, with theta corrections retained symbolically.
Polynomially many copies with an output selected from one of their positions
can increase the small-ball bound by at most that polynomial copy count.
Operations that construct new output positions are not position-preserving.

Many close temperature steps do not supply reflections/projectors for free.
If a claimed Zeno route assumes inexpensive exact Gaussian reflections,
it assumes an unresolved preparation operation. Recursive preparation/
amplification costs must be exposed. A short state path alone is not a
compiled algorithm. This is consistent with the explicit gap/time resources
in [Somma--Boixo--Barnum--Knill](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.1571) and
[Boixo--Knill--Somma](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.1652); (6)-(14) are local
source-specific arguments, not general impossibility results from those papers.

## 6. Controls And Attempts To Break The Argument

- Seed 290947: 32 EXACT natural coordinate-neutral bases across
  d=8,16,32,64,128. Checked F constraints, inverses and determinant form.
  Long-column premises failed in all eight d=8 cases and seven of eight
  d=16 cases; they passed all d>=32 controls. Minimum free-column norm/q
  was 1.9357854 at d=64 and 2.9432414 at d=128. No failed premise is
  assigned the cooling obstruction. These are basis checks, not growing
  Gaussian quantum simulations.
- Seed 290948: finite Gibbs parents and time-dependent dynamics on 841
  and 1,681 states, with long UNIMODULAR bases of Z^2. Checked Hermiticity,
  Gaussian ground vectors, diagonal low-ball blocks, cross operator norm,
  arbitrary initial phases, diagonal controls and Duhamel bounds. Six
  three-stage protocols per lattice obeyed (6)-(7); max state discrepancy
  from the block reference was 3.32e-12. Sixteen parent/dynamics checks.
- Crucial scope counterexample: those Z^2 lattices have trivial scalar
  Gaussian samplers and short standard bases. Their bad-basis parent is
  frozen, NOT their underlying sampling problem. This falsifies any attempt
  to turn the theorem into general DGS hardness.
- Seed 290949: 36 exact finite Gaussian overlap, cooling-filter and
  small-ball controls. Max BC identity error 4.45e-16. Recomputed all
  three natural label, coupling, tail, BC and filtering reference rows.

## 7. Next Target And Gemini Contract

centered starting point, a formal parent Hamiltonian or an uncharged filter.
Record actual generators, their exact squared norms, pivot/unit contracts,
temperature ceiling, normalized action, inside-ball mass and coupling bound.
Treat short-column, hotter, nonlocal and changed-basis cases as OUTSIDE this
certificate, not automatically dequantized or automatically fast.

Gemini should implement the scoped certificate and identity-lattice
countercontrols, leaving polynomial DGS unresolved. Main-model work should
now specify a new relation-generating operation or short basis with total
cost, and show useful label diversity as well as length. Strong bare
translation hopping, global Fourier moves or a different Hamiltonian can
escape (5); their resulting law need not be the required Gaussian and may
concentrate at the uninformative zero vector. That correctness burden is
as important as escaping this bound.
