## Proved components: formal seeds and exact saturation

For monic integral f, put F(z)=z^(2g+1)f(1/z) and
F(t²)^(-1/2)=sum_(m>=0)c_m t^(2m). Then c0=1 and v2(c_m)>=-2m.
For omega_k=x^(g-1-k)dx/(2y), the anchored logarithm is
L_k(t)=-sum_(m>=0)c_m t^(2(k+m)+1)/(2(k+m)+1).
Cauchy--Binet expresses the determinant at t_i=2^N i as the leading
Vandermonde times a convergent Schur series. A column set of excess Delta
has coefficient valuation>=-2Delta and Schur factor valuation>=2N*Delta.
For N>=2 every nonleading term has at least two extra factors of2. Thus

    D0 = v2 det(L_k(t_i))
       = N*g² + sum_i v2(i) + sum_(i<j) v2(j²-i²).

This is finite and O(Ng²+g²logg), for EVERY g; no finite-example inference.
The infinity chart w=y/x^(g+1)=t*sqrt(F(t²)) is smooth over Z2, so the
points lie in the actual Neron K2. Their closed Z2-span L has free rankg.
The initial H0=L+J(Q2)[2] is a pro-2 open subgroup. Include ALL rational
2-torsion, computed from local factors, not merely those with nonzero labels.

## Polynomial dyadic index via a proof-only volume cover

Let D=v2(disc f), N_c=4D+12 and M=2 ceil(log2 g)+4. The new closed
addendum audits/reviews/2026-11-07-local-saturation-index.md proves

    kappa <= D0 + 2g*N_c + g*M + 3g - t.                 (I)

This is polynomial in the native coefficient bits and growing genus.
No local fields, charts or Neron data used in the COVER are enumerated or
supplied to the algorithm; their finite COUNT bounds a Haar volume.

1. There are at most e*p^(eM) isomorphism types of degree-e extensions of
Qp for e<=g, with M=2ceil(log_p g)+4. Split off the unique unramified
subextension. A totally ramified degree-e' extension has an Eisenstein
polynomial; distinct derivative term valuations modulo e' imply
v_p(F'(pi))<=v_p(e')+1. Hensel show that a matching Eisenstein polynomial modulo p^M has a root in the
original field and generates it; thus the residue class determines its type. This gives at most4^g*p^(gM) ordered field tuples
with sum of degreesg. This is an ANALYSIS count, never an enumeration cost.
2. Every Jacobian point has a rational effective reduced divisor of degree
at mostg. Add copies of infinity and decompose into closed points. Therefore
it lies in a trace image product_i C(E_i)->J(Qp), total field degreeg.
Multiplicity/degree-drop loci introduce no missing points.
3. On the integral affine locus, y is integral and the Sylvester Bezout
identity A*f+B*f'=disc f, A,B integral, implies
min(v_p(2y),v_p(f'(x)))<=D+1. At most p^(2[E:Qp]N_c) residue pairs modulo
p^N_c cover this locus. Choose one exact center per nonempty pair for
ANALYSIS ONLY. Set A0=D+2, scale the free coordinate by p^(N_c-A0), and
the dependent coordinate by p^(N_c-2A0). Dividing the equation by the
selected derivative times the latter scale gives a strict Hensel contraction.
Its graph contains the entire original residue pair. Pullbacks of every
standard differential have integral coefficients after this scaling.
4. The remaining infinity region has w²=uF(u), u=1/x. Its u-derivative
F+uF' is a unit for u in the maximal ideal. A single analytic chart with
w in that ideal covers it, and omega_j=-u^(g-1-j)dw/(F+uF') is integral.
5. In integral O_E bases, each trace-map derivative entry is the trace of
an integral element, hence lies in Z_p. The p-adic analytic area inequality
bounds the image volume of each product chart by1 for the Haar form
omega0 wedge...wedge omega_(g-1). It follows locally from the inverse
function theorem on noncritical balls; critical images have measure0.
Thus mu_omega(J)<=8^g*p^(gM+2gN_c). At2, mu_omega(L)=2^-D0 and adding
J[2] multiplies by2^t (K2 is torsionfree). Comparing Haar volumes proves(I).

This removes the dyadic iteration-count blocker WITHOUT a conductor-
