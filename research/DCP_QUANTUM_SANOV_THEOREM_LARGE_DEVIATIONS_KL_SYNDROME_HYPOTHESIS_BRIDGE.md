# Subsystem 145: Quantum Stein's Lemma & Umegaki Relative Entropy Syndrome Hypothesis Bridge

## 1. Mathematical Formulation
By Quantum Stein's Lemma (Hiai-Petz; Ogawa-Nagaoka), the optimal type-II error $\beta_K(\alpha)$ at fixed type-I false alarm $\alpha \in (0,1)$ between $K$ copies of a CNOT-peeled state $\rho_1$ and a carry-corrupted state $\rho_0$ satisfies:
$$\lim_{K\to\infty} -\frac{1}{K}\ln \beta_K(\alpha) = D(\rho_1 \| \rho_0) = \operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1(\ln \rho_1 - \ln \rho_0)) \ge \frac{1}{2}\|\rho_1 - \rho_0\|_1^2.$$
