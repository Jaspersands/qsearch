## Explicit Task

Let P contain every prime through B except 3, with m=|P|. Set
c=floor(7m/200), choose a power M=3^k with

    M/3 > 2 B^(2c+1),

and N=2*3^(k-1). The public labels a_p=log_2(p) modulo N are computed by
the existing classical ternary lifting algorithm and checked by
exponentiation. No discrete-log oracle or exponential advice is supplied.

For x in Z_N define chi_x(p)=exp(2*pi*i*a_p*x/N), extending to the
Dirichlet character modulo M, zero on multiples of 3. Find x with

    x mod 3 != 0,
    S(x) = sum_(p in P) Re(chi_x(p))/p >= W/4,
    W = sum_(p in P) 1/p.

The first condition means conductor exactly M, not merely nonprincipal.
The principal character x=0 would otherwise solve the optimization
trivially. Small-conductor lifts are also excluded.

The prime prefix, zero centers and weighted objective are part of this
NEW task. It is not the previous random-target CVP instance. Successful
witnesses for either task cannot be silently transferred to the other.

## Primitive Projection

Use the existing signed ternary source with local probabilities
(19/20,1/40,1/40), restricted coherently to at most c positive and c
negative entries. Its syndrome is s(y)=sum a_p*y_p modulo N.
After reversible decoding erases y, Fourier measurement gives x.

For j=1,2 the expectation of exp(2*pi*i*j*x/3) is the source overlap
at syndrome displacement j*N/3. Suppose such an overlap exists. Reducing
the multiplicative ratio modulo M/3 makes its value 1. The height guard
and unique prime factorization force the exponent difference to be zero.
Its original syndrome is then zero modulo N, not j*N/3: contradiction.

The same argument works with an additional displacement +/-a_p. Its
cross-product height is at most B^(2c+1). Modulo M/3, unique factorization
forces the difference to be exactly the ordinary +/-coordinate shift.
Its original syndrome then cannot differ by j*N/3.

Consequently x mod 3 is exactly uniform in the ideal distribution and
every cosine moment has the same mean in each residue class. This also
holds with source-center phases, although this application uses zero.
In particular,

    Pr[primitive] = 2/3,
    E[S/W | primitive] = mu > 601/2000  (m >= 20000).

This is a proof about overlaps, not an assumption that arbitrary
postselection preserves an expectation. The original height license
alone does not establish this stronger statement. The new verifier
refuses it without the smaller-modulus guard.

Primitivity follows because the kernel of reduction from units modulo
3^k to units modulo 3^(k-1) has order 3, generated by 2^(N/3).
chi_x is trivial on that kernel iff 3 divides x. Since the divisors of
3^k form a chain, failure to descend one level is full primitivity.

## From A Verified Score To An L-Value

This is an elementary, deliberately loose implication. It applies at

    sigma = 1 + 1/h, h=bit_length(B),

**not at s=1 and not inside the critical strip**.

First, the complete Euler product over primes <=B contains the harmonic
sum through B. Taking logs and bounding its higher prime powers by

    sum_(n>=2) sum_(r>=2) 1/(r*n^r)
      <= sum_(n>=2) 1/(n*(n-1)) = 1

shows W >= log(log(B+1))-4/3, subtracting at most 1/3 for prime 3.

Here are self-contained bounds for the remaining analytic errors:

1. Every prime between n and 2n divides binomial(2n,n), so dyadic
   summation gives vartheta(t)=sum_(p<=t) log p < 4 log(2)*t.
   Split the prime count at sqrt(t), using log t <= sqrt(t), to obtain
   pi(t) <= (1+8 log 2)*t/log t < 8t/log t for t>=2.
2. Put delta=1/h. The difference between the first-power sums at 1 and
   sigma is at most
   delta*sum_(p<=B) log(p)/p
   <= 4(log 2)^2+4 log 2 < 5.
   This uses 1-exp(-v)<=v and partial summation of vartheta.
3. The omitted prime first powers are bounded using partial summation:
   sum_(p>B) p^(-sigma)
   <= 8*sigma*integral_B^infinity t^(-sigma)/log(t) dt
   = 8*sigma*E1(delta log B).
   Since sigma<=3/2 and delta log B>=log(2)/2>1/3,
   E1(a)<=exp(-a)/a<=3 gives the bound 36.
4. At sigma>1 the entire higher-prime-power error in log|L| is at most
   1 by the same convergent series as above.

Absolute convergence justifies these expansions. Hence every accepted
witness obeys

    log |L(sigma,chi_x)| >= S(x)-42
      >= (1/4)*log(log(B+1))-43,

or |L| >= exp(-43)*(log(B+1))^(1/4).

This is an asymptotically growing lower bound on an explicit mathematical
object. It is much too loose to imply |L|>1 at the native parameters.
No native L-function value is computed or certified as large here.
The directly evaluated finite Euler product is recorded separately.
Do not extend the statement to s=1 using an unproved approximation.

## Classical Critique And Prior Art

Large character L-values and resonance are established mathematics.
[Aistleitner, Mahatab, Munsch and Peyrot](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.00760)
prove stronger extremal existence results at s=1 and in the strip. Their
resonator-weighted sums are directly relevant to the sampling idea.
An existence proof is not automatically a polynomial-log-conductor
construction, but neither is it evidence of computational difficulty.
This pass establishes no novelty relative to algorithmic versions.

[Languasco's computational work](https://www.dei.unipd.it/~languasco/Littlewood_ineq.html)
uses FFT methods to evaluate many characters. The all-character table
cost must be charged; it cannot be equated with a polynomial in log M.
More importantly, a constructive resonator, efficient simultaneous
approximation, or sampler exploiting the smooth conductor could remove
the apparent algorithmic motivation without enumerating characters.

Classical threats that remain OPEN:

- LLL/BKZ and primitive-coset approximation on the high-weight primes.
  The objective weights small primes heavily; uniform-coordinate CVP
  experiments are not adequate comparators.
- A polynomial-time construction exploiting 3-adic logarithms and
  conductor lifting. A low-conductor solution alone is invalid.
- Classical sampling of the squared resonator or a different distribution
  that finds the same outputs. Exact distribution matching is unnecessary.
- Derandomizing the resonance existence argument without expanding an
  exponentially large smooth-number support.

Published distribution statements for prime moduli or real characters
must not be imported as hardness claims for these primitive 3-power
characters. Uniform random search is only a control, not the strongest
classical algorithm. Failure by it would prove no advantage.

The explicit finite control is frozen at seed 2026102027 and 32 uniformly
sampled primitive labels, using the existing J16 public labels with zero
centers. Every complete weighted score and Euler product is saved,
including failures. No seed, threshold or budget is adjusted after seeing
the outcome. Native quantum sampling is NOT executed.

Observed result on m=1024, B=8167, k=706, 1119-bit N:
**2 of 32 classical labels pass**, at zero-based trials 2 and 24.
The best normalized score is 0.3236971958, with S=0.6885637432 and
W=2.1271847642. Its log truncated-Euler-product modulus is 0.7571841716.
The inherited ideal quantum mean is 0.3040408367, not an executed score
or a maximum. The full-L lower formula is -42.450472305 in log scale,
so it supplies no useful native large-value certificate. These numbers
rule out presenting this finite task as a surviving quantum separation.

## Falsifiers And Decision

- A counterexample to primitive overlap cancellation invalidates the
  application. Tests cover exact conductor descent and explicit small
  Fourier identities; they are not proofs for unbounded m.
- An efficient classical accepted-witness generator removes the proposed
  computational distinction, even if it does not sample the quantum law.
- Existing quantum resonance constructions can remove novelty.
- If the only consequence remains a weak extremal-character search with
  no useful external reduction, its expected breakthrough contribution
  is limited. Do not promote it just because the objects are natural.
- Random native success invalidates a native separation story, but does
  not by itself settle asymptotic complexity.

Do not optimize the constants or extend this into another local benchmark
campaign. The next hard question is algorithmic: whether the primitive
resonance construction is already classically efficient, or enables a
substantive external computation. If neither route becomes concrete,
keep this as a review-pending application note and pivot.
