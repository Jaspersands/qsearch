# Subsystem 146: Majorana Fermionic Gaussian Matchgate $SO(2n)$ Parity Peeling Bridge

## 1. Mathematical Formulation
Under the Jordan-Wigner map ($\gamma_1 = X_1 I_2, \gamma_2 = Y_1 I_2, \gamma_3 = Z_1 X_2, \gamma_4 = Z_1 Y_2$), quadratic Majorana Hamiltonians generate $SO(2n)$ rotations preserving fermionic degree $1$. By contrast, $\mathrm{CNOT}_{1\to 2}$ conjugates $\gamma_1 = X_1 I_2 \mapsto X_1 X_2 = -i\gamma_1\gamma_2\gamma_3$ (Majorana degree $3$), explaining why combining planar matchgates with non-parity-preserving CNOT or SWAP gates breaks $SO(2n)$ Gaussian simulability.
