## Search The Actual Supremum

The all-POVM lemma uses K_F=sup_(||w||2=1) E_s|sum_x w_x chi_s(Ax)|^4.
For actual full-frequency pair-sum buckets g, its objective is

    P(w)=sum_g |sum_(i,j in bucket g) w_i*w_j|^2.

Triangle inequality shows P(w)<=P(|w|), so nonnegative real weights suffice
for the GLOBAL fourth-moment supremum. This does NOT say the best mutual-
information POVM has nonnegative seeds, nor that our local search finds the
global maximum. The objective is a fourth power of a norm of a linear Fourier
map and hence convex on real vectors. Its gradient is4*S(w)*w, where
S_ij=sum_(a,b in bucket(i,j)) w_a*w_b. Replacing a unit w by the normalized
gradient cannot lower P, by the supporting-hyperplane inequality and Cauchy.
This monotone power step on the sphere can still converge to a local optimum.

Every retained result is converted into bounded nonnegative INTEGER weights
and replayed with integer bucket sums. Thus a witness is exact rational
sum_g (sum_(i,j in g) a_i*a_j)^2/(sum_i a_i^2)^2; floating-point optimizer
values never falsify a claimed bound. Simple threshold sparsification is also
tested and rationally replayed, rather than hiding small optimization tails.

## A Large Moment Still Does Not Give A Useful POVM

For a normalized seed w and injective uniform-secret fiber ensemble, its
covariant orbit can be completed to a valid POVM by diagonal padding. Set
effects E_g=|w_g><w_g|/(N*max_x|w_x|^2), with N the secret-group size.
Their sum is diag(|w_x|^2/max|w|^2)<=I. Complete with the remaining nonnegative
diagonal effect. That effect carries no secret information.

The ENTIRE covariant-orbit branch has probability

    p_branch=1/(D*max|w|^2).

Its posterior divergence is J(w)=E_s |f_w(s)|^2 log2|f_w(s)|^2, which is
at most log2 P(w), not at least it. Therefore this completed single-seed
measurement has information at most p_branch*log2 P(w). A high fourth moment
alone is not an information lower bound. The n10 uniform12-vertex support
has p_branch=12/1016=3/254 and information<=.022523 bit. Treating it as
a constant-probability receiver would discard nearly99% of the actual source.

This bound concerns that completed SINGLE seed, not all POVMs. Multiple seed
families can share the diagonal capacity budget; other readouts may work.
No dense covariant effect table has been compiled into an efficient circuit.

## Exact Source-Paid Cube Counts

A physical disjoint-flip d-cube is generated by d nonempty pairwise disjoint
support directions and a Boolean origin. Each coordinate is constant or
assigned to one of d directions. Inclusion-exclusion and quotienting the2^d
origin choices and d! direction orderings gives EXACT distinct-cube count

    N_(m,d)=2^(m-d)/d! * sum_(j=0)^d (-1)^j binom(d,j)(d+1-j)^m.

For IID A mod4, making all vertices of a fixed cube share a syndrome requires
d independent unit-coefficient linear constraints per row. Its probability is
G^-d, G=4^n. For an independent uniform original word x*, an additional
syndrome equality has probability1 if x* is IN that cube and G^-1 otherwise.
If outside, a changed constant coordinate or a partially flipped direction
block supplies a unit (d+1)-minor, so this statement is valid over Z4, not a
field-rank guess. The exact expectation of cubes in the BORN-sampled fiber is

    E[cubes in F_(A*x*)]
      = N_(m,d)/G^(d+1) * [1+(G-1)*2^d/2^m].

The bracket is the Born size-bias correction; do not silently replace the
syndrome by an independent uniform group element. Markov bounds the probability
that ANY such cube exists, including adaptively chosen cubes after seeing A,y.
