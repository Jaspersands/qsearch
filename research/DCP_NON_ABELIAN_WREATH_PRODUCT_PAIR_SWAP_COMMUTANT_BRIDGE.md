# DCP Non-Abelian Wreath-Product ($S_2 \wr S_M$) Pair-Swap Commutant Bridge (Subsystem 90)

## Scope & Overview
This subsystem establishes the exact sector decomposition and distinguishing distance laws for $M$ pairs of two-copy DCP states under the hyperoctahedral / wreath product symmetry group $B_M = S_2 \wr S_M = (\mathbb{Z}_2)^M \rtimes S_M$.

## Exact Mathematical Laws
1. **Wreath-Product Singlet-Count Sector Decomposition**:
   Under intra-pair swap $\mathrm{SWAP}_m$ and inter-pair permutation $S_M$, the $4^M$-dimensional space $(\mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^2)^{\otimes M}$ decomposes into $M + 1$ sectors indexed by the singlet count $k \in \{0, \dots, M\}$ of exact dimension $\binom{M}{k} 3^{M-k}$, with $\sum_{k=0}^M \binom{M}{k} 3^{M-k} = 4^M$.
2. **Binomial Singlet Sufficiency vs. Global $\mathrm{Sym}^{2M}(\mathbb{C}^2)$ Dominance**:
   Measuring each pair in the singlet/triplet basis yields $K_{\mathrm{sing}} \sim \mathrm{Binomial}(M, \frac{1 - \gamma^2 \cos \theta_0}{2})$, whereas when all $2M$ copies share the same signed phase $\theta_0 \in \{0, \pi\}$, projecting onto the global symmetric subspace $\mathrm{Sym}^{2M}(\mathbb{C}^2)$ strictly outperforms the Binomial singlet-count statistic ($D_{\mathrm{TV}}^{\mathrm{Sym}^{2M}} > D_{\mathrm{TV}}^{\mathrm{Binomial-singlet}}$).
