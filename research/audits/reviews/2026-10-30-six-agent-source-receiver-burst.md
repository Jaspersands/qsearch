## Decision

Do not implement a general SDE backend as though that were a new algorithmic
result. Its missing branches reproduce existing coverage. Prioritize a
constructive reduction in the field receiver's recursive source cost, with
complete-channel correctness and a paid classical controller. The first
coherent all-grade shortcut now has an exact, narrowly scoped falsifier.

No examined application establishes a new useful speedup. For public
arithmetic circuits, source construction is feasible; a compelling surviving
application is the missing step. For unknown quantum states, preserve the
copy-access contract and use actual measurement competitors, not a classical
description supplied for free.

## New Receiver Result

See [COHERENT_GRADE_SELECTOR.md](../../COHERENT_GRADE_SELECTOR.md).
On four genuine class-three Rees sources over F5, condition on a specified
central record but leave lower-grade characters coherent. Every one of
625 lower-label configurations admits a simultaneous moment witness.
Nevertheless no COMPLETE scalar-diagonal, moment-supported selector can
preserve the required hidden-subgroup coherence on every successful branch.

An integer dual certificate on 31 projective directions and 775 affine
lines bounds conditional acceptance by 109/125, hence abort by at least
16/125. This is a finite instrument-class obstruction, not an asymptotic
copy lower bound, an optimality claim, or proof that the sequential source
product is necessary. Its central record has probability 5^-8; rare sectors
cannot be promoted into unproved global constant losses.

Uniform-over-witness selection also fails a simpler control: a legal hidden
action takes a label with 124 witnesses to one with 44, changing the selected
witness's amplitude despite valid cancellation at both points. Reversibly
computing a witness does not by itself repair that normalization.

Gibbs's review identified an essential boundary: moment support is a
hypothesis, not forced by quotient correctness alone. For this particular
hidden line, a suitable fixed section can work without cubic cancellation.
The proof note now says this explicitly and does not rule out that broader
class of receivers.

## Next Constructive Gates

1. A larger-bank or non-diagonal collective receiver must exhibit a
   succinct selection rule, paid implementation, and a proved unconditional
   acceptance bound. Test the complete quotient channel before improving
   source-count formulas. An exponentially large feasible LP is not an
   implementation.
2. A public-polynomial application must name a motivated circuit family and
   survive orbit-module, Frobenius, factorization and fixed-anchor attacks.
   Do not continue treating amplitude access as missing for ordinary
   division-free arithmetic DAGs with paid extension-field semantics.
3. A copy-only state application must beat the decoded Fourier, window-table,
   and reconstruction/index competitors under the SAME input contract.
   Degenerate parent spaces and low bond dimension alone establish neither
   easiness nor hardness.

These are focused research questions, not newly accepted candidates. No
