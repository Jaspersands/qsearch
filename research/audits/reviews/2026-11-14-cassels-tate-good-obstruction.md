# A rational Jacobian class need not satisfy Shukla's good-conic assumption

Independent local proof check, 2026-11-14. No computation was rerun.
Source locators below are physical LF from `split('\n')` in
`audits/sources/cassels_tate_selection/shukla_thesis.txt`.
The existing exact control is
`audits/controls/cassels_tate_good_obstruction/result.json`.

## 1. Source contract and signs

Shukla LF5504–5514 defines, for a fixed root theta_1,

    C_1j: d_j u_j^2 - d_1 v_j^2 + theta_j - theta_1 = 0.

The required points lie in k(theta_j), with their Galois conjugates chosen
compatibly. Assumption 4.4.3 is exactly this requirement (LF5529–5530).
Algorithm 2 explicitly requires a good first Selmer argument (LF5725–5727)
and solves, over the ordered-pair root field,

    d(theta_1) U^2 - d(theta_2) V^2 = theta_2 - theta_1

for the root pairs (LF5730–5740). The formulas agree after exchanging
the names of the two variables in C_1j. In our example every root and
ordered-pair root field is Q. Galois conjugation supplies no larger allowed
field; adjoining an extra square root would change this source requirement.

## 2. An infinite family of genuine rational Mumford classes

For integers b,c satisfying b^2-5c^2=-1 and b>=38, put

    f_b(x)=x(x^2-1)(x^2-4)(x^2-b^2),
    u(x)=x^2+1,                 v(x)=5c(1-x).

The seven roots of f_b are distinct; y^2=f_b is a smooth genus-three curve
with its rational point at infinity. Modulo u, x^2=-1, whence

    f_b = -10(1+b^2)x,          v^2 = -50c^2 x  (mod u).

These are equal. Moreover u is monic, deg(v)<deg(u)=2<=3, and gcd(u,f_b)=1.
Thus (u,v) is a reduced rational Mumford pair, under the exact conventions
of Definition 1.2.17 and Proposition 1.2.18 (LF851–878). Concretely its
effective divisor consists of (i,5c(1-i)) and (-i,5c(1+i)); the points are
Galois conjugates, are non-Weierstrass, and are not hyperelliptic conjugates.
It defines P=[D-2 infinity] in J_b(Q).

Proposition 1.5.2 (LF2156–2166) gives the true odd-degree Kummer map.
Since gcd(u,f_b)=1 and deg(u)=2, its value is d=u(theta), with no minus sign
and no quotient by diagonal rational squareclasses. Explicitly, in root order
(-b,-2,-1,0,1,2,b), it is

    d=(5c^2,5,2,1,2,5,5c^2).

Its norm is (50c^2)^2, as required. This is the Kummer image of a rational
point, hence belongs to Sel^2(J_b/Q) with all local conditions satisfied.
It is nonzero in J_b(Q)/2J_b(Q): its theta=2 component is 5, not a square
in Q. No assumption about the Mordell–Weil rank or Sha is needed.

There are infinitely many such native inputs: take

    b+c sqrt(5)=(2+sqrt(5))(9+4sqrt(5))^k,     k>=1.

The factors have norms -1 and 1. The recurrence
(b,c)->(9b+20c,4b+9c) gives positive strictly increasing b,c, starting at
(38,17). Thus every member has distinct roots and the same obstruction.

## 3. The required conic is insoluble even locally

Choose theta_1=0 and theta_j=2. Then d_1=1,d_j=5, and the source equation is

    U^2-5V^2=2.

The extension Q_2(sqrt(5))/Q_2 is unramified quadratic: (1+sqrt(5))/2
satisfies X^2-X-1, whose reduction X^2+X+1 is irreducible over F_2.
Every nonzero norm therefore has even 2-adic valuation. The right side has
valuation one, proving insolubility. This also rules out every nontrivial
point on U^2-5V^2=2W^2: W=0 would make 5 a square in Q_2.
Equivalently (5,2)_2=-1, matching the saved PARI result for (b,c)=(38,17).
Independent square rescalings of d_1,d_j merely rescale U,V; the obstruction
is therefore to the given Kummer class, not to its chosen representative.
Reversing the ordered root pair gives the same equation after rearrangement.

## 4. Exact consequence and limitation

Automatic Assumption 4.4.3 is false even for nonzero rational
Mordell–Weil/2 classes, in this fixed odd-degree model: the required row
theta_1=0 fails, so Algorithm 2's all-pair good-input condition fails.
This is an all-k proof, not an inference from the finite control's PASS label.

It does NOT show the absence of a good generating basis, failure of a
different model or pairing algorithm, or difficulty of computing this
class's pairing. Indeed its image in Sha is zero, so its Cassels–Tate pairing
with every Selmer class is zero. Definition 4.4.5 (LF5662–5664) deliberately
adjoins the entire Mordell–Weil image to the subgroup generated by good
elements when defining a good curve. Our example does not refute that
strategy, any claimed index bound, or the genus-two result in Theorem 4.4.6.
The usable obstruction is precisely to treating the good-conic hypothesis
as automatic for an arbitrary basis returned by a native Selmer compiler.

## 5. Good elements are not closed under addition: an exact genus-two example

Let f=x(x^2-1)(x^2-4) and T_a=[(a,0)-infinity] for a rational root a.
Proposition 1.5.2 gives d_a(theta)=a-theta for theta!=a and
d_a(a)=f'(a). Thus every T_a satisfies every required good conic:
for roots theta_i,theta_j both different from a, take U=V=1;
for theta_i=a take U=0,V=1; for theta_j=a take U=1,V=0.

The zero coordinate in the last two points is harmless despite Algorithm 2's
UV!=0 condition. Write the first such conic as A U^2-B V^2=-B, where
A=f'(a)!=0 and B=a-theta_j!=0. Its rational parametrization is

    U=2Bt/(A-Bt^2),             V=(A+Bt^2)/(A-Bt^2).

Choose rational t avoiding 0 and the roots of A-Bt^2 and A+Bt^2.
Both coordinates are then defined and nonzero. Reversing the ordered pair
handles the other case. All root fields are Q, so compatibility under Galois
conjugation is automatic. Square rescalings of d only rescale U,V.

In root order (-2,-1,0,1,2), the exact tuples are

    delta(T_0)=(2,1,4,-1,-2),   delta(T_2)=(4,3,2,1,24),
    delta(T_0+T_2)=(8,3,8,-1,-48).

Here f'(0)=4 and f'(2)=24, fixing the exceptional-coordinate signs.
The pair theta_i=-1,theta_j=1 for the sum requires 3U^2+V^2=2.
Its homogenization 3U^2+V^2=2W^2 has no nonzero Q_3 point: scale to
primitive integral coordinates. Modulo 3, V^2=2W^2 forces 3|V,W;
the equation then forces 3|U, contradicting primitivity.

Consequently two good rational MW/2 (hence Selmer) classes have a nongood
sum. Goodness is genuinely not an F_2-linear condition, even for this
native split genus-two curve. The saved `nonlinear.json` agrees with these
identities and records (-3,2)_3=-1; it was read, not rerun.
This does not refute a good spanning set (the two summands already span
this bad element), imply generic quadratic-system hardness, or obstruct
the Cassels–Tate pairing: these classes still lie in its MW radical.
