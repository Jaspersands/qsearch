Quantity: the tail index gamma of X = B/median(B) across primes at fixed kappa and lambda window, by the Hill estimate
  over the top k = floor(N/4) primes, with a 2000-resample bootstrap over primes (numpy seed 1) for its 95% range.
  Reader: osc-mc/tail_index.py (written 2026-11-11 09:39:24, before the runs). B from k6_spike_survey.analyse (point estimates).
Why: if P(X > x) ~ x^(-1/gamma), the sup over the N_T ~ kappa^{(1/2+o(1))kappa} primes near the threshold multiplies the
  median by kappa^{gamma (1/2+o(1)) kappa}. That spends a fraction ~gamma of the B_L tolerance (1/2 - 1/(2 ln kappa))kappa
  and ~gamma_D/2 of the B_D tolerance (1 - 1/ln kappa)kappa. Obstruction (c) in spread form: gamma_L -> >= 1, or
  gamma_D -> >= 2, along the threshold path. This is a heuristic extrapolation from tens of primes to kappa^{kappa/2}.
