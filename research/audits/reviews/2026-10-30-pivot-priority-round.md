   residue rank. The two-factor mod25 example has rank1 and no legal
   frozen-support lift. If its prefix defined the outer frame instead,
   the suffix fails membership; neither interpretation repairs it.
2. The d1 deterministic shortcut can select a zero pivot and a singular
   field witness even when another regular witness exists. Production
   code already disclaims regularity; it was not defective or changed.
3. The regular-pivot conditional moments and a paid all-prime schedule
   work, but do not solve every bank with a regular witness. The anchor
   restriction itself can cause rejection. Zero belongs in the suffix
   alphabet; omitting it destroys candidate growth at d=p-1.
4. Polynomial workspace does not require this backend. Existing Boolean
   suffix length L=O(u logu) permits exhaustive2^L-mask enumeration within
   the same total2^O(u logu) parameter class and same bank success event.
5. The all-prime FIELD pivot method already supports depth-first trees,
   streaming final Fourier readout and sequential rank-preserving outer
   calls. Under the written source/conversion contracts, live workspace
   is polynomial while the existing rank-FPT time remains unchanged.
6. A supposed copy-only distinction was withdrawn by the parent during
   inspection: one-copy controlled-right-action transport already gives
   legal local restrictions/quotients with flattened representatives.
   It does not reconstruct f, and does not need to do so.
