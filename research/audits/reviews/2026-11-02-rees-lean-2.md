## 4. New theorem statements (Lean)

### 4.1 `BCHClassFour` (field F, 2 ≠ 0 and 3 ≠ 0, L of class ≤ 4)

- `bch x y = x + y + ½[x,y] + (1/12)([x,[x,y]] + [y,[y,x]]) − (1/24)[y,[x,[x,y]]]`.
- `bch_assoc : lcs F L L 4 = ⊥ → bch (bch x y) z = bch x (bch y z)`.
- `instGroup` defines the group `BCH F L hnil`, with identity 0 and inverse −x.
- `val_pow` and `val_zpow` say that `(x^n).val = n • x.val`.
- `lieHom`, `lieEquiv` and `map_bch` say that Lie maps are group maps, with no class
  hypothesis.
- `bch_of_lie_eq_zero` says that commuting pairs multiply additively.
- **Proof method.** Both sides are normalised to right-normed brackets with a sorted innermost
  pair. The normalisation uses `jacobi3` (`[z,[x,y]] → [y,[x,z]] − [x,[y,z]]`) and three
  degree-four rules `r1`, `r2` and `r5`, each derived from one instance of `jacobi4b`. Then
  `match_scalars; field_simp; ring` finishes. The elaboration takes about 1 minute.
- **A failed first attempt.** It used a rule set chosen without regard to `jacobi3`, and simp
  looped. The rule `[x,[x,[y,z]]] → … [x,[z,[x,y]]]` and the degree-three rewrite of
  `[z,[x,y]]` undo each other, and simp hit its recursion limit. The fix was to choose the
  degree-four rules among words whose degree-three suffix is already reduced
  (`rules4_lean.py`).

### 4.2 `BCHClassFourSubgroups` (ZMod p, p > 3)

Notation: `C(a,b)` is `commPoly`, the logarithm of `x y x⁻¹ y⁻¹`. In normal form,
`C(a,b) = ab + ½(aab + bab) + (1/6)aaab + (1/4)baab + (1/6)bbab`, where `ab = [a,b]`,
`aab = [a,[a,b]]`, `bab = [b,[a,b]]`, `aaab = [a,[a,[a,b]]]`, `baab = [b,[a,[a,b]]]` and
`bbab = [b,[b,[a,b]]]`.

- `val_comm`, `val_comm_left` and `val_comm_right` give the values of the commutators and
  double commutators. The three triple commutators are exactly `aaab`, `baab` and `bbab`.
- `bracketWord x y = c₁ · c₂ₓ^{−1/2} · c₂ᵧ^{−1/2} · c₃ₓₓ^{1/3} · c₃ᵧₓ^{1/4} · c₃ᵧᵧ^{1/3}`.
  `val_bracketWord` proves that its value is `[x,y]`.
- `addWord x y = x y · bw^{−1/2} · c₂ₓ^{1/6} · c₂ᵧ^{1/3} · c₃ₓₓ^{−5/24} · c₃ᵧₓ^{−3/8} · c₃ᵧᵧ^{−11/24}`.
  `val_addWord` proves that its value is `x + y`.
- `toLieSubalgebra` maps any subgroup to a Lie subalgebra. `toSubgroup` maps any Lie subalgebra
  to a subgroup. Both round trips hold (`toLieSubalgebra_toSubgroup`,
  `toSubgroup_toLieSubalgebra`).
- The word coefficients come from the exact model `words4.py`. Lean checks every identity
  independently.

### 4.3 `ReesGeneralLawClassFour`

- `bchLaw : NilpotentGroupLaw F 4`, `bchLaw_additive`, and
  `bchLaw_subgroups : SubgroupsAreSubalgebras (bchLaw (F := ZMod p))`.
- `classFour_tuple_hides_arbitrary_subgroup` holds for any points.
- `canonicalPoints_isUnit (4 < p)` says the points 1, 2, 3, 4 have a unit Vandermonde
  determinant.
- **`classFour_tuple_hides_coeff`.** Take p > 4, any L of class ≤ 4 over ZMod p, any subgroup
  H ≤ G and any f hiding H. Then there is a Lie subalgebra h with H = exp h, and for all a, b in
  G̃:
  `(f(E_{s_i} a))_{i=1..4} = (f(E_{s_i} b))_i ↔ ∀ r, coeff(a⁻¹ ⋆ b) r ∈ h`.
- `classFour_step_output` gives each measured step at class four. Its remaining inputs are the
  source, the SDE witness and the branch traces.
- `classFour_terminal_source` gives the terminal source for an arbitrary H.

### 4.4 `ReesGeneralMeasuredReceiver` (any c, F finite, L finite; conditional on `NilpotentGroupLaw` and `AdditiveOnCommuting`)

- `convertStep` is one complete measured stage on fresh copies of ρ. It applies the
  full-register central instrument (`linearKraus`), sums over all label records, and applies the
  padded dilation action. It takes neither H nor log H.
- `measured_step_output` handles one record: the output is the branch mass times the next coset
  source, impossible records included.
- `averaged_step_output` and `convert_step_source` state the summed version:
  `convertStep(source_{d+1}) = source_d`. They need `chi ≠ 1`, a total policy that is active
  and satisfies the SDE, and admissible points.
- `descend` composes the stages from stage c down to stage d, by recursion on c − d, using the
  public section. `descend_top` and `descend_step` unfold it.
- **`descend_source`.** Started from the exact top coset source, `descend` equals the exact
  stage-d coset source, for every d ≤ c.
- **`measured_terminal_source`.** The stage-1 entry at `(x, y)` is
  `if y − x ∈ h then 1/|L| else 0`.
- **`classFour_measured_terminal_source`.** The same holds at c = 4 for an arbitrary subgroup H,
  with every group-law hypothesis discharged.

## 5. What remains outside the boundary

1. **No group law is constructed for c ≥ 5.** That needs the degree-5 BCH terms (with
   denominator 720, so p > 5), new degree-5 normal-form rules, and longer subgroup words. A
   general-c proof needs the Dynkin formula and the free-nilpotent argument. The `jacobi3`
   compatibility rule of §4.1 is the trap to avoid at higher degree.
2. **Algorithmic inputs are unchanged.** As in round 1, the following remain inputs: valid SDE
   witness policies, the solvers and their arity bounds (`thm:power-sde`, `thm:random-column`,
   `eq:is-arity`), preparation of the exact initial lifted coset source, oracle implementation,
   approximation errors, the final Fourier step and running time. Fresh banks are supplied
   copies, not cloning.
3. **Scope of the general-class receiver.** It assumes a finite field F and a finite L. The
   class-four instance is over `ZMod p` only. The subgroup correspondence over an extension
   field is false in general, and is not claimed.

## 6. Exact statement that now holds (Lean)

For every prime p > 4 and every finite Lie algebra L over `ZMod p` with
`lowerCentralSeries L L 4 = ⊥`, the following hold:

- The quartic BCH product makes L a group G.
- Every subgroup H ≤ G equals exp h for a unique Lie subalgebra h.
- At the points 1, 2, 3, 4, the tuple oracle on the Rees group G̃ hides exactly exp h̃, where
  h̃ = ⊕_r t^r(h ∩ γ_r).
- Each central step kernel is central and additive, with weight u^{d+1}.
- Run on fresh banks from the exact top coset source, the three-stage measured receiver
  outputs the additive coset source of all of h. It is given a nontrivial character and total
  valid SDE policies.

In general class, the same conclusions hold conditionally on an explicit natural group law
(plus `AdditiveOnCommuting`, plus `SubgroupsAreSubalgebras` for an arbitrary H). That law is
discharged at c = 3 and at c = 4.

The access model is the tuple oracle built from the original hiding function. Lean charges no
query count, error or running time.
