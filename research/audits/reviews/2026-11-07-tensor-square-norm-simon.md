## Bounded-degree versus full-closure support

The displayed GENERAL Simon construction uses fixed fields of cyclic
subgroups of the full closure, not only bounded-degree intermediate fields.
For instance, in the natural S_n extension a transposition-fixed field has
degree n!/2. When n is even, its order2 subgroup fits the prime-divisor
restriction stated in the page74 recipe. This illustrates the possible
auxiliary size; it does not prove that every such field is indispensable or
that Simon's algorithm has no instance-specific simplification.

The present tensor-square hypothesis instead uses the factors of L tensor L,
whose total degree is n^2, and its paired-relative-norm proof. That particular
auxiliary choice and its polynomial discriminant/support estimate are not
supplied by the inspected displayed Simon criteria. The norm-one
principalization strategy and S-unit linear-algebra endpoint ARE prior art.

There are important existing shortcuts: **page71, Corollaries III.2.12--13**,
visually verified, establish equality for every S when gcd(h(L),[L:Q])=1
and either the degree is odd or the field is totally complex of even degree.
These must not be erased by saying Simon always requires the full closure.
They impose additional class-number hypotheses and do not state the proposed
unrestricted tensor-square support bound. Pages72--73 discuss integral and
S-integral solutions, including degree3/4/6 results and a degree5 obstruction;
those are different from the present rational S-unit output contract.
