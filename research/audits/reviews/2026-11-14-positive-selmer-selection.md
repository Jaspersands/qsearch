## 1. Exact object being evaluated

GENERAL_NATIVE_SELMER_COMPOSITION.md claims, under its stated GRH,
bounded-error quantum poly(n,B,log(1/eta)) time for

    dim_F2 Sel^(2)(Jac(y^2=f)/Q),

where f is native monic squarefree integral of odd degree n=2g+1 and its
coefficients are explicitly supplied in binary. Branch fields may have
growing degree, be nonnormal, and have arbitrary bad reduction. No units,
orders, local images, tower, or normal closure is given. The output is a
dimension, not the Mordell--Weil rank or rational points on coverings.

The potential value is a uniform arithmetic quantum upper bound whose
global expensive operation is complete compact units; the local-image
compiler is classical. This is a more concrete native endpoint than the
recent phase/norm/copy proposals that have no charged output decoder.
Its substantial existing proof coverage makes redundant rereview low-value.

## 2. Specific suspected failures and the existing answers

I checked the global note in full, LOCAL_KUMMER_ALL_PRIMES.md, and targeted
proof passages rather than relying on their acceptance labels.

* **Independent units versus complete squareclasses.** The global theorem
  explicitly invokes complete Biasse--Song corrected-v2 Theorem 1 and
  exact compact recovery, not merely independent valid units. The GRH
  class-generator set T has polynomial numerical norm cutoff. In the
  parity kernel, outside-T even valuations can be cancelled modulo squares
  because the corresponding half-ideal class is T-supported. This is a
  completeness argument, not an unpaid principalization by the controller.
  Locators: global note 47-69; global proof review Sections 1-3.

* **Missing torsion or wrong norm quotient.** Iterated square tests from
  -1 recover the 2-primary root-of-unity generator, including fields such
  as Q(i). The separating norm polynomial has degree at most twice the
  branch degree, with a polynomial-size deterministic separating grid.
  The odd TOTAL branch degree splits the diagonal norm map; no quotient
  by rational squareclasses is incorrectly imposed. The real image is the
  adjacent-root-pair span, consistent with signs of points in the positive
  bounded intervals. Locators: global note 70-84 and 102-126; global proof
  review 255-273. These checks supplied no new counterexample.

* **Uninterpretable local frames or approximate-factor advice.** The native
  local backend explicitly names ACTUAL factors of the original f by
  faithful OM identifiers. Its integral-basis construction uses the lower
  frame at the actual root, not an uncharged field isomorphism from a
  finite approximant. Finite precision is refined inside that fixed factor.
  Locators: 2026-11-13-native-local-squareclass-backend.md:58-100 and
  340-377. Global compact atoms are evaluated in those same frames;
  reducing their exponents modulo two avoids expanded huge units.

* **Random-coordinate independence and uncharged refinement.** The Haar
  proof treats conjugates of one closed point using embedding DIFFERENCE
  linear forms and a discriminant content bound, not independent samples.
  Its parameter-to-Jacobian estimate is an area upper bound, so it does
  not insert an inverse Jacobian denominator. The final local compiler
  separately pays finite-grid transfer and uses a fixed descending
  precision clock P_star=P_cert+T0*C_max. The extra Res(U,W) half-extraction
  guard is already mandatory; a determinant alone is not its certificate.
  Locators: 2026-11-13-kummer-coordinate-haar-tail.md:49-180;
  LOCAL_KUMMER_NATIVE_END_TO_END.md:242-341; all-prime note:62-97.
  The odd-prime source explicitly pays integration denominators, binary
  rejection and log p; it does not enumerate p residues.

These are genuine logical interfaces worth attacking, but the obvious
missing-premise versions are already repaired. This pass did not re-prove
the entire Haar, finite-grid, materializer or halving theorem and does not
label an uninspected detail false merely because its proof is long.

## 3. The classical comparison is stronger than the old final paragraph

For general branch fields, retain the SAME descent with classical global
class/unit arithmetic. No reduction proving that Selmer DIMENSION requires
full units or a class group has been supplied. The existing classical-bypass
disposition correctly rejects that necessity claim: a scalar dimension
need not determine a localization subspace or a class-group presentation.

The literal dyadic cyclotomic family already has a pure classical match.
More generally, QUARTIC_RELATIVE_NORM_CLASSICAL.md:11-42 now covers EVERY
quartic branch field containing a quadratic subfield, with no odd S-class
promise, by a polynomial-bit reduction to integer factoring. The old
final paragraph of GENERAL_NATIVE_SELMER_COMPOSITION.md retains the
earlier narrower promise; it is not the strongest current comparison.
Polynomially many factoring queries on polynomial-bit INTERMEDIATES are
charged, not merely factorization of the original discriminant.

QUADRATIC_TOWER_RECURRENCE.md:11-39 goes further for supplied explicit
quadratic towers, under its relevant-Hecke ERH and factoring oracle.
It supplies complete expanded squareclasses and coordinate routines;
joining the existing local compiler removes a separate quantum-unit
advantage for that supplied-tower arithmetic scope. It does NOT give a
general native-f theorem: tower/order tables and maps are charged input,
and acquiring such a tower from an arbitrary branch polynomial is not
silently free. Degree being a power of two does not supply a quadratic
tower. Neither comparison refutes the general quantum upper bound.
