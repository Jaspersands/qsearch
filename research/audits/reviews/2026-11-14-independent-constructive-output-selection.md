## 1. Compact integral ternary transport

**Native problem.** An expanded totally real field K, its full maximal-order
basis/table, and binary integral nonsingular ternary Gram matrices A,B,
indefinite at a real place. Return NO or an integral unimodular U with
U^t A U=B. Use a polynomial-size arithmetic straight-line output with
structural integrality/unimodularity certificates; consumers pay expansion.
This would supply usable changes of basis for arithmetic lattices and their
associated orders, beyond the reviewed Boolean classification.

An expanded-output promise needs its OWN height theorem. The exponential
minimum-witness family in INTEGRAL_SIMILARITY_OUTPUT_SIZE.md concerns matrix
SIMILARITY; it has not been reduced to this ternary-isometry problem.
Neither a polynomial height claim nor a Pell obstruction transfers for free.

**Already paid.** DYADIC_NONFULL_SPINOR_TRANSPORT.md supplies general DECISION,
under the retained complete GRH arithmetic: rational transport, local genus
tests, relevant spinor offsets, and the complete ray-class obstruction.
INDEFINITE_TERNARY_SPINOR_ADMISSION.md:56–66 records the exact distinction:
Kirschmer Corollary 5.4.10 identifies indefinite spinor genus with class by
strong approximation; it supplies no polynomial-time global transporter.
Full local spinor-image cases were deliberately skipped by the decision
compiler and cannot silently become available local witnesses.

**Concrete proposed operation.** After the spinor obstruction vanishes,
construct the strong-approximation correction in the norm-one group of the
even Clifford quaternion algebra. A useful compiler must return a compact
word whose action has the specified finite local transporter cosets AND
preserves the required lattice at every other finite prime. It must obtain
any additional local witnesses itself. The proposed arithmetic mechanism is
factorization into norm-one elements of explicitly found quadratic tori,
using the existing complete field-unit/norm algorithms for those factors.
The target is quantum poly(native bits,log(1/eta)), with all field degrees,
moduli, word lengths, output representations and failures charged.

**One missing operation, stated sharply.** A polynomial, constructive
integral strong-approximation WORD COMPILER: find sufficiently many such
tori, compute the actual images of their integral norm-one units, and solve
the prescribed transporter coset with a polynomial-length compressed word.
Existence of rational quaternion factors or field units does not prove this.
No complete word algorithm is derived here. In particular a principal
quaternion ideal or order isomorphism must preserve the original ternary
lattice data; forgetting that data is not a valid reduction.

**Cheapest falsifier of a proposed shortcut.** A solution to the rational
norm equation can introduce new denominator primes. The reviewed norm
shortening explicitly does not preserve integrality or S-unit support
(EXPANDED_NORM_TERNARY_TRANSPORT.md:67–70). Even in split Spin=SL_2, for any
finite congruence modulus M, h=diag(r,r^-1), r=1+M>1, satisfies h=I mod M
locally at primes dividing M but is nonintegral at every prime dividing r.
Its adjoint action on the split ternary space scales the two root vectors
by r^2 and r^-2. Thus finite congruence matching alone does not certify
global integral transport. Conversely, choosing factors from one commuting
torus cannot supply arbitrary SL_2 residue targets. Neither observation is
an obstruction to a genuinely integral, noncommuting compiler.

**Classical comparison and source gate.** The same ray-class reduction,
classical lattice reduction, and constructive indefinite quaternion ideal
algorithms are the relevant competitors. The compact matrix-similarity
composition is already reviewed and cannot be relabeled as this result:
its split matrix-algebra unit descent does not construct norm-one quaternion
transporters with these lattice constraints. Before paid work, inspect
Aurel Page, *An algorithm for the principal ideal problem in indefinite
quaternion algebras*, and Kirschmer–Voight, *Algorithmic enumeration of ideal
classes for quaternion orders*. These are source requests, not imported
complexity theorems. A source proving the required constructor reduces this
candidate to a composition/baseline check; a mere existence theorem does not.

## 2. Central elliptic-curve L-values from coherent coefficient evaluation

**Independent native problem.** Given a nonsingular integral Weierstrass
equation E/Q with binary coefficients and rational epsilon>0, approximate
L(E,1) within absolute epsilon, failure eta. The desired clock would be
poly(input bits,1/epsilon,log(1/eta)), with no modular-symbol table, newform,
Fourier coefficients, conductor factorization or period ratio as advice.
This is a quantitative arithmetic output relevant to BSD computations;
it is NOT exact analytic rank, a zero test, or a rational-point algorithm.

**Concrete operation to screen.** Compute the conductor N and root number
w using native arithmetic; compute a_j coherently by factoring j, classical
elliptic point counting at its prime factors, and the multiplicative Hecke
recurrences. Then estimate the central-value sum by amplitude estimation.
For the standard weight-two normalization, the proposed functional-equation
interface is

    L(E,1)=(1+w) sum_(j>=1) (a_j/j) exp(-j/X),   X=sqrt(N)/(2 pi).

For w=-1 the value is zero. For w=1, grant this identity, its effective tail,
and even cheap preparation of the following positive envelope;
the normalization problem persists. The identity/source and current fastest
classical clock still require parent primary checking before admission.

The elementary bound |a_j|<=d(j)sqrt(j) gives the bounded variable
z_j=a_j/(d(j)sqrt(j)) and envelope

    W(X)=sum_(j>=1) d(j)/sqrt(j) exp(-j/X),    L(E,1)=2W(X) E[z_j].

Here W(X)=Theta(sqrt(X) log X) as X grows. Upper bound: use
sum_(j<=t)d(j)<=t(1+log t) and dyadic intervals. Lower bound: expand d(j)
as the count of j=ab, retain a<=sqrt(X), b<=X/a, and use exp(-ab/X)>=e^-1
and sum_(b<=T)b^-1/2>=c sqrt(T). This leaves
c sqrt(X) sum_(a<=sqrt(X))1/a. Thus W=Theta(N^(1/4) log N).

Absolute epsilon requires additive error epsilon/(2W) in the bounded mean.
The standard amplitude-estimation compiler therefore has certified cost
O(W/epsilon), up to confidence and coefficient-arithmetic factors, even
GRANTING its envelope-state constructor. It has not achieved a clock
polynomial in binary N. Truncation, coefficient evaluation, and clean inverse
preparation would also need explicit caps; they cannot remove this factor.
This is a failure of the proposed normalization/estimator, not a lower bound
for elliptic L-values or for all variance-aware quantum estimators.

**Strongest comparator to check.** Classical approximate functional equations
with roughly sqrt(N) terms, coefficient sieving/point counting, and modular
symbols; period computation alone does not determine the period multiple.
Ask for actual Tim Dokchitser, *Computing special values of motivic L-functions*,
and Cremona, *Algorithms for Modular Elliptic Curves*, plus a current
conductor-aspect algorithm/prior quantum screen. No absence-of-source
hardness claim. Without a new cancellation-preserving observable normalized
by poly(log N), this is not the next high-upside native operation.
