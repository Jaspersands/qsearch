appendix:15:

```latex
For general $\kappa$, let $C_i$, of order $n_i$, be the group generated by $\eta$ after $i-1$ restarts, so $C_1=H_1$ and $\Omega(n_i)\ge\Omega(|H_1|)+i-1$, and let $D\le H_1$, of order $\prod_q q^{a_q}$, be generated by the quotients of the $\pi$-parts. ### 6. ERROR (edge case) — rem:sde-worst-case-q1(i): "The condition U_L≠0 holds whenever the bank has at least D_3(r)+r columns" fails for banks with zero columns
```
*Where.* `appendix-sde-side.tex:82`.

*Counterexample.* Take κ = 2, p = 7 and the bank e₁, e₂, 0, 0, 0, 0. It has rank 2 and
D_3(2) + 2 = 6 columns.
- Under the forward pass of rem:sde-worst-case(b), every zero column satisfies c ∈ U = 0 and
  has no coefficient on B ∖ U. So it is a true-pool event and is discarded.
- The pass therefore stalls with U_L = 0.
- Run in `O/sde/zero_cols.py` → `zero_cols.out`: "stall, dim U_L = 0, true-pool events = 4".
- Control: 2000 random rank-2 banks of 6 nonzero columns, 0 stalls with U_L = 0.

*The claim holds for nonzero columns.* With D_3(r) ≥ r + 1 nonzero columns outside B, the
first nonzero dependency gives a circuit of size ≥ 2, and U grows.

*Origin.* The sentence comes from the q1-check's §3.1, where "π ≥ D_3(q) − q ≥ 1, so U ≠ 0"
silently assumes nonzero pool columns.

*Fix.*

```latex
The condition $U_L\ne0$ holds whenever the bank has at least $D_3(r)+r$ nonzero columns; a zero column is itself a solution.
```
