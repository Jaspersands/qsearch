## 4. `rem:fc-tradeoff` and its source sentence (appendix-free-coordinate.tex, end)

**fine** — (a), construction. The K triangular rounds produce U_K inside the label space from the
first K(2a-K+3)/2 = sum_{i<K}(a-i+1) labels; fresh labels have images independent and uniform
on the quotient given U_K; the free-coordinate node on the images returns a nonzero
x in D^{g+n_0} with sum x_i lambda_i in U_K, and the back-substitution of
`prop:triangular-sde` works for any element of U_K (it only needs tau in U_k), on disjoint
supports, so the combined weight vector is nonzero with entries in D. K = 0 and K = a give the
free-coordinate node and (with n_0 = 1) exactly (a+1)(a+2)/2 labels.

**error 4.1** — (a): "a nonzero solution is found except with probability
$\beta(g+n_0, n_0; g) + 2^{-B}$". The free-coordinate node has three capped random steps, so
`lem:fc-node`(c) gives beta + 3*2^{-B}; in addition, the weights of the K triangular rounds need
kappa-th roots (Las Vegas in `prop:triangular-sde`), and for kappa >= 3 without a given generator g
the coset-generator search is Las Vegas too. The stated failure bound is therefore too small.
Replacement:
`a nonzero solution is found except with probability at most $\beta(g+n_0, n_0; g) + 5\cdot 2^{-B}$, where the root extractions for the weights of the $K$ rounds and the search for the coset generator $g$ (when it is not given) are capped at $2^{-B}$ each as in~(F4)`.

**imprecision 4.2** — (a): with L = M - n_0 = g, the second term of beta(g+n_0, n_0; g) is
(p^g-1)/((p-1)p^g), close to 1/(p-1); the bound is not small for small p. If a small failure
probability tau is intended, use `$K(2a-K+3)/2 + g + n_0 + \lceil\log_p(2/\tau)\rceil$ labels` and
`$\beta(g+n_0+\lceil\log_p(2/\tau)\rceil, n_0; g)$`, as in `lem:fc-choice`.

**imprecision 4.3** — (a): "Suppose $\kappa = 2$, or $p \notin E_2(\kappa)$" is redundant
(E_2(2) is empty, `prop:decoupled-sde` discussion). Also "the images ... in
$\Fp^a/U_K \cong \Fp^{g}$" treats the ambient label space as F_p^a; in the recursion the ambient
