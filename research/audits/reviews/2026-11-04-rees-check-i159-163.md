### 1. lem:qft-mod and the hybrid argument in "Gate-level control"

**F1 (Imprecision, hybrid numbers).** Location: main.tex, Gate-level control,
"except that a forward transform whose input is $|0\rangle$ is charged only
$3\pi 2^{-\tau} \le \eta/(b+3)$ ... with $\eta = 1.5\cdot10^{-3}$ ... with
$\eta = 2.8\cdot10^{-4}$, a seeded run with $7$ resp.\ $8$ terminal samples
therefore agrees ... except with probability below $0.1$."
Why: the "below 0.1" holds only with the actual value of $3\pi2^{-\tau}$ at the
lemma's $\tau$ ($\tau = 15$ resp. $18$), which the text does not state. A
reader who uses the displayed inequality $3\pi2^{-\tau}\le\eta/(b+3)$ gets
$7(6 + 18/5)\cdot1.5\cdot10^{-3} = 0.1008$ and
$8(24 + 124/6)\cdot2.8\cdot10^{-4} = 0.10005$, both above $0.1$. With
$3\pi2^{-15} = 2.876\cdot10^{-4}$ and $3\pi2^{-18} = 3.595\cdot10^{-5}$ the
totals are $0.0992$ and $0.0894$ (recomputed). The artifact
(`2026-11-04-rees-qft-wire/lemma_level.json`) actually uses
$\eta = 0.1/(7\cdot 9.6) = 1.488\cdot10^{-3}$ and $2.7985\cdot10^{-4}$, of which
the paper's values are round-ups; the circuits are the same (same $t,r,\tau$).
Fix:
```latex
On the Heisenberg group over $\mathbb{F}_3$ ($24$ transforms per terminal sample, $6$ of them on inputs other than $|0\rangle$) with $\eta = 1.5\cdot10^{-3}$, hence $(t,r,\tau) = (26,19,15)$ and $3\pi2^{-\tau} < 2.9\cdot10^{-4}$, and on the class-three filiform algebra over $\mathbb{F}_5$ below ($148$ transforms, $24$ of them on inputs other than $|0\rangle$) with $\eta = 2.8\cdot10^{-4}$, hence $(t,r,\tau) = (32,22,18)$ and $3\pi2^{-\tau} < 3.6\cdot10^{-5}$, the bound per terminal sample is below $0.0142$ resp.\ $0.0112$, so the output distribution of a run with $7$ resp.\ $8$ terminal samples is within total variation distance $0.0993$ resp.\ $0.0895$ of that of the run with exact transforms.
```

**F2 (Imprecision, coupling).** Location: same sentence, "a seeded run ...
agrees with the run with exact transforms, outcome for outcome, except with
probability below $0.1$." Why: the hybrid bound controls the distance of the
final pure states, hence the total variation distance of the outcome
distributions. That gives agreement "outcome for outcome" only under a maximal
coupling, not for two runs driven by the same seed (with inverse-CDF sampling
from a common uniform, the mismatch probability can exceed the TV distance by
a factor of the number of outcomes). The code says the same thing correctly:
`coupling_bound` in `theorems/rees_circuit_qft_wire.py` speaks of "the coupled
exact run". Fix: replace by the TV statement in F1, or
```latex
... so there is a coupling of the two runs under which all $7$ resp.\ $8$ terminal samples agree except with probability below $0.1$.
```

**F3 (Imprecision, gate count).** Location: proof of lem:qft-mod, "The gate
count is the sum of the block counts: ... $E$ at most $t(40b+13)+tr$, $\mathrm{DEC}$
at most $6bt+6b^2+35b+13$ ...". Why: the stated polynomial
$328b^2+92bt+2tr+(b+5)\tau+26t+240b+60$ equals
PREP + PH + 2E + 2DEC + ADD + SW (checked coefficient by coefficient), i.e. it
counts $E$, $E^{-1}$, $\mathrm{DEC}$, $\mathrm{DEC}^{-1}$; the text says "sum of
the block counts" without saying that two blocks occur twice. Fix:
```latex
The gate count is $\mathrm{PREP} + \mathrm{PH} + 2E + 2\,\mathrm{DEC} + \mathrm{ADD} + \mathrm{SW}$, since $V$ contains $E$, $E^{-1}$, $\mathrm{DEC}$ and $\mathrm{DEC}^{-1}$:
```

No mathematical error found in lem:qft-mod itself. Checked in particular:
$\sin^2\theta = 2^b/(4N)\in(1/4,1/2)$ for odd $N$ with $2^{b-1}<N<2^b$, so
$\alpha(\theta)=\pi/6$; $d\alpha/d\vartheta\le1$ iff $\rho\le1$; the tail bound
$|\beta_\nu(l)|\le 1/(2^{t+1}|d_l|)$ and the two-sided sum
$\le (1/A+1/A^2)/2$ with $A = 2^{t-1}/N \ge 18/\eta^2$; the rounding
$\lfloor (lN+2^{t-1})/2^t\rfloor$ is the half-up nearest integer and lies in

**F7 (Gap, uniformity of the canonical constants).** Location:
lem:coherent-quadratic-sde ("$m$ the least quadratic non-residue") and
lem:coherent-power-sde ("$g$ the least integer whose image generates
$\Fp^\times/P$", "$(\alpha,\beta)$ the lexicographically least ... such
that ..."), and the coverage sentence "it makes the solve of~\eqref{eq:sde-target}
coherent at every grade with $p\notin E_2(\kappa_d)$". Why: the lemmas give gate
counts polynomial in $(a,b)$, but the gate list contains these constants, and
no classical cost of computing them is stated. The least non-residue and the
least coset generator are known to be computable deterministically in
$\poly(\log p)$ time only under GRH (unconditionally the best bound is
Burgess's $p^{1/(4\sqrt e)+o(1)}$). The lexicographically least contraction is
found in the code by a DFS over $[0,p)$ (`canonical_contraction`,
`_canonical_contraction_lazy` in `theorems/rees_coherent_sde_kappa.py`),
which costs at least order $p$ in general; Proposition~\ref{prop:decoupled-sde}
gives a polynomial sampler only for some contraction, not the least one. The
proofs never use minimality except to get $m-1\in P$ in the quadratic lemma, so
the fix is to make the constants parameters. Fix:
```latex
% lem:coherent-quadratic-sde
... $m$ a quadratic non-residue with $m-1$ a nonzero square (the least non-residue is one; a uniform element of $\Fp$ is one with probability $1/4+O(1/p)$), $\mu = m-1$, ...
% lem:coherent-power-sde
... $g$ any integer whose image generates $\Fp^\times/P$, and $(\alpha,\beta)$ any normalised universal two-level contraction (for definiteness in the artifact, the lexicographically least ...). The circuit takes $m$, $g$ and $(\alpha,\beta)$ as classical parameters; producing them costs the Las Vegas search of Proposition~\ref{prop:decoupled-sde} (a constant-size search for fixed $\kappa$ below $T_S(\kappa)$), and the gate list is then computed in time polynomial in its length.
```
(For the quadratic case the proof sketch's sentence "By minimality of $m$,
$\mu\in P$" becomes "By the choice of $m$, $\mu\in P$".)

### 3. rem:is-import(b) and lem:app-s2-gcd(b)

Correct. With $\kappa=\gcd(d,p-1)$, $d=\kappa d'$, $p-1=\kappa q'$,
$\gcd(d',q')=1$ gives $t\ge1$ with $td'\equiv1 \pmod{q'}$, hence
$td=\kappa\,td'\equiv\kappa \pmod{\kappa q'}$, i.e. $td\equiv\kappa\pmod{p-1}$. For
$u_i\ne0$, $s_i^d=u_i^{td}=u_i^{\kappa}$ by Fermat; for $u_i=0$,
$s_i=0=u_i^\kappa$ because $t\ge1$ and $\kappa\ge1$; and $s_i\ne0\iff u_i\ne0$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:is-import} applies at exponent $\kappa$ because $\kappa\mid p-1$,
and $B_\kappa(a)\le B_d(a)$ because $\kappa\le d$ and both $G(\cdot,a)$ and
$\lceil\log_2(\cdot+1)\rceil$ are nondecreasing. The appendix lemma states and
proves exactly this.
