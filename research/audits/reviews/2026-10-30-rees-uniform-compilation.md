## Findings And Disposition

**Uniformity:** Bacon concluded that the required bounded, flagged circuit
existence follows from public polynomial arithmetic, coherent arbitrary-
modulus QFT approximation and the bounded classical solver, conditional on
the existing Rees identities. Missing emitted circuits should not be called
an independent mathematical primitive. Parent added an explicit derivation,
not an `implemented` or `certified` status.

**Restricted-domain approximation:** Kant found the ideal-prefix/actual-suffix
hybrid sufficient. It does not require agreement on invalid binary strings.
Local error must be phase-sensitive and reference-stable on canonical inputs
with clean workspace. Adaptive canonical records require a uniform bound;
malformed records can abort. Canonical labels outside the hidden annihilator
cannot be recognized without hidden information and must receive the normal
bounded policy. Their exceptional mass is paid unconditionally.

**Sources and adjoints:** joint source error, not marginal error, is needed.
Clean isometry approximation supports inversion; output-only agreement
after discarding dirty work does not. Actual predecessor states need not
remain independent: the global hybrid compares ideal prefixes. None of this
asserts coset-state correctness for arbitrary correlated inputs.

**Counts:** Sagan found no actionable discrepancy. Four BCH passes per clean
in-place action are required by two compute/copy/uncompute XOR evaluators.
The Python semantic reference evaluates two abstract functions; that is not
a measured four-pass gate implementation. The ordinary source uses cL, not
2cL, oracle calls because its oracle labels are retained and public linear
evaluation workspaces are uncomputed separately.
