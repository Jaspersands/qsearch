## Decision-Changing Results

1. **A different quotient repairs the polarization algebra in genus two.**
   With H+H^-1=tI, N=t+2, eta=H+I and mu=I+H^-1, one has
   mu^dagger H mu=N and eta mu=N. K=ker(mu)=eta(B[N]) is maximal
   isotropic for the ORIGINAL model. A paid quotient by K gives the
   changed polarized model. This avoids the earlier wrong-complement map.
2. **Weak signs are not needed for this kernel.** Common masks change
   generators, not the subgroup. Coprime marking transport has an explicit
   known-source formula, retaining one common mask at each order.
3. **Fixed-mark and characteristic overlap can be removed constructively.**
   Right congruence shears give a quadratic norm expression. Two/three
   local choices followed by source-coordinate CRT avoid fixed odd primes.
   The adjustment preserves the whole joint marked polarized class.
4. **Affordability is still missing, not hidden in the formula.** The
   remaining problem is a shifted quaternion norm in a prescribed affine
   coset with an actually affordable multiplier. Six existing lifts have
   enormous full-torsion fields; pure powers of 2 or 3 do not repair this
   control. These statements do not exclude mixed-prime selectors or
   implicit-kernel methods. No uniform oracle is implemented.
5. **A source-order classical attack needs less than a target lattice.**
   For an actually computable target polarized automorphism u, reconstruct
   beta=phi^dagger*u*phi in the known SOURCE order. Its exact squared
   Rosati norm is2g*ell^2, and weak common masks cancel. The joint image
   of the reconstructed maps recovers K if it has dimension g.
6. **The attack is scoped.** With J=im(phi_ell), rank(beta_u)=
   g-dim(uJ intersect J). Several maps succeed exactly when
   J+sum u_iJ=B[ell]. No guarantee supplies such u on an arbitrary target;
   even a non-scalar commuting u gives zero information. Forward integral
   ring operations on phi*a*phi^dagger also give zero reverse information.
