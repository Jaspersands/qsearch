## 1. Controlled perturbations of joins: classical match under the stated promise

Native input would be an explicit subcomplex K of the join of r discrete q-point
sets, with efficiently decidable face membership (for example a multipartite
clique complex). Output is a relative-epsilon estimate of its top Betti number.
The proposed new operation was a perturbation-stable high-dimensional Laplacian
compiler: retain constant top-kernel mass, native top-simplex preparation and an
inverse-polynomial gap while destroying the exact product formula.

The full join has q^r top faces, (q-1)^r top Betti number and (q+1)^r faces
including the empty face. Its Laplacian is an explicit sum of local all-ones
operators; the nonzero top gap is q. At q=Theta(r) the kernel fraction and
uniform top-face source are favorable. But this alone does not select a task.

The parent's cheap classical attack is decisive whenever the perturbation retains
homology concentration at the known top degree and Beta_top >=c q^r. Draw a
uniform ambient face by independently choosing 'absent' or one of the q vertices
in each block. Let
  Y=(-1)^(number of selected vertices) * 1_(face belongs to K).
Then, including the empty face,
  E Y = (-1)^r Beta_top/(q+1)^r
under the homology-concentration premise. Therefore
  |E Y| >= c (q/(q+1))^r >= c exp(-r/q).
For q>=a r this is constant. Bounded-summand sampling estimates Beta_top to
relative error epsilon in O(c^-2 exp(2r/q) epsilon^-2 log(1/eta)) samples,
each using a native membership check. The large scaling integer (q+1)^r has
only O(r log q) bits. The spectral gap is irrelevant to this classical estimator.

Thus this family is rejected as a replacement. Distributed homology could break
the Euler argument, but no native perturbed family paying source, gap AND an
individual-Betti readout was identified. That missing family is not a reason
to start a new barrier or fixture project. The statement is scoped to the
concentrated-homology/ambient-face-budget promise, not all TDA.

## 2. Hypergraph community labels from a Kikuchi spectral state: precise lead only

Native input would be the explicitly observed p-uniform hypergraph under a
specified planted multi-community model. Output would be vertex labels with a
specified nonzero correlation, modulo the model's label symmetry. The retained
compendium §4.3 describes a detector with dominant time n^(ell/4) poly(n), versus
the classical Kikuchi n^ell route, and says general label recovery is not yet
established there. This is QUARTIC scaling, not polynomial versus superpolynomial.

A concrete proposed new operation is a one-body readout of the accepted lifted
state. On an ell-subset basis, the native reversible deletion isometry is
  |S> -> ell^(-1/2) sum_(i in S) |i>|S minus {i}>.
It uses only public sorted-subset arithmetic and a uniform position register;
it needs no unknown-state inverse. Tracing the second register yields an
n-dimensional reduced matrix, estimable with polynomially many state copies.
For the exact binary population state
  |x;ell>=binom(n,ell)^(-1/2) sum_(|S|=ell) product_(i in S)x_i |S>,
with x_i in {+1,-1}, direct counting gives
  R_ii=1/n,
  R_ij=x_i x_j (n-ell)/(n(n-1))  for i!=j.
Its planted eigenvector is x/sqrt(n), with eigenvalue (n-ell+1)/n and gap
(n-ell)/(n-1). This identity is proved for that exact state; it is NOT a theorem
about the state actually produced by a noisy multi-community algorithm.

The useful proof question would be whether every state (or the specific mixed
state) accepted by the actual spectral filter has a reduced matrix with a
robust planted community subspace, and whether the source probability and all
readout copies preserve the quoted total clock. The cheapest falsifier is an
exact population accepted subspace whose one-body matrices erase the labels,
or an accepted uninformative direction. A mere overlap with some informative
vector is insufficient without a quantitative readout theorem.

Strong classical attacks are ordinary lower-order spectral clustering, belief
propagation/nonbacktracking operators, Kikuchi/SoS methods, and the newer
classical improvements for planted XOR under their actual arity assumptions.
The binary identity alone does not open a new result: XOR recovery already has
separate literature. Generic full tomography, hidden label advice, or a presumed
constant-overlap eigenstate is not granted.

Primary gate: Schmidhuber–Zlokapa, *Quartic quantum speedups for community
detection*, arXiv:2510.08494, specifically its exact population signal space,
accepted-state interface and recovery discussion. I requested that source from
the parent, but it was not retained/read before this note was frozen. Hence
no claim that this readout is absent from that paper, or valid for its model,
is made. Even a positive result would be an output upgrade at the known runtime
scale, not by itself the requested transformative asymptotic endpoint.

## 3. Native convex-boson vacuum preparation: a source operation, not yet a selection

An independent candidate would start from a public bounded-degree interaction
graph and rational positive coefficients for
  H=-1/2 sum_i d^2/dx_i^2 +1/2 sum_i m_i^2 x_i^2
    +sum_i g_i x_i^4 +sum_(ij) k_ij (x_i-x_j)^2,
with numerical parameter bounds and m_i bounded below by inverse-polynomial
size. A proposed operation is a uniform finite Galerkin cutoff and an adiabatic
vacuum preparation justified by a convex-potential gap comparison, with all
cutoff/precision/gap costs derived from the native coefficients. This would
pay a source instead of supplying an unknown ground state. A subsequent
explicit force-quench work law would be a possible observable endpoint.

Expected quantum cost would be polynomial in the charged dimension, numerical
bounds and inverse requested accuracy IF that compiler is proved. It must not
be claimed polynomial merely in binary couplings while their magnitudes enter
the simulation norm. Actual fundamental-gap and convex-Schrodinger algorithm
sources would be needed before importing such a theorem; none was read here.
The corpus's broad annealing and tensor-network impossibility claims are
quarantined and are not used.

Strong classical attacks include log-concave Euclidean/path-integral sampling,
variational and tensor-network methods, and the exactly Gaussian/free subcase.
Ground-position or equilibrium-moment sampling might be directly classically
matched. Adding ordinary real-time simulation/QPE afterward is not by itself
a new research mechanism. Cheapest falsifiers: the source compiler is already
covered by existing convex-Schrodinger algorithms, or the chosen observable
admits a positive classical path-integral estimator with a polynomial clock.
Without a distinct paid readout and primary-source comparison, this direction
is not selected and no new preparation/barrier infrastructure is authorized.
