## 1. Target lattice and the circulant stabilizer

Let ell>=5 be prime, and let the two degree-ell fields have disjoint splitting
fields with group G=S_ell x S_ell. The compositum L has degree ell^2. Put

    T=ker(Res_(L/Q) G_m -> Res_(A/Q) G_m x Res_(B/Q) G_m).

The embedding-basis cocharacter map sends e_(i,j) to e_i+e_j. Its image is the
saturated lattice of pairs having equal total sums: the complete bipartite
incidence matrix generates it integrally. Thus the kernel is a torus, and

    K=X_*(T)={integer ell-by-ell matrices with all row/column sums zero}.

Let c cyclically shift both indices and C=<c> of order ell. Then K^C consists of
M_(i,j)=a_(j-i), sum a_r=0. Identify this with the augmentation ideal
I in Z[t]/(t^ell-1), using the COMMON PER-POINT coefficient on each diagonal orbit.

For nonzero M, distinct columns cannot coincide: their equality would make a
periodic under a nonzero translation; prime ell forces a constant, and its zero
sum then forces zero. No column can be the negative of another: iterating the
translation ell times gives a=-a. The same statements hold for rows.

Let H=Stab_G{M,-M}. Projection H->S_ell onto either factor is injective.
Indeed an element in its kernel would permute rows (or columns) to equal rows,
or to their negatives, both just excluded except for the identity. Therefore

    |H|<=ell!,        |G.{M,-M}|>=ell!.                         (1)

This controls the orbit UP TO SIGN, so no missing factor two remains. Primality
is essential for the distinct-translate argument; the theorem is not claimed
for arbitrary odd composite degrees.

## 2. Arbitrary auxiliary splitting fields and local traces

Let P be a signed-permutation cocharacter lattice of rank D, and let
f:P->K be the equivariant map of a Q-torus homomorphism. Work with the absolute
Galois group, or with a finite common splitting field. Its action on K factors
through G, but its action on P need not. If e is a signed basis vector and w=f(e),
then every image of w up to sign comes from one of the D basis LINES. Hence

    |G.{w,-w}|<=D.                                           (2)

Surjectivity of the absolute Galois action onto G pays this assertion even when
auxiliary fields are not contained in the target normal closure. From (1),(2),
if D<ell! and w!=0, then w cannot lie in K^C. Its C-orbit has length ell.

Choose a rational prime unramified in all source/target splitting fields, with
target Frobenius a pair of ell-cycles, conjugated to c. Let h be its full source
Frobenius; its order can exceed ell. Unramified torus valuations are exactly
cocharacter invariants, and the homomorphism acts on them by f. A positive-sign
h-cycle of basis lines, of length m, has invariant generator sum_(j<m) h^j e.
Its image is sum_(j<m)c^j w. Since c^m w=w, nonzero w forces ell|m; the image is
(m/ell) N_C(w). A negative-sign cycle has no invariant over Z. This accounts for
ALL source local valuations, without pretending that h itself has order ell.

For several sources, the same argument applies to their direct sum of total
rank D; integer exponents and combinations do not escape the resulting subgroup.

The lattice K is generated by elementary rectangles. Tracing a rectangle gives
 t^r(1-t^a)(1-t^b), up to a sign and a unit t^j, in the per-point identification.
These generate exactly I^2, since a=b=1 gives a unit multiple of (t-1)^2. Hence

    sum source valuation images subset N_C(K)=I^2,
    K^C/I^2 = I/I^2 = Z/ell.                                (3)

The quotient is detected by sum r*a_r mod ell; t-1 has nonzero class. This is
integral failure, not a rational span defect. Saturation or an uncharged ellth
root is not an available homomorphic transfer.

## 3. Exact small-coefficient S_ell x S_ell field family

Choose primes ell<r<2ell and 2ell<s<4ell. Coefficientwise CRT modulo 2*r*s^2
constructs two monic degree-ell polynomials f_A,f_B satisfying:
- each is irreducible modulo2;
- modulo r, each is a squarefree product of one irreducible quadratic and ell-2
  distinct linear factors;
- f_A splits squarefreely modulo s;
- f_B congruent T^2 G(T)+s modulo s^2, G(T)=product_(i=1)^(ell-2)(T-i).
All ingredients exist since r,s>ell; CRT representatives have O(log ell) bits.
An irreducible binary polynomial can also be found by bounded randomized trials
and certified by finite-field gcd/powering tests; irreducible density is
(2^ell-2)/(ell*2^ell). No splitting field is computed in this construction.

Irreducibility modulo2 makes both fields degree ell and their Galois actions
transitive. Prime degree makes the actions primitive. The factorization at r
supplies a transposition. The graph formed by all conjugate transpositions has
components forming a block system; primitivity makes it connected, and its edge
transpositions generate S_ell. Thus both Galois groups are exactly S_ell.

The valuation-one discriminant assertion at s is exact. Modulo s, f_B has one
double root at0 and otherwise simple roots. Hensel factorization over Z_s writes
it as (T^2+uT+v) times distinct lifted linear factors, with u in s Z_s,
v_s(v)=1: evaluate at0 and use G(0) a unit and f_B(0) congruent s mod s^2.
Thus v_s(u^2-4v)=1, while all other discriminants and pairwise resultants are
units. Therefore v_s(disc f_B)=1. For f_A that valuation is zero. The index-square
formula also shows the corresponding field discriminant valuations are 1 and0.

Their sign quadratic fields Q(sqrt(disc f_A)), Q(sqrt(disc f_B)) are distinct,
because only the latter ramifies at s. For ell>=5, the only nontrivial proper
quotient of S_ell is C2. The intersection of two S_ell splitting fields is Galois
and gives a common quotient; a nontrivial intersection would force their sign
fields equal (also if the two entire splitting fields coincided). Thus their
intersection is Q and the joint group is S_ell x S_ell.

## 4. The supplied expanded input really can have polynomial size

With coefficient height H=O(ell^3), all roots have modulus<=1+H. The elementary
root-difference discriminant bound gives log|disc f_i|=O(ell^2 log ell).
For R=Z[alpha,beta] in L, the tensor trace pairing gives

    disc R=(disc f_A)^ell (disc f_B)^ell,
    log|disc R|=O(ell^3 log ell).

If O is the maximal order and q=[O:R], then 2 log q<=log|disc R|. Since qO
is a lattice between qR and R, its HNF basis has entries bounded by q; dividing
that basis by q gives O-basis coordinates with polynomially many bits. Polynomial
size here counts the full degree ell^2, all table entries and all coefficients.
Transforming the tensor power-basis multiplication table and the A,B embeddings
by these rational matrices has polynomial bit cost/height by determinant bounds.
The same argument applies to the two individual maximal orders.

This proves existence of legal supplied maximal-order tables/embeddings of size
poly(ell); it does not smuggle in a deterministic polynomial maximal-order
construction claim. The native contract SUPPLIES and charges these tables.
Targets 1,1 already have constant size. Thus ell! is superpolynomial in this
family's original expanded input size, not just in a secretly tiny degree label.

Chebotarev gives infinitely many pair-of-ell-cycle primes, outside any finite S
and all ramification of any fixed finite source family. Only qualitative
existence is used: no GRH, effective prime bound or prime-search algorithm is
needed for the obstruction.

## 5. Single-relative-norm sources and exact scope

For U=Res_(F/Q) Res^1_(E/F) G_m, all paired norm quotients from the field factors
of E tensor_F E land in U. Their total field degree is [E:Q]^2/[F:Q]. At an
unramified F-prime with E residue degrees f_i, their valuation vectors are
 (f_j/gcd(f_i,f_j))e_i-(f_i/gcd(f_i,f_j))e_j.
They generate the FULL integer kernel of (f_i): put h_i=f_i/gcd_j f_j,
choose integers b_i with sum b_i h_i=1, and write any kernel z as
sum_(i,j)b_i z_j(h_i e_j-h_j e_i). Each summand is an integer multiple of one
primitive pair vector. This is local generation, not a global Hilbert-90 oracle.

After composing with U->T, these field transfers generate exactly the same local
valuation image as U. Therefore, for any family of such sources, completeness
requires sum_i [E_i:Q]^2/[F_i:Q]>=ell!. In particular sum_i[E_i:Q]>=sqrt(ell!).
Do NOT upgrade this to an ell! lower bound on the original norm-one source degree.
It still excludes polynomial total expanded degree.
