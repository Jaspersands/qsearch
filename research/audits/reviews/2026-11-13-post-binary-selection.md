## 1. Full-ratio Kronecker: complete quantum endpoint, selected subcase already matched

Input: explicit partitions lambda,mu,nu of numerical n, fixed K, eta>0, with
R=f_mu f_nu/f_lambda<=n^K; output the exact integer g with error<=eta. Reorder to
choose the largest-dimensional target when useful. Dimensions and R are public
hook-formula computations. No hidden subgroup, tableau oracle, unknown carrier
state or coherent multiplicity basis is supplied.

Actual primary source checked: Larocca–Havlicek2407.17649v5 Theorem1/equation10,
and equation14/LF247–255: native irrep preparation and weak Fourier measurement
have probability g/R and an O(n^4 R^2) constant-error gate clock. Confidence
repetition is charged. This source theorem is not a fresh finite-gate audit here.
Panova2502.20253v2 Theorem1.1 matches polynomial minimum dimension; Conjecture1.3
and Question2 distinguish the whole polynomial-R regime. Fixed Durfee is now
additionally matched by the reviewed numerical and compact-input compilers.

External value: exact representation-theoretic counting with a native known quantum
upper bound. A genuine unmatched polynomial-R family would merit scrutiny, but
neither unmatched status nor the source's open question establishes classical
hardness. Generic character enumeration and all small-dimension, fixed-row,
fixed-Durfee and near-row algorithms must be charged on the SAME input.

The earlier positive family mu=nu=(n-k,k), lambda=(n-2k,k,k), n=k^3 has g=1 and
R=O(k). It is EASY, and only falsifies the proposed exponential ratio-to-tail
inequality. It is not an algorithmic advantage example. Large near-row padding
also forbids inferring fixed Durfee from R without a theorem; the proposed
square-tail Cartan family still needs its own finite-n stability check.

### Decisive stronger baseline: do not build the proposed LR sampler

The initially considered operation was a native classical induction sampler:
uniform permutations conditioned on RSK shapes alpha,beta, uniform allocation of
labels to two blocks, concatenation, then RSK. The candidate output law is

    Pr[Gamma=gamma] = c_(alpha,beta)^gamma f_gamma /
                       (binom(a+b,a) f_alpha f_beta).

It has a clean prospective tableau-count proof, but it is NOT selected. No sampler
was implemented. The actual Panova text LF783–791 states Theorem4.8 [Ike16]:

    LR coefficient c can be computed in O(c^2 poly(n)) time.

Thus EVERY native promised family for which ordinary g equals an LR coefficient c
is already classically polynomial whenever R<=n^K, because 0<=g<=R follows directly
from g/R being a probability. No induction-ratio comparison is required. In
particular this covers the stable top-tail-degree family, once its finite-n LR
identity is paid, for arbitrary growing Durfee and arbitrary c, not just c=1.
The retained Ikenmeyer–Panova2024 `.../kronecker-ratio/reduced.txt` LF71–80 states
the stable LR equality; a limit statement alone is not a finite threshold.

The quotation of Panova is verified. The parent is checking the actual Ikenmeyer2016
algorithm, including zero outputs, stopping and native bit/input conventions;
this review does not pretend that citing a later theorem substitutes for that
backend check. It nevertheless removes any reason to build a new sampler first.
If the original source exposes a precise missing premise, reassess that premise,
not an unrestricted new sampler. No novelty claim about the proposed law survives.

Full-ratio inputs beyond LR reductions remain open. A worthwhile new operation

## 2. Class-two exponent-p group isomorphism: higher upside, unpaid common-frame step

Native input: an odd prime p and two public alternating maps beta,beta':
F_p^r x F_p^r -> F_p^m, given by coefficient tensors; assume surjective maps
with zero common radical. Output A in GL_r and D in GL_m satisfying beta'(Ax,Ay)=D beta(x,y), or NO. The identity is directly
verifiable. This is a group-isomorphism/transporter problem, not a supplied
hidden-subgroup or secret-evaluation oracle. Its algebraic and cryptographic
motivation is independent of Kronecker coefficient estimation.

Explicit constructive mechanism to examine: compile commutator phases
omega^(s.beta(x,y)), Fourier sample the central coordinate, coherently reduce each
scalar alternating form to symplectic normal form, and align the frames to recover
ONE common A,D. Phase evaluation and individual reductions are native polynomial
operations. The new common-frame alignment is missing. It must include automorphism
ambiguities, both YES/NO cases, success/error amplification and a full
poly(r,m,log p,log(1/eta)) bit/gate clock; a list of ranks is not a decoder.
A complete such algorithm would have much greater potential reach than another
restricted multiplicity subcase, but no new alignment operation is supplied here.

Cheapest decisive test for a rank-only proposal: for irreducible degree-j f over
F_p, use the alternating pencil with cross block uI+v C_f, where C_f is its
companion matrix. Every nonzero member has full rank, while its Pfaffian is
(up to sign) det(uI+v C_f). Inequivalent root sets under PGL_2 give inequivalent
pencils with identical rank profiles. Such pairs exist already for p=3,j=6:
there are 116 monic irreducibles but |PGL_2(F_3)|=24. Thus individual-form ranks
cannot determine a common congruence even in a structured pencil subcase.
This does not rule out richer coherent data or general quantum algorithms.

Applicable classical comparisons include matrix-pencil canonical forms and
simultaneous isometry through adjoint/*-algebras once the output-coordinate change
D is fixed. Actual uniform hypotheses/clocks of Ivanyos–Qiao's *-algebra work
must be read before a claimed comparison; no numerical best exponent is asserted.
The corpus's unrelated broad HSP obstructions are not imported. A source/result
that would change this selection is an explicit native common-frame procedure
that survives the pencil test and is not already supplied by these classical
methods. Without it, commissioning a generic group-Fourier wrapper is premature.

## 3. Many-body operator spreading: meaningful endpoint, known simulation mechanism

Input: an explicit bounded-local Hamiltonian H on N qubits, numerical time t,
Pauli W, epsilon. Output a Pauli label P, or its weight, with probability |c_P|^2,
where exp(iHt)W exp(-iHt)=sum_P c_P P. Infinite-temperature normalization is one.
Bell pairs, evolution of one half by that operator, and Bell measurement give the
full law directly: the Bell amplitude is 2^(-N)Tr(P^* exp(iHt)W exp(-iHt)).
Source preparation, both time directions, numerical arithmetic and readout are
paid by polynomial-time digital simulation for numerical polynomial t. There is
no ground-state, thermal, postselection or exponentially small signal premise.

The retained compendium LF1731–1746 identifies external operator-growth motivation
and cites Qi–Davis–Periwal–Schleier-Smith1906.00524. That primary text was not newly
read; no experimental or hardness conclusion is imported. Best comparisons must
include tensor networks, Pauli-path methods, solvable models and ensemble-averaged
Markov laws, with fixed-realization and ensemble outputs kept distinct.

A possible new operation would be a rigorously controlled interference-aware
compression of the doubled evolution that changes attainable times or readout
accuracy. No such compiler is constructed here. A sign-free gatewise Pauli-path
replacement already fails on a Pauli rotation by pi/4 followed by its inverse:
the unitary cancels, while dephasing between the two gates generally does not.
For generator X_1 X_2 and initial Z_1, the path model gives weights 1 and 2
each with probability 1/2, whereas the exact output has weight 1 certainly. Those inputs
are classically easy; the test is not evidence of hardness.

The Bell/Choi endpoint is a known complete simulation construction, not a new
algorithmic mechanism. Merely relabeling it as a useful OTOC or operator-size task
fails the requested selection criterion. A concrete physically motivated family,
new compression/readout operation and matched classical analysis would change
that decision; no generic source-preparation or gap pass should be launched.
