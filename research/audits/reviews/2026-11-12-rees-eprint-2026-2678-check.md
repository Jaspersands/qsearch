# Priority check: IACR ePrint 2026/2678 (HSP survey), 2026-11-12

**Status:** AI-read, pending human review. The owner downloaded the PDF by hand. It is not in the repo (third-party text), and nothing from it is reproduced beyond short locators.

**Source.** F. Di Concilio, R. Flores, D. Kahrobaei, M. Noce, "From Quantum Mechanics to the Hidden Subgroup Problem: an Overview from Finite to Infinite Groups", Cryptology ePrint Archive 2026/2678. The PDF was created on 29 Oct 2026 and has 34 pp. The whole text was read via pdftotext.

## Findings

1. **Nilpotent groups (§8.5).** The survey cites only Ivanyos–Sanselme–Santha: class two, plus the classical reduction to exponent p with a trivial or cyclic hidden subgroup. It also cites Radhakrishnan–Rötteler–Sen for the Heisenberg group of order p³. It gives no algorithm for class ≥ 3. It does not cite Imran–Ivanyos 2023 (arXiv:2304.08376); it cites only their 2022 exact abelian paper (§7.1). This is consistent with "no prior polynomial-time algorithm for arbitrary H at fixed class ≥ 3 with p growing", but it is weak evidence, since the survey misses II 2023.
2. **Infinite groups (§9).** The survey covers only Kuperberg 2025: NP-completeness of HSEP in Q/Z and in F_k, HSP in Z^k, and virtually abelian groups. It has no result on finite-index hidden subgroups of UT_n(Z) or of other non-abelian nilpotent infinite groups. So it raises no priority conflict for Corollary cor:utz.
3. **Open problems (§10, item 5).** "HSP in infinite non-abelian groups": the survey says it is open whether the HSP in the discrete Heisenberg group H_3(Z) can be solved efficiently. The scope is not stated, so it presumably includes infinite-index H. For finite-index H the problem reduces to the following:
   - the abelian period reduction;
   - generation of Γ(M) by the x_ij^M;
   - ISS 2012 class two.

   This is the content of rem:utz-scope(b). The combination was not found stated anywhere.
4. **Nothing else relevant:** no Lazard, no Rees/cover, no zero-sum sequences, no Lie-algebra methods.
