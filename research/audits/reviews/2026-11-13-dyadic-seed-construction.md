# Constructive dyadic seed step: coupled three-move BONG compiler

13 November 2026. STATE/TRAPS, the native ternary note, the retained spinor
source audit, actual Beli classification and Dubey--Holenstein were read.
No network, API, Git, or tests. This gives actual coordinate moves and a
polynomial search for two specified three-move words. It does NOT prove
that these words suffice for every ramified ternary lattice. A complete
case and the exact remaining condition are stated below; no all-input
seed or integral-isometry theorem is promoted.

## 1. Fixed interfaces and actual source scope

Let F=K_P, O its valuation ring, v(pi)=1, e=v(2), residue degree f. All
field/ring arithmetic and precision are charged in the expanded native
input. A,B are nonsingular integral ternary Gram matrices known locally
isometric. Good BONGs give orthogonal vectors with norms a_i and b_i and
equal valuations R_i; R_1<=R_3. Their construction and local Hilbert/
squareclass linear algebra are paid by the 7-November spinor-source audit.
Use the previously paid local square-root backend for actual square units.

Beli classification, LF980–998, gives the BINARY move criterion

    (a,b) -> (eta*a,eta*b),  eta in g(b/a),
    g(b/a)=U_alpha intersect N(−ab),
    alpha=min(e+(v(b)-v(a))/2, v(b)-v(a)+d(−ab)).       (1)

Here U_r is the subgroup of UNIT squareclasses of defect >=r; for r<=0
it means all unit classes. Beli LF107–109 permits replacing an adjacent
sub-BONG in the whole good BONG. It does not assert that the pair is an
orthogonal lattice summand. Theorem 3.1 is a decision criterion. The tail
LF1049–1069 says binary words connect isometric good BONGs when the residue
field has more than two elements, but supplies no polynomial word bound.
Its residue-F2 counterexample is rank FOUR, not a ternary counterexample.
Dubey--Holenstein LF67–76 supplies polynomial coordinate canonicalization
over Z/p^k, not the growing-degree completion F. Its finite mod-8 tables
are not used as tables for ramified F.

## 2. Reconstruct and change the actual adjacent binary lattice

For orthogonal BONG vectors x,y with norms a,b, the binary lattice is

    L_xy = O*x + O*(c*x+y),
    2c in O,   c^2+b/a in O.                           (2)

If v(b/a)>=0 take c=0. Otherwise BONG validity implies v(b/a)=−2m,
0<m<=e, and d(−pi^(2m)b/a)>=2m. Put beta=−pi^(2m)b/a. Express its
squareclass as a product w of the explicit principal-unit generators of
depth >=2m, using the Hilbert-pairing linear algebra. Then w=1 mod pi^(2m)
and beta/w is an actual local square. A square root, truncated to sufficient
precision and lifted to O, gives c0^2=beta mod pi^(2m). Set c=pi^−m*c0.
This proves (2) constructively without assuming a defect witness.
If c,c' both satisfy (2), their difference is integral: otherwise
v(c−c')<0 while 2c' is integral, so v(c+c')=v(c−c'), contradicting
(c−c')(c+c') in O. Thus (2) is independent of the choice of c modulo O.
It has norm generator x and projection O*y, hence is the binary BONG lattice.

In the basis x,z=c*x+y, write T=2c and N=c^2+b/a, both integral. The
quadratic ORDER

    R=O[theta],  theta^2−T*theta+N=0

has the exact identity q(p*x+q*z)/a=Norm_R/F(p+q*theta). It need not be
maximal. A norm preimage in R^* therefore realizes a primitive norm
generator; a field norm preimage outside this order would not suffice.

Here is a polynomial explicit unit-squareclass generating list for R.
For j=1,...,2e and a lifted F2-basis beta_l of the residue field, use

    1+pi^j*beta_l,   1+pi^j*beta_l*theta.               (3)

If R/piR is a separable quadratic field or a split algebra, its unit group
has odd order, so all residue units are squares. If it is the dual-number
algebra k[epsilon]/epsilon^2, additionally use 1+beta_l*epsilon_lift.
These are all units. The residue-algebra case is found by degree-two
finite-field arithmetic; no residue enumeration or discrete logarithm
is required. The successive principal-unit quotients are generated by
the additive images of (3). Also 1+pi^(2e+1)R consists of squares INSIDE R:
the convergent binomial series for sqrt(1+u) has integral R coefficients
when v(u)>2e. Completeness and finite freeness of R justify convergence.
Thus O(ef) elements generate R^*/R^*2, without a maximal-order oracle.

For eta in (1), its class belongs to the norm image of R^*. Compute the
norms of (3) and the possible extra generators, solve for their product
u by Hilbert fingerprints over F2, and write Norm(u)/eta=z0^2 with z0 a
unit of O. Then u/z0 has EXACT local norm eta. No large exponent product
is expanded. This constructs a primitive v in L_xy with q(v)=a*eta.
Extend v to an O-basis (v,w0), of determinant d0 in O^*. Its projected
second vector has norm b*d0^2/eta. Replace that vector by eta/d0 times
it: its norm is b*eta. The resulting orthogonal vectors are a new BONG
for the SAME binary lattice and realize (1) in actual coordinates.
Embed this replacement by Beli's sub-BONG lemma, leaving other orthogonal
vectors fixed. This is not an assumed direct-summand congruence.

## 3. Coupled word search is affine linear algebra plus one avoidance test

Work additively over F2 for squareclass coordinates, but write products
below multiplicatively. Let <u,v> be the Hilbert symbol bit, d the defect,
V the unit squareclass space, and put

    C=−a1*a2, D=−a2*a3, u=R2−R1, v=R3−R2,
    x=b1/a1, z=b3/a3,
    h(q)=min(e+u/2, u+d(C*q)),
    k(p)=min(e+v/2, v+d(D*p)).

The target ratios x,z are units. Determinant matching determines the
middle ratio modulo squares. Equality of rational ternary Hasse invariants
is exactly

    <C,x> + <D,z> + <x,z> = 0.                        (4)

This follows by expanding the three pair Hilbert symbols of
(a1*x,a2*x*z,a3*z), using <t,t>=<−1,t>.
Consider the actual word

    23(t), 12(x), 23(z/t).                             (5)

Its final norm tuple is (b1,a2*x*z,b3), so one final unit square-root
rescaling of the middle vector gives exactly (b1,b2,b3). The first and
last vertical groups are fixed, because the ratio within a scaled pair
is unchanged. Define the affine set

    S = {t in U_k(1) intersect z*U_k(x):
                   <D,t>=0,  <x,t>=<C,x>}.            (6)

By (4), the remaining last-move Hilbert condition is AUTOMATIC. The middle
move's only untested condition is d(x)>=h(t). If d(x)>=e+u/2 it is
automatic. Otherwise, putting r=d(x)−u, it requires d(C*t)<=r. If r<0
there is no solution. If r>=0 it excludes precisely

    S intersect C*U_(r+1).                             (7)

An odd-valuation C makes this forbidden coset disjoint from V. All
filtrations in (6)–(7) have already computed F2 bases. Solve (6); if its
base point lies in (7), a direction-basis vector escaping the underlying
subspace gives a valid t. If every direction stays inside, (5) is
impossible. This is exact polynomial linear algebra, not random trials
or enumeration of 2^f residues / 2^(ef+2) squareclasses.

Also test the symmetric word 12(t),23(z),12(x/t). Its affine set is
U_h(1) intersect x*U_h(z), with <C,t>=0 and <z,t>=<D,z>.
If d(z)<e+v/2, exclude D*U_(d(z)−v+1), with the same negative-threshold
convention. An accepted word is realized by Section 2, not just certified
at the squareclass level.

## 4. Complete case, finite precision, and the remaining all-input question

For e=1 and R1=R2=R3, every h(q),k(p) in these words is 1 and U_1=V.
There is no defect-avoidance condition. The two equations in (6) are
always consistent. If <x,−> vanishes on V, then <C,x>=0. If it equals
<D,−> on V, (4) gives <C,x>=0 since z is a unit. These are the only
possible linear dependencies over F2. Thus (5) is a COMPLETE constructive
transporter in this case, uniformly in growing residue degree f.

For general ramification, Beli's classification does at least force one
of the two FILTRATION intersections to be nonempty. Indeed its rank-three
invariants are alpha1=min(h(1),u+k(1)) and alpha2=min(k(1),v+h(1)), with
the same formulas h(z),k(x) at the target. If d(z)<min(k(1),k(x)), then
alpha2=v+h(1)=v+h(z), so h(1)=h(z); u+v>=0 forces alpha1=h(1), and
d(x)>=alpha1 makes the horizontal intersection nonempty. This does NOT
prove simultaneous Hilbert feasibility and escape from (7). The precise
remaining claim is that at least one complete test succeeds, or that a
uniform polynomial number of additional coupled moves always suffices.
No proof of either statement for all ramified ternaries is supplied here.

All accepted-word operations have polynomial bit cost in e,f, the input
valuations, and requested output precision. Ring generators use only
O(ef) factors; square-root guards and BONG-coordinate denominators are
paid explicitly from their valuations. Carry precision M plus these
polynomial guards, convert the final BONG map back to the original bases,
and verify det X is a unit and X^T A X=B modulo pi^M. For
M=s_B+2e+3 the retained inverse-square-root verifier lifts this to an
integral isometry and extracts the proper relative spinor offset. Failed
word search is UNKNOWN, not a nonisometry decision. No general seed is
obtained merely from classification, connectivity, or this successful case.
