cross-review of the useful deductions. These are AI reviews, not external

## Results And Decisions

**Einstein: allowed affine flats.** The existing scalar-normalization
method already replaces blind flat search with linear equations, but
enumerates2^d points and has only a logarithmic-dimension guarantee. Do
not describe that existing algorithm as executing its much larger proof
union bound. A new full-degree trace-plane construction makes the existing
eK-zero subfield SEARCH method deterministic for n=e*m,e>=3,2^m>=e.
Hubble independently checked the algebra, generation cost and transversal
argument. Parent reviewed the proof; implementation remains a mechanical
handoff in INVERSE_TRACE_SUBFIELD_DERANDOMIZATION.md. It does not give
target-one flats, a prime-degree solver or uniform native sampling.

**Pasteur: residual sampler.** Independently verified the improved
support-sensitive variance argument below. Derived a global binary-hash
heat bath with a uniform minorization, rather than assuming mixing.
Its missing primitive is complete enumeration of random native hash cells,
including empty cells and worst-anchor cost. Full-rank hashes still leave
K Boolean variables; exhaustive enumeration is2^K and does not beat
accepted Prange. Preserve this as a precise solver target, not a working
sampler. Popper and parent independently checked the saved proof, including
every-anchor runtime, deficient hashes and the1/epsilon total clock;26
focused tests passed in both runs. After nine added capped-initialization
controls, parent reran all35 successfully. See INVERSE_TRACE_HASH_HEATBATH.md.

**Hubble: quantum output law.** Rechecked that the ideal fixed-padding
full-list receiver is exactly uniform AFTER native verification; the
earlier singleton receiver is only approximately uniform. Its finite F8
singleton control has TV126/1885 while the full-list control has TV0.
Quantum source preparation, complete coherent lists, cleanup, clocks and
physical error accounting remain required. No native-scale quantum
receiver or practical advantage is newly established by this audit.

**Chandrasekhar: primary literature and counting.** The bounded search
found no verified same-input classical polynomial sampler, not evidence
that one cannot exist. In particular, [Marwaha et al., Theorem24](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14443v2#S4)
uses an NP oracle; an efficient verifier and approximate total count do
not instantiate it. MDS product bounds in [Horinaga--Yamakawa, Theorem3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14650v2)
motivated the elementary support-sensitive variance deduction. Parent
reopened both primary statements. Existing OPI search/oracle theorems
must not be relabeled uniform sampling of these fixed explicit predicates.

The new variance bound is

    v_j <= [sum_(t=0)^(K-1) binom(j,t)] beta^(2(j-K)),
    beta=min(1,(1+2*ceil(sqrt(q)))/q).

It reduces a sufficient residual surplus from ceil(4K/n) to
ceil(3K/(2n)), with exact outward budgets. Chandrasekhar implemented the
public rational bound and independent histogram/spectrum tests; Pasteur
and parent checked the proof. At n17/31/61, the free-field-variable counts
become4/6/12 instead of9/16/31. The missing residual sampling algorithm
is not supplied by this smaller dimension. See the dated addition to
INVERSE_TRACE_COMPLETION_TRANSFER.md.

**Mendel: Argon/Rees disposition and next application.** Confirmed that
Argon's formal promotion, normalization and exposition requests are
completed or superseded by the later receiver/SDE work. Do not repeat
packet identities or treat sufficient SDE arity as a lower bound. The
main scientific application question remains an explicit native problem
that survives classical attacks; succinct circuit stabilizers without
degree/orbit-rank promises are one such unresolved interface.

Mendel supplied this additional method-level falsifier: for prime p>3,
F=(x-z)^(p-1)(y-z)^(p-1) has the diagonal translation period, but the sum
of coordinate truncated finite logarithms applied to F equals
sum_(a=1)^(p-1)(x-z)^(p-1-a)(y-z)^(a-1), which is nonzero. Individual
(T_i-I)^p=0 does not make log(T_i*T_j)=log(T_i)+log(T_j). Parent checked
the mechanism and independently replayed the polynomial identity with
exact modular coefficients at p3,5,7,11, not a new production artifact. This easy
factored family falsifies a method, not the classical stabilizer problem.

**Popper: nonlocal sampling and adversarial review.** Independently
checked parent's menu-refresh proof, code and exact finite controls.
Uniform anchored independent menus are reversible but asymptotically
immobile for polynomial menu size. Uniform unanchored menus followed by
uniform fiber selection are biased. Review improved the exact correction
envelope to Z/L^(N-K); its success(1+e)(L/q)^K remains exponential.
All screened raw draws and ABORT must remain charged. See
INVERSE_TRACE_MENU_REFRESH.md. No defect was found in that stated scope.

Popper also derived an ideal overlapping-large-block coupling: with a
3K-coordinate shared reservoir whose all-syndrome count error is eta<1,
two alternating conditional heat baths coalesce with probability at
least(1-eta)/(1+eta). Random-scan two-step blocks therefore contract by
at most1-(1-eta)/(2(1+eta)). Parent checked the minorization argument.
The conditional update implementation is still missing; the direct exact
implementation again costs about2^K. This is not an efficient sampler.

## Resulting Priority

Stop treating validity, varied witnesses, stationary laws or small expected
hash cells as sampling algorithms. The useful next experiment is a paid,
complete solver for the explicitly reduced native hash cells, with empty
cells, all costs, capped failures and exact small-instance distribution
controls. Compare it to the already exact accepted Prange sampler.
The deterministic subfield construction is suitable for the separate
mechanical implementation agent. More receiver wiring or another minor
counting improvement is lower priority absent an application or solver.
