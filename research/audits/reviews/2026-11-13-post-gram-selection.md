## 1. Ordinary-sample LWE recovery: highest cryptographic upside, no operation

**Native input/output.** Polynomially many ordinary classical samples
(A,b=A*s+e modq), q polynomial in n, secret s in Z_q^n and independent
specified discrete-Gaussian errors of width sigma. A representative
nontrivial regime has sigma~sqrt(n), far from noiseless linear algebra.
Output s with bounded failure, using poly(n,logq,sigma,log1/eta) quantum
time and only the supplied sample table. No quantum-example oracle, fresh
chosen rows, trapdoor, Gaussian-preimage oracle or short lattice basis.
This would have direct cryptographic significance if proved in a standard
nontrivial parameter regime; no hardness assumption is used as an algorithm.

**Route screened.** Reversibly compute residuals b-A*s, attach a Gaussian
likelihood flag, Fourier-transform the secret/error registers and try to
remove the error without revealing s. This specifies operations one can
write, but supplies no new error-cancellation map. The exact acceptance
mass of the naive likelihood stage is

    Z = q^(-n) * sum_s w(b-A*s),  0<=w<=1.

When a sharp filter leaves one dominant candidate, Z is of order q^-n
(or smaller after per-row normalization). Conditioning on its success and
calling the posterior state “available” therefore hides exponential work.
Amplitude amplification pays Z^-1/2. This is a failure of that proposed
route, not a lower bound for native LWE algorithms.

A superposition over the polynomial supplied sample INDEX is not the
quantum-example state over all a in Z_q^n. Random linear combinations also
reuse the same original error vector: new rows need not have independent
Gaussian errors. The corpus already records that exact amplified-error
independence failure and native RLWE single-witness targets. They must not
be repackaged as a fresh receiver.

**Strong classical comparison.** Primal/dual lattice attacks, BKZ/sieving,
BKW/FFT sample combination, hybrid coordinate guessing and information-set
methods; LLL/Babai only under its actual decoding-radius hypothesis. Compare
the same q,sigma,m and original sample access, not only uniform exhaustive
search or Prange. No unconditional exponential classical lower bound follows.

**Decisive gate.** An explicit costed map must eliminate the Z factor or
produce verified s through a different channel, without importing coherent
examples. Merely requesting a polynomial Gaussian posterior/preimage state
is essentially restating decoding. No such new operation was found here.
Do not ask Claude to “solve LWE” from this nonconstruction.

**Primary fetch before reconsideration.** Regev, *On lattices, learning with
errors, random linear codes, and cryptography* (STOC2005/JACM2009), actual
noise/access reductions; Grilo--Kerenidis--Zijlstra, *Learning-with-errors
problem is easy with quantum samples*, actual quantum-sample input theorem.
Use these to specify the mismatch, not to infer an ordinary-sample algorithm.
No directly matching actual primary statement was located in the bounded
local source-file search, so no theorem from those papers was imported.

## 2. Native doped Hubbard preparation: high materials value, preparation unpaid

**Native input/output.** A charged finite two-dimensional repulsive Hubbard
Hamiltonian, explicit rational hopping/interactions/chemical potentials,
particle sector and precision. Seek a low-energy state and certified energy
within epsilon, for physically specified doped families. A useful ambitious
target is poly(inputbits,system size,1/epsilon), or poly in an explicitly
promised inverse gap, WITHOUT supplied ground-state overlap, imaginary-time
oracle, gapped interpolation or useful variational ansatz.

This would enable strongly correlated materials calculations beyond small
instances. It is independent of the known integrable folded-chain or Kerr
samplers: the missing task is interacting-state preparation from native
Hamiltonian data, not a new observable of an already prepared state.

**Route screened.** Block-encode H and apply a ground-energy filter or
imaginary-time polynomial, perhaps followed by local quasiadiabatic or
Lindblad continuation. Hamiltonian simulation/filter evaluation is not the
missing mathematical operation. The filter's conditional success depends
on the initial ground overlap; continuation depends on an actual path or
mixing theorem. Neither follows from a final Hamiltonian gap.

A concrete inference falsifier is the local product Hamiltonian
sum_i(I-X_i)/2: its gap is1 but a computational-basis trial has squared
ground overlap2^-n. Its ground state is EASY to prepare by Hadamards, so
this example is neither Hubbard hardness nor a general preparation no-go;
it shows exactly why final gap plus an arbitrary trial cannot pay filtering.
No corresponding native interacting-state overlap/reset operation was found.

**Strong classical comparison.** DMRG/MPS on narrow cylinders, PEPS and other
tensor methods, determinant/auxiliary-field Monte Carlo where sign structure
allows it, perturbative/strong-coupling methods, and exact diagonalization
on the same finite input. Benchmarks must use matching energy/local-observable
accuracy; a sign problem in one method is not classical hardness. General
local-Hamiltonian complexity results must not be asserted for a narrower
uniform Hubbard family without their actual model reduction.

**Decisive gate.** Exhibit a native family with a NEW computable preparation
or continuation operation and prove its overlap/mixing/branch clock, before
optimizing QPE or gate synthesis. A gap promise in place of that construction
would be another conditional interface, so no paid task is recommended.

**Primary fetch before reconsideration.** Lin--Tong, *Near-optimal ground
state preparation*, Quantum4:372(2020), actual overlap/gap hypotheses;
Ding--Zhan--Preskill--Lin, *Simple and efficient end-to-end quantum thermal
and ground state preparation*, Nature Physics2026, exact end-to-end input,
Lindbladian/preparation and complexity assumptions; Schuch--Verstraete,
*Computational complexity of interacting electrons and fundamental
limitations of density functional theory*, actual fields/geometry reduction.
The retained compendium LF4287 and4295--4296 gives the first two precise
bibliographic targets, NOT their theorem assumptions. No missing theorem
was assumed from that title or overview.

## 3. Native high-dimensional homology: output significance and spectral mass

**Native input/output.** An explicit point cloud/rational distance threshold
or an explicit graph defining its flag complex, a possibly growing dimension
k, and a raw question such as beta_k=0 versus beta_k>0, or beta_k to fixed
additive error. One bit is an adequate output contract; do not require an
exponentially long cycle vector unless a compact representation is defined.
Target poly(vertex/distance input,k,precision), without a simplex-state,
clique-count, harmonic representative, kernel-weight or inverse-gap oracle.
Potential value lies in meaningful high-dimensional topological inference;
ordinary low-dimensional geometry may already have efficient classical tools.

**Route screened.** Prepare valid simplices, simulate the sparse combinatorial
Laplacian, project onto its kernel and estimate its mass. This is an existing
spectral route, not a new operation. A normalized trace measures beta_k/D,
D=number of k-simplices; fixed raw additive accuracy may require precision
of order1/D. Efficient boundary evaluation and a gap do not by themselves
pay that mass or valid-simplex preparation.

**Exact structural falsifier.** The flag complex of the complete t-partite
graph with two vertices per part is the join of t copies of S^0. It has
2t vertices, D=2^t top simplices and top Betti number1. Valid top simplices
have a simple product sampler, yet uniform spectral mass of its one-dimensional
top harmonic space is2^-t. The topology is classically immediate from the
join decomposition. This defeats the claimed inference “easy simplex
source implies easy raw Betti estimation,” not every possible TDA algorithm.
It also prevents presenting a join/near-join family as unexplained advantage.

**Strong classical comparison.** Sparse boundary reduction/persistent-homology
methods, discrete Morse reductions, clique enumeration, collapses and join/
product formulas; actual low-dimensional geometry where applicable. No
catalog hit is evidence these methods fail. The needed NEW operation would
construct/read a useful raw harmonic witness without paying tiny kernel mass,
on a source-native family not already handled by those decompositions.
No such operation was specified, so this direction is not selected.

**Primary fetch before reconsideration.** Lloyd--Garnerone--Zanardi,
*Quantum algorithms for topological and geometric analysis of data*, Nature
Communications7:10138(2016); Gunn--Kornerup, *Review of a quantum algorithm
for Betti numbers*, arXiv1906.07673; Berry et al., *Analyzing prospects for
quantum advantage in topological data analysis*, PRX Quantum5:010319(2024).
Retained compendium LF4745--4763 identifies these actual sources, and
LF3188--3197 flags raw-versus-normalized output. That SECONDARY discussion
is a source pointer, not a newly verified theorem or a universal TDA no-go.
