## Decision

Prioritize one bounded core derivation for arbitrary Gaussian-rank-two copy
learning over another Legendre/OPI/DCP receiver search. The reason is the newly
specified covariance/pair-block operation, not chronological adjacency to the
two-Fock result. Admission remains conditional on a uniform approximate theorem.

but the bounded corpus check still supplies no polynomial native relation

cost. It therefore does not displace the concrete rank-two derivation now.

## Why the rank-two task has independent motivation

Actual local primary text `audits/sources/two_gaussian_learning/impurity.txt`,
Boutin--Bauer arXiv:2105.01088v1, Section II.C, equations (4)--(5), defines a
coherent sum of nonorthogonal Gaussian states and its Gram normalization. The
introduction and Section III motivate impurity-state approximation and describe
variational energy minimization; the text explicitly withholds a general global
minimum guarantee. This is external motivation for learning this representation,
not a claim that every impurity state has rank two or that rank-two learning solves
ground-state preparation.

The old two-Fock theorem has a common Gaussian frame and two orthogonal
occupation branches. It cannot be applied to a supplied decomposition into two
arbitrary nonorthogonal Gaussians without a reduction. Conversely, exponential
Fock expansion in one canonical frame is not an all-frame lower bound. The new
candidate has to establish its own full source and precision contracts.

## One decisive first question

Does the proposed covariance-plus-quartic pair-block extraction admit a uniform
polynomial-precision dichotomy: either it recovers an adequate approximate frame,
or the state is epsilon-close to an explicitly paid compressible/dominant-branch
fallback? This must include coalescing pair angles and near-cancelling Gaussian
components after normalization, without a minimum covariance gap or component
weight promise. Prove or falsify that statement before any implementation.

The pair singular-value identity communicated by the parent is a concrete
mechanism to test, not independently certified in this selection review.
Likewise, the exact degenerate-block operator and tomography interface still
need their assigned mathematical/source reviews. Success would be a copy learner
with classical reconstruction processing, not a classical-input quantum speedup.
