Hume checked two alternative algebraic routes:

1. For a nilpotent matrix algebra J with J^(c+1)=0 and G=1+J, module
   transporter equations `(1+X)A_i=B_i(1+X)`, X in J, are linear:
   `X*A_i-B_i*X=B_i-A_i`. Every solution is automatically invertible.
   The stabilizer uses the explicitly computable commutant algebra in J.
   Constructible conjugation labels do not establish a quantum advantage.
2. Principal congruence conjugacy over Z/p^(c+1) similarly reduces to
   linear congruences. Even stronger complete local GL-conjugacy information
   need not determine integral conjugacy, as the exact example below shows.

Take

    A=[[0,-5],[1,0]],   B=[[-1,-3],[2,1]],
    P=[[2,1],[0,1]],    Q=[[-1,-3],[1,0]].

Then A*P=P*B, det(P)=2, A*Q=Q*B, det(Q)=3. P is invertible modulo every
odd prime power and Q modulo every power of two. Every integral intertwiner
has form `[[2e-d,e-3d],[d,e]]` with determinant

    2e^2-2ed+3d^2 = ((2e-d)^2+5d^2)/2.

It is zero only at d=e=0 and never +/-1. Thus no integral conjugator
exists, despite local conjugacy everywhere. P and Q are NOT claimed to
lie in the principal congruence subgroup; this is a limitation on a proposed
global payoff even when stronger local answers are available. A global
class-separating label and constructive lift remain unpaid obligations.
The local-global failure itself is established mathematics; see
[Afandi](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.02130v1).

## Classical Shortcut Rejected

Hubble supplied an explicit counterexample to linear recovery from exact
finite-difference logarithms. Each generator's logarithm individually has
the correct invariant kernel, but their linear combination loses a genuine
joint symmetry. The issue is polynomial MODULE action length, not ambient
group class. The [note and exact controls](../../FINITE_DIFFERENCE_LOG_STABILIZER_FILTER.md)
preserve the correction. The family is easy by factorization and supplies
no hardness evidence. This is not a new quantum candidate.
