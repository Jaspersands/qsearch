                          5. Computing Unit Lattices
   Let K be a field, X = {x1 , . . . , xn } a set of indeterminates, and K[X] the
polynomial ring over K in the indeterminates X. Let f1 , . . . , fk ∈ K[X], and let
I ⊆ K[X] be an ideal such that the residue classes of f1 , . . . , fk in K[X]/I are non-
zerodivisors. This section is concerned with providing an algorithm for computing
the unit lattice and the associated character of f1 , . . . , fk modulo I. In the final
section of this paper we then show how computing the binomial part of a general
polynomial ideal reduces to computing unit lattices.
   The main idea is to reduce the computation to 0-dimensional ideals. Recall that
a subset U ⊆ X is said to be an independent set of indeterminates modulo I if we
have I ∩ K[U ] = h0i.

maximal independent set of indeterminates modulo I. Let σ be an elimination term
ordering with respect to X \ U , and let G be a Gröbner basis with respect to σ.
(a) The ideal IK(U )[X \ U ] is a 0-dimensional ideal.
