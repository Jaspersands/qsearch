## Four-move feasibility from native local data

Let F/Q2 have charged e,f, and let isometric ternary good BONGs have norms
(a1,a2,a3),(b1,b2,b3), common valuations(R1,R2,R3), R1<=R3. Work in local
squareclasses, with V the UNIT subspace and <,> the Hilbert symbol bit. Put
 C=-a1*a2, D=-a2*a3, m=R2-R1, n=R3-R2, x=b1/a1, z=b3/a3.
For a normalized squareclassd let defect(d) be its relative quadratic defect,
zero at odd valuation and infinity for a square. U_r consists of unit classes
with defect>=r, using ceil(r), allV forr<=0, and{1} forr>2e. Define scalar
 h(b)=min(e+m/2,m+b), k(a)=min(e+n/2,n+a),
 H0=U_h(defect(C)), Kx=U_k(defect(D*x)).
Rational determinant and Hasse matching give
 <C,x>+<D,z>+<x,z>=0.

Consider the word12(t),23(u),12(x/t),23(z/u). Its final tuple is
 (a1*x,a2*x*z,a3*z), so the middle target matches after one unit-root rescaling.
Allowed binary moves have two conditions, a defect filtration and a norm
character. The independent norm conditions are exactly
 <C,t>=0, <D*x,u>=<C,x>, <t,u>=<D,u>.             (1)
The final norm condition follows from the displayed Hasse relation. No
quartic norm equation or independent squareclass guess is being suppressed.

Enumerate a=defect(D*t), b=defect(C*u). Each ranges over0,...,2e,infinity,
so there are O(e^2) cases, not exponentially many classes. If D has odd
valuation, onlya=0 is allowed and its defect imposes no t-condition. Otherwise
its valuation is even, identify its unit squareclass, and impose
 t in D*U_a, excludingD*U_(a+1) whena finite; forinfinity impose t=D.
Apply the analogous rule toC,u,b. Infeasible parity/filtration cases vanish.

For fixeda,b, the remaining filtration conditions are
 t in H0 intersect x*U_h(b),
 u in U_k(a) intersect z*Kx.                            (2)
Combine(1)'s two LINEAR conditions with(2) and the exact-defect inclusions.
They give affine subspaces T,U, each with at mostone excluded affine coset.
If an exclusion is empty, ignore it; if it covers its domain, rejectthecase.
Otherwise express the forbidden coset by its defining linear equations.
Outside that coset means at leastone equation has the opposite bit. Branch
on one such equation forT and one forU. There are O(dim(V)^2) branches; each
has only affine subspaces and the single bilinear equation from(1).

Parameterize t=t0+A alpha, u=u0+B beta. The final equation becomes
 alpha^t M beta +l^t alpha+v^t beta+c=0 overF2.
If c=0, the zero parameters solve it. If c=1 and a linear coefficient is1,
set that coordinate to1 and the otherszero. Otherwise if M has a1 entry,
set its two coordinates to1. If none applies, the polynomial is constant1
and has no solution. This is exact deterministic polynomial linear algebra.
Thus the specified four-word feasibility and a witness(t,u) are computable
in polynomial BIT cost in nativeinput,e,f,precision. Also test the reversed
orientation23/12/23/12 by symmetry. No enumerationof2^f or2^(ef) is used.
