Analyze before C. Bell labels v=(x,z) in F2^{2n}, q(v)=sum_i x_i z_i, p=(0,1^n), ell(v)=[p,v]=sum_i x_i. Let B={(x_i,z_i) != (1,1) for every i}; base Bell law mu is a product with local probabilities I=1/2,X=Z=1/4,Y=0. Direct identical-copy Bell expansion gives the proposed exact law:
 nu(v)=mu(v) if ell(v)=1;
 nu(v)=(mu(v)+mu(v+p))/2 if ell(v)=0.
Thus S=supp(nu)=(B union(B+p)) intersect {q=0}. Full-swap parity q=0 cannot be discarded. In particular S is NOT p-periodic. Unknown C relabels v by an invertible affine symplectic map preserving q; p transforms by its linear part.
For i<j, cubics E_ij=x_i*x_j*(z_i+z_j) vanish on S. Together with q, they cut S exactly over F2: active x positions must have common z; q rules out z=1 when their count is odd. Degree is Boolean algebraic normal form, x_i^2=x_i.

Universal span q and q times affine forms is always contained and must be quotiented/handled; it is NOT extra structure.
