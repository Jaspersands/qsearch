4.2   From Free to Contact-Repulsive Magnons
We construct now a quantum circuit that maps XX eigenstates into purely magnonic eigenstates
of the folded XXZ model, realizing the unitary U0 we detailed in Subsection 3.1:
                                                                                                      
                                              |ΨM,0 ⟩ = U0 |ΦM ⟩N −M +1 ⊗ |0⟩⊗M −1 .                                               (4.2)

This transformation introduces a contact repulsion among magnons by shifting free-magnon
positions according to (3.15). Recall that we focus on folded XXZ eigenstates with the non-
dynamical boundary spins at |0⟩, such that |ΨM,0 ⟩ denotes the state of the N bulk spins of the
chain. The qubit requirements of the circuit are the following.

  • A physical register Rphys with N qubits initialized in the state |ΦM ⟩ ⊗ |0⟩⊗M −1 .

  • An auxiliary register Raux with M + 1 qubits initialized in |1⟩|0⟩⊗M , which keeps track of
    how many magnons have been already shifted.
