## Native question, selection and baselines

Input: a number field L/Q of arbitrary growing degree n, given by an explicit
integral basis and multiplication table for its MAXIMAL order O_L, and a
binary rational a. The field/maximal-order representation is a promise;
no Galois closure, class group, units, target solution or auxiliary fields
are supplied. For a=0 return0; below a!=0. For n=1 return a.
Output either NO or a polynomial-size COMPACT power product x in L with
N_(L/Q)(x)=a. With probability at least1-eta, the answer is correct. Positive
outputs admit a classical exact compact-norm certificate. NO depends on the
complete-basis success event. Target time is polynomial in explicit input
bit length, n, log|disc L|, bit length of a, and log(1/eta), under GRH.
Expanded coordinates of x need not have polynomial bit length.

The key question is whether the auxiliary Galois closure in the general
rational norm method can be replaced by a polynomial-size support bound.
Selection followed STATE/TRAPS, fresh corpus been-tried then literature and
baseline lookups. Independent RLWE/PIP/unit balancing repeats the already
falsified determinant-ideal shortcut; tensor-isomorphism and cycle-code
routes supplied no new paid receiver beyond known classical methods.
No further quantum-copy learner extension was selected by inertia.

Actual Biasse--Song arXiv2510.02280v2 Theorem1 gives compact S-units in
quantum polynomial time at growing degree. Its Section8.6 explicitly limits
its direct rational norm composition to GALOIS extensions and says the general
Simon method uses a potentially exponential-degree Galois closure. This is a
source-scope observation, NOT a priority or novelty certificate. Denis Simon,
Math.Comp.71(239):1287--1305 (2002), and Fieker--Jurk--Pohst66(217):399--410
(1997) are prior norm solvers. Their mere mention does not prove applicability
of a polynomial clock in growing degree.

The proposed support reduction itself is CLASSICAL. The matched classical
algorithm uses exactly the same reduction with classical S-unit/class
arithmetic and integer factoring; index-calculus/subexponential arithmetic
remains applicable under its hypotheses. No classical lower bound is claimed.
Quadratic rational norms also have the stronger conic/factoring comparator.
The intended improvement is an end-to-end polynomial growing-degree quantum
upper bound without constructing the closure, using existing primitives.

## 1. Paired relative norms preserve the absolute norm

For PROOF ONLY decompose the finite etale algebra

    L tensor_Q L = product_j E_j.

There are at most n fields, total degree n^2. Each E_j has two embeddings
 iota_1,iota_2:L->E_j.
For b in E_j^*, define the element of L

    y_j(b) = iota_1^-1 N_(E_j/iota_1(L))(b)
             / iota_2^-1 N_(E_j/iota_2(L))(b).

Transitivity gives N_(L/Q)(y_j(b))=N_(E_j/Q)(b)/N_(E_j/Q)(b)=1 EXACTLY,
including signs. These are not rationally diagonalized quaternion norms.
No independent evaluation oracle for unknown elements is assumed.

## 2. The support theorem, unconditional as a mathematical statement

Let S be any finite set of rational primes containing every prime ramified
in L. Assume the ORDINARY class group of each E_j is generated by primes
above S. Then

    N_(L/Q)(L^*) intersect Z[S^-1]^*
       = N_(L/Q)(O_L[S^-1]^*).                              (1)

Proof. Take x with N(x) supported in S. At p outside S,

    L tensor Q_p = product_i K_(f_i),
    K_f tensor_(Q_p) K_g = K_lcm(f,g) ^ gcd(f,g),

where K_f is the unramified degree-f extension. Thus a prime Q of a tensor
factor over a pair of L-primes P_i,P_j has relative norm multiplicities
f_j/g_ij and f_i/g_ij, where g_ij=gcd(f_i,f_j). Its two norm pushforwards
have difference

    s_ij = (f_j/g_ij)e_i - (f_i/g_ij)e_j.                   (2)

Every pair occurs. The integral lattice generated by ALL these vectors is
ker_Z(f_1,...,f_r). Here is a direct integral proof. Put g=gcd_i f_i and
h_i=f_i/g. Choose Bezout integers b_i with sum_i b_i h_i=1. For any z with
sum_i h_i z_i=0,

    z = sum_(i,j) b_i z_j (h_i e_j - h_j e_i).

Each parenthesized vector is an integer multiple of s_ij, because g divides
g_ij. Conversely every s_ij has weighted sum zero. Equivalently localizing at
any prime ell and choosing i minimizing v_ell(f_i) makes its star vectors a
basis of the localized kernel. A SINGLE fixed star need not generate it
integrally, and unprimitive coefficients may lose saturation.

Since v_p N(x)=sum_i f_i v_(P_i)(x)=0, (2) expresses its outside-S divisor
as paired pushforwards of finitely supported FRACTIONAL divisors D_j in E_j.
By the class-group hypothesis, choose A_j supported above S so D_j+A_j=(b_j)
is principal. Norms of A_j remain above S. Consequently

    x' = x / product_j y_j(b_j)

has zero valuation outside S, and N(x')=a exactly. This proves (1).
Only ordinary class groups are used: no positivity or narrow-class condition
is needed because each correction has norm+1. The theorem asserts neither
that local norms are global norms nor that an integral preimage exists.
It remains valid unconditionally whenever its stated support hypothesis
holds. GRH below pays a uniform polynomial-size S; it is not part of this
support proof. The elements x,b_j and fields E_j are proof objects only.

## 3. Pay the support without computing any auxiliary field

Let D=|disc O_L|. The tensor order A=O_L tensor_Z O_L has rank n^2 and trace
Gram matrix T tensor T, where T is the trace Gram matrix of O_L. Hence

    |disc A| = D^(2n).

It is an order in product_j E_j contained with finite index in the maximal
product order B=product_j O_(E_j). Thus

    D^(2n) = [B:A]^2 product_j |disc E_j|,
    log|disc E_j| <= 2n log D.                              (3)

Neither B, E_j nor the index is constructed. The bound uses the maximal
order supplied for L and does not silently assume a power basis is maximal.

Actual Biasse--Song Section8.2 gives, under GRH, class generators of prime
ideal norm at most48(log|disc E|)^2. Use that verified constant48, not an
unverified12. Put b_D=bit_length(D), and choose the INTEGER cutoff

    B0 = 48 * max(1,2n*b_D)^2.

Let S contain every rational prime p<=B0 and every prime divisor of D and
the numerator/denominator of a. A prime ideal of E_j below the source bound
lies over such a rational prime. Equations(3) therefore guarantee ALL the
class-group hypotheses of(1). GRH is assumed for these auxiliary fields'
class-generator theorem even though they are not computed.

B0 is polynomial NUMERICALLY in n and log D, so enumerating primes up to B0
has polynomial bit cost. It is not a succinct exponential loop. Factor D
and a with capped checked Shor calls. Compute every prime ideal of L above
S from the explicit maximal-order multiplication table, using finite-algebra
radicals/factorization over F_p. At most n primes lie over each rational p;
log Norm(P)<=n log p. This avoids a false Dedekind-power-basis shortcut at
bad primes. All S-unit input parameters are polynomial in the native input.

## 4. One compact S-unit computation and exact norm-image arithmetic

Run the actual Biasse--Song complete compact S-unit algorithm on L and the
computed prime ideals above S. Its complete-basis success guarantee must be
used, not merely the return of independent elements. Theorem5, Proposition2
and compact-recovery Proposition7 pay precision and polynomial dependence
on log(1/eta). Cap output sizes and all clocks.

A complete free generating set modulo roots of unity suffices after adjoining
KNOWN -1. Every root of unity other than +/-1 has absolute rational norm+1;
N(-1)=(-1)^n. Thus no large torsion enumeration or roots-of-unity advice is
needed for the rational norm image.

Write each returned compact generator as a product of polynomial-size field
atoms to binary exponents (base2 compact levels give exponents2^k). For each
atom beta compute its rational norm by an exact multiplication determinant.
Its bit length is polynomial by determinant bounds. Do NOT expand the whole
unit or its norm, whose height may be exponential.

Construct a classical gcd-free basis for the numerators and denominators of
ALL these small rational norms and the target a. The resulting positive
integer bases >1 are pairwise coprime, so multiplicative equality is exactly
integer equality of their exponent vectors; primality is unnecessary.
Aggregate norm coordinates using binary compact exponents and keep signs
modulo2. This includes any atom factors outside S, which can cancel; dropping
them would invalidate verification. Alternatively the source's supplied
S-valuations give the finite norm rows, but the all-atom route independently
checks the output without trusting those coordinates.

Solve the integer linear system for norm exponents, with one sign row

    sum_j sign_j*z_j - 2t = sign(a).

The finite rows are over Z, NOT modulo2. Polynomial-bit HNF/SNF decides
membership and returns polynomial-bit z_j if solvable. A redundant COMPLETE
generating list is sufficient. If insoluble return NO; on the complete-basis
event equation(1) makes this a genuine no-norm answer.

If soluble return x=product_j epsilon_j^(z_j), retaining the compact atoms
and binary powers. A flattened signed power-product encoding stays polynomial;
no expanded coordinate output is promised. Independently recompute its rational
norm coordinates and sign using the same classical gcd-free method. This is
a polynomial-time exact certificate N(x)=a. No factoring of atom norms is
required; initial support factorization remains a charged quantum operation.

Allocate failure eta/2 to the complete S-unit computation and distribute
eta/2 among capped factoring/finite-field randomized calls. Exact coordinate
arithmetic and positive witness verification are deterministic. Total failure
is at most eta. Incomplete output may never justify a certified NO outside
this bounded-error guarantee. No repeated voting on noncanonical bases is used.

Cheapest decisive falsifiers: a missing primitive pair relation; a lattice
index overlooked by a fixed star; wrong relative residue norm exponents;
class principalization introducing support outside S; an exponential prime
list hidden by bit notation; loss of a norm sign or torsion contribution;
expansion of large unit powers; or prior source coverage of the same growing-
degree route. The new proof and resource audit address the first seven.
Priority remains UNKNOWN, not novel. No hypothesis on a field's Galois group
or on local solvability is substituted for the norm-image computation.

No integral norm equations, short/minimal-norm solutions, unconditional-GRH
removal, generic rational-point algorithm, new quantum primitive, classical
separation or executed end-to-end quantum solver is claimed. The target is a
compact rational solution of one absolute field norm equation.

Next concrete control: exact Sage/PARI non-Galois degree5 tensor norm identity,
