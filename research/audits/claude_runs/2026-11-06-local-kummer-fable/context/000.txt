# Proposed classical large-p local Kummer image algorithm — NOT YET VALIDATED

Input monic separable f in Z[X], degree d=2g+1, binary coefficients, rational prime p>d, error eta. Let C:y^2=f, J its Jacobian, L=Q_p[X]/f. Output a complete F2 basis of delta(J(Q_p)) in L*/L*^2 (norm-square subspace), using explicit local valuation/residue-square coordinates. Target poly(g, coefficient bits, log p, log1/eta). Genus grows; arbitrary bad reduction at this p is allowed. No cluster data, local points, source, complete group order or inverse advice. No statement for p<=d, especially p=2. This would remove one named local-source blocker in growing-genus two-descent; a full global quantum Selmer algorithm does not follow until other places and global arithmetic are paid.

Proposed mechanism:
1. Stoll Lemma6.1 and the trace/norm diagram say that images of C(K) over ALL extensions K/Q_p of degree<=g generate the desired image. Since p>d>g, every such extension is tame. For each ef<=g, construct the unramified K_f of degree f and K=K_f(pi_e), pi_e^e=p*u, with u running over residue units modulo e-th powers. There are gcd(e,p^f-1)<=e choices, hence polynomially many fields (duplicates harmless). Enumerating their unit classes needs factoring only e<=g, not p^f-1. Any tame extension is obtained this way by adjusting a uniformizer by a principal-unit e-th root. All degrees <=g are charged.
2. For fixed K, factor original f over K into fields E_i with distinguished root alpha_i; total relative degree d. Work on O_K only: Stoll6.3(3) makes x outside O_K trivial for monic odd degree at odd p. Explicit p-adic factoring/valuation arithmetic is to be paid by polynomial precision and standard OM/finite-field algorithms, not full splitting fields.
3. Traverse K-rational residue discs D(xi,pi^t). At a disc, each root field records v(alpha_i-xi) and, if integral, beta_i=(alpha_i-xi)/pi^t modulo its residue field. The exceptional residues z in k are those equal to a beta_i residue lying in k. At most d such residues; compute them without scanning k. Recurse only into those child discs. Some fractional-distance roots yield an exceptional child with no root; then all squareclasses are constant on that child and one test suffices. Distinct live discs contain disjoint geometric roots. With D0=v_K(disc f)=e*v_p(disc f), depth D0+1 suffices: a disc then contains at most one root; if one, Krasner/Stoll6.3(2) puts the root in K and all point images in that disc equal its Weierstrass-point image. Those images are added explicitly. Thus polynomially many discs, not |k|^depth. This needs a rigorous count/precision check.
4. On nonexceptional residues at a disc, all valuations of x-alpha_i are constant and all local squareclass coordinates are constants or quadratic characters of Norm_{k_i/k}(z-beta_i). Pulling any linear functional on the BASE L*/square coordinates back through Norm_{L tensor K/L} therefore gives +/- chi_k(R(z)), deg R<=sum residue degrees<=d, outside the exceptional set. The existence of y is determined by parity c of the Gauss valuation of f(xi+pi^t Z), and, when c is even, chi_k(F(z))=1 for a nonzero residual polynomial F of degree<=d. Odd c gives no generic points.
5. Key sampling lemma: for q=|k|>=256*d^2, ANY F2 linear functional that is nonzero on the span of the ACTUAL valid generic images is 1 on at least q/8 choices of z (uniform on k, invalid choices counted as failures). Prove using the quadratic character sums for F,R,FR. Square or square-multiple degeneracies must be handled exactly: on the valid set they make the functional constant, either irrelevant zero or all ones. Nondegenerate Weil errors have degree<=2d and the exceptional set has size<=d. If q is smaller, enumerate q<=256*d^2 values, polynomial cost. N=O(d+log(number_discs/eta)) independent residue draws per large-field disc then span its complete generic image, by a union bound over <=2^(2d) base linear functionals. No unconditioned p-adic rare-point assumption is used.
6. Evaluate only actual valid representatives x=xi+pi^t lift(z). Their delta images push to Q_p as (-1)^[K:Q_p] charpoly_x(theta). At a Weierstrass point alpha, use Stoll's modified unit alpha-theta + (f/(X-alpha))(theta) before taking the norm; never substitute a zero element. All arithmetic must have a polynomial p-adic precision bound. Candidate bound should follow from t<=D0+1 and monicity: the Gauss valuation of f(xi+pi^t Z) is <=d*t; disc(f) bounds root separation and Weierstrass derivative valuations. Do NOT assume this settles every precision or integral-basis cost; audit it.
7. Combine all vectors by F2 linear algebra. At odd p the target dimension is dim J(Q_p)[2]=r-1 for r irreducible factors of f over Q_p. If this dimension statement is actually verified, it gives a completeness check; otherwise use the proved failure bound only. Every admitted vector must be a genuine local point/divisor image. No finite success proves the algorithm.

Cheapest falsifiers: a tame extension omitted by the binomial enumeration; a generic-disc coordinate not captured by a bounded-degree quadratic character; a square-degeneracy giving a rare nonzero functional despite the claimed q/8 bound; exponentially many rootless branches; uncharged precision; misleading image generation from only Q_p curve points. Ordinary Stoll residue recursion and random polynomial-divisor search are the classical baselines. The candidate is already classical and does not claim quantum advantage.
