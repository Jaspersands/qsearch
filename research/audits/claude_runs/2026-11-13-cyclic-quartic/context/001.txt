# Parent-checked primary-source notes
These are scope summaries, not new theorem claims; URLs and exact locators permit later source checking.

Dummit, Dummit, Kisilevsky, Signature ranks of units in cyclotomic extensions of abelian number fields, Pacific J Math298(2019), pp285-298. https://msp.org/pjm/2019/298-2/pjm-v298-n2-p03-s.pdf . Actual Proposition3, journal291/PDFpage8: for m=2^a, a>=2, circular units in Q(zeta_m)^+ have maximal signature rank 2^(a-2). Following paragraph gives Hasse norm-minus-one circular unit (zeta_(2m)^5-zeta_(2m)^-5)/(zeta_(2m)-zeta_(2m)^-1), and its Galois conjugate signatures span. Over F2 only simple representation of a2group is trivial. This is NOT Weber class-number-one conjecture. A polynomial construction of the explicit units plus full signatures can pay real unit squareclasses; CM transfer and torsion must still be proved.

Sharifi, Iwasawa notes Chapter4 https://math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/notes/iwasawa-ch04.html . Actual Theorem4.3.3 states circular-real-unit index equals real class number for FULL odd-prime-power cyclotomic fields. Its displayed scope is odd p, not p2 and not arbitrary subfields. Do not transfer this to arbitrary binary prime conductor subfields without source/proof, nor treat a length-p cyclotomic product as polynomial in logp.

Simon, Rational points on conics and generalizations, workshop2005 notes by Stamminger edited Stoll, https://www.mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/workshop2005/ConicsSimon.pdf . Actual Section2: relative conic X²-aY²=bZ² is a relative norm equation; an S-unit target need not be norm of an S-unit without a condition on the extension's S-class2part. The short note does NOT prove a general polynomial BIT relative-conic algorithm. Rational Cremona-Rusin algorithm is separately fully checked in the supplied conic proof.

Biasse-Fieker-Hofmann-Page, Norm relations and computational problems in number fields, arxiv2002.12332v4. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.12332 . Actual Theorem4.18 is GRH-conditional polynomial reduction given a suitable norm relation and subfield S-unit input. It is not a free subfield-unit algorithm. The actual subgroup criterion excludes cyclic groups: a faithful one-dimensional character kills every norm element of a nontrivial subgroup, hence precludes any rational identity with those norm elements. For noncyclic2groups, even denominators cannot simply be inverted modulo2. No full decomposition algorithm is supplied by this summary.
