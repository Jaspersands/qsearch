Let L/Q be an arbitrary degree-n number field. Decompose the finite etale algebra L tensor_Q L=product_j E_j. Each E_j has two embeddings iota_1,iota_2 of L. For b in E_j^*, define y_j(b) in L^* as Norm_(E_j/iota_1 L)(b), transported back by iota_1^-1, divided by the analogous second norm. Transitivity gives Norm_(L/Q)(y_j(b))=1.

THEOREM TO TEST. Let S be a finite set of rational primes containing every prime ramified in L. Suppose for every E_j its ideal class group is generated by primes lying above S. Then
  Norm_(L/Q)(L^*) intersect Z[S^-1]^* = Norm_(L/Q)(O_L[S^-1]^*).
In words: every rational S-unit that is a norm has an S-unit preimage. No integrality of the desired preimage is required.

Proposed proof. Fix any x with norm a, supported in S. Outside S, choose rational p and its L-primes P_i with residue degrees f_i. Unramified Frobenius on X=Hom(L,M) for a Galois closure M acts in cycles of lengths f_i. Primes of all E_j over p correspond to Frobenius orbits on X times X. For any pair of cycles i,j there are gcd(f_i,f_j) pair orbits, each length lcm(f_i,f_j). A prime Q in such an orbit has two norm pushforwards to L whose difference is
  (f_j/gcd(f_i,f_j)) P_i - (f_i/gcd(f_i,f_j)) P_j.
These primitive pair syzygies generate the ENTIRE integer kernel of the row (f_1,...,f_r). Proof candidate: localize at any rational prime ell and choose an f_i of minimal ell-valuation. In the i,j relation the j coefficient f_i/gcd(f_i,f_j) is an ell-adic unit, so eliminate all other coordinates; the weighted-zero condition kills the i coordinate. The generated lattice has full rank and is saturated at every ell, hence equals the kernel over Z.

Because Norm(x) is supported in S, the outside-S divisor of x lies in these kernels. Assemble finitely supported fractional divisors D_j in E_j whose paired norm pushforward equals div(x) outside S. The class-group hypothesis gives a divisor A_j supported over S with D_j+A_j principal, say (b_j). Then x / product_j y_j(b_j) has zero valuation outside S and unchanged absolute norm. This proves the theorem. Galois closure and arbitrary x are used only in the proof, not algorithmically constructed.
