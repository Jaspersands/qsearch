# Admission candidate: compact isotropic vectors over growing-degree number fields
Native input: number field K of arbitrary charged degree n, explicit defining polynomial/maximal-order basis and multiplication table, and a nondegenerate symmetric d-by-d matrix Q over K in expanded polynomial-bit coordinates. d grows. Output NO if anisotropic, otherwise a NONZERO isotropic vector represented by polynomial-size arithmetic circuits over the supplied K basis. Arithmetic-circuit output and a charged bounded-error classical verifier are explicit parts of THIS contract; expanded short/integral points, a compact Witt decomposition with repeated compact-input calls, and cryptographic short witnesses are NOT claimed. Target bounded-error quantum polynomial BIT time under EXPLICIT GRH, with failure eta. No point/classgroup/unit/splitting advice supplied.
Motivation: effective rational quadratic geometry over arbitrary number fields. This is a possible end-to-end reuse of existing Sunit/central-simple-algebra splitting machinery, not a novelty or separation claim. Strong classical baselines: rational forms factoring algorithms (dimension>=5 can avoid factoring); quadratic-base special algorithms; general global-field norm/squareclass algorithms, index calculus, LLL. Actual Koprowski2111.08569v2 has a prime-enlargement loop and local weak-approximation algorithms; its AppendixA inverse-product norm bound does NOT by itself prove polynomial BIT iterations. Do not infer actual exponential runtime from this weak bound either.
Proposed chain: diagonalize expanded Q; decide local/global isotropy using paid factoring/sign/Hilbert computations. For d>=6 choose one explicit5dim restriction indefinite at every real place via simultaneous weak approximation, with polynomial-bit coefficients; at finite places every5form is isotropic. Thus avoid recursive coefficient-height blowup. For dimension5 choose local binary values c=a4*s²+a5*t² making <a1,a2,a3,c> locally isotropic at each bad place, then finite/real weakapprox preserves these local squareclasses and signs with polynomial bit size. The remaining ternary/quaternary isotropic form is handled by existing under-GRH quantum splitting of a quaternion algebra over K or K(sqrt(discriminant)). Outputs of that splitting are compact; no unjustified expansion.
For q4=<a,b,c,d>, D=a*b*c*d. Over L=K(sqrt D), h=sqrt D/(b*c), quaternion B=(-b/a,-c/a)_L, and x maps to X=x1+x2*i+x3*j+h*x4*i*j, with q(x)=a*Nrd(X). If D is square use L=K. When q is isotropic, B splits. A compact splitting rho:B->M2(L) produces a maximal isotropic L-plane W={x:rho(X)e1=0}. For nonsquare D, conjugation exchanges the two quadric rulings, so W intersect sigma(W) should be a1dim Galoisstable line and yield a K-isotropic vector via projective normalization. Need exact derivation and paid compact linear algebra, not source advice. For square D pick any nonzero vector of W. q3 can use its split quaternion/conic correspondence or q4 padding witha zero coordinate carefully.
Each compact field operation has polynomial-size DAG. Exact zero/nonzero tests for these circuits can be done with charged bounded-error modular evaluation: clear conceptual rational numerators/denominators; height BIT bounds exponential in circuit size, hence logarithms of those bit bounds polynomial. Random primes with polynomial bit length avoid all nonzero numerator/denominator norms with high probability. Evaluate in FULL K modp algebra (not an assumed prime field embedding) and use determinant invertibility. This is a randomized verifier, not deterministic expansion or PosSLP.
