# One operation to remove the character-reconstruction factorial

Research question: can a uniform quantum algorithm recover an unknown monic
squarefree degree-d Q over F_p in poly(d,log p), from the coherent BIT oracle
for F(x)=chi(Q(x)) with chi(0) padded to +1? p is odd, binary, and may be
arbitrarily large compared with d. Output Q's coefficients, with bounded
error and public statistical verification. This is a native published
learning problem, not an invented oracle separation. No field-value,
extension-field, factor, root or target-dependent reflection advice exists.
Fresh pure phase copies are available; the chosen-BIT oracle is the stronger
permitted model. Keep any use of its inverse/coherent control explicit.

The attached notes give the existing uniform exp(O(d log d)) algorithm and
two precise failed improvements. Do not rederive them. We need ONE genuinely
different, paid operation that plausibly removes the factorial bottleneck,
not generic PGM/QSVT/Schur-transform/adiabatic terminology.

An avenue to consider critically is replacing the completely split masking
polynomial by masks with nonsplit factors of small degree, or a collective
transform that combines the factor-pattern sectors. The split source's
permutation garbage is ALREADY unprepared in polynomial time. The factorial
comes from support density, not from enumerating permutations. Extension
character values cannot simply be supplied. A generic coefficient shift
changes the unknown resultant phase. A norm polynomial is proof-only until
its sign can actually be obtained from the original BIT oracle.

Try to construct a new operation, or disprove one precise attempted operation
by an explicit counterexample/resource bound. A useful answer may be that no
operation survives. Do not claim a general impossibility from a failed route.
The strongest classical comparators include full-table poly(p) reconstruction,
empirical exhaustive coefficient search, and the attached polynomial classical
estimator for every chosen additive Fourier mean/candidate-subspace score.
Thus an additive-accuracy statistical-query decoder must face that match.

Give formulas, how each register is prepared/uncomputed, success scaling,
the single most important remaining proof obligation, and the cheapest exact
test. No source citations from memory should be treated as verified; the
parent will check new references. Do not rely on quantum query complexity
as a circuit bound. Keep the final report under 1800 words.
