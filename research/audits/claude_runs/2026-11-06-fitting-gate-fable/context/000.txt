## 2. Exact circular submodule, without a PID assumption

K is the real cyclic degree-ell field of conductor p, sigma induced by
zeta_p->zeta_p^g. U=O_K^*/{+1,-1}, R=Z[zeta], with zeta acting as sigma.
U is invertible rank one over the Dedekind ring R, not assumed free.
The norm operator kills both U and Cl(K). For ANALYSIS only, set

    alpha=Norm_(Q(zeta_p)/K)(1-zeta_p),
    c=Norm_(Q(zeta_p)^+/K)
       [zeta_p^((1-g)/2)(1-zeta_p^g)/(1-zeta_p)],
    delta=c²=sigma(alpha)/alpha,   C=R*c.                 (1)

The half exponent is modulo p. No degree-(p-1) computation is authorized.
For j=1,...,ell-1 define

    T_j(w)=sum_(t=0)^(ell-1) zeta^(jt) log|sigma^t(w)|.

The same Fourier expansion as in the cubic note gives

    T_j(c)=(1-zeta^(-j))*tau(chi^j)*L(1,bar chi^j)/2.      (2)

All modes are nonzero. Circulant cofactors give

    R_C=(1/ell)*product_(j!=0)|T_j(c)|
       =p^((ell-1)/2)*product_(j!=0)L(1,chi^j)/2^(ell-1)
       =h_K R_U.

Thus C is free rank one over R and[U:C]=h_K. The exact SAME-action identity
is

    C=Fitt_R(Cl(K))*U.                                   (3)

For every rational prime q not dividing2ell, All2015v2 Theorem1.2(d=1)
gives equality of character-component orders, hence local lengths. At
q=p its ramification hypothesis holds because e=ell<p-1; elsewhere e=1.
At q=ell, R has only one prime, so the scalar index fixes its length.

At q=2 use the ACTUAL intermediate formula in Greither1992, proof of
Corollary4.15, printed p496. K is unramified at2 and its degree ell is
odd, so the Sinnott correction lattices agree over Z2. With e' the
nontrivial-character idempotent, the formula is

    [U_2/C_small,2]=[Cl(K)_2]+[e' F2[C_ell]]              (4)

in the Grothendieck group of finite Z2[C_ell]-modules. Prime-conductor
norm distribution gives C_small=2C modulo signs. Since C is free rank
one, C/2C=R/2R=e'F2[C_ell]. Subtract this term using the exact sequence
for C/2C inside U/2C. Equality of lengths follows at EVERY prime of R
above2, even when2 splits into several primes. No identification with
Greither's larger circular-unit convention is imported. Over a DVR an
invertible rank-one module with finite quotient satisfies
C=Fitt(U/C)U; the local identities prove(3) globally.

Sources: [All v2, Theorem1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01578) and
[Greither1992, pp495--497](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.1299.pdf).
Independent source review ACCEPTED; this is equality of Fitting ideals,
not an isomorphism of class modules or a free-generator algorithm.
