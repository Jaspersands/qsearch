# Candidate: two unknown Fock strings under a Gaussian unitary

UNREVIEWED working claim, not established by this packet. Current repo state
has completed general sparse algebraic-constant lattice and parked hidden
cubic Clifford learning. This is an independent copy-learning problem.

Native input: independent copies of one exact parity-definite n-mode pure
state psi=G(alpha|x>+beta|y>), x,y distinct unknown occupation strings of the
same parity, G an unknown even fermionic Gaussian unitary. Known physical
Majorana coordinates/Jordan-Wigner encoding; arbitrary polynomial quantum
circuits and single-copy Pauli measurements allowed. No G, inverse, branch
bits, moments or minimum amplitude supplied. Output a polynomial preparation
circuit within trace distance epsilon, failure eta. No classical separation
claim: supplied G,x,y admits Gaussian/Pfaffian simulation. Aim is polynomial
copy/time versus unrestricted exponential tomography.

Let k=Hamming(x,y), p=|alpha|². For k>=6, quadratic and quartic Majorana
operators have zero cross matrix elements. Choose covariance convention so
pure branch covariances J0,J1 square to -I. They commute. Put
J=(J0-J1)/2, P=-J² (rank2k), B=(J0+J1)/2. Then B is a complex structure on
Pperp and zero on P; C=B+(2p-1)J.

Write W(J)_abcd=Jab Jcd-Jac Jbd+Jad Jbc. Quartic moment tensor T (with its
usual minus sign relative to raw four-Majorana expectation) satisfies
K=T-W(C)=a W(J), a=4p(1-p). Regard antisymmetric matrices X as vectors with
inner product sum_(a<b) Xab Yab = Tr(X^T Y)/2. Flatten W on pair indices:
L_J(X)=<J,X>J+J X J. On the active 2k-space it has unique top eigenvalue k-1
with eigenvector J, eigenvalues +/-1 on the orthogonal complement; outside
active pairs it is zero. Thus top K eigenvector is J/sqrt(k) up to sign,
gap a(k-2). Check this formula/sign carefully.

Candidate algorithm: estimate O(n^4) two/four-point Pauli means to additive
delta by independent measurements, form K, diagonalize O(n²)-square matrix.
Loop all even k>=6 if needed. Scale top eigenvector by sqrt(k), round its
largest 2k singular subspace to a rank2k orthogonal complex structure Jhat.
On its complement round projected C to a complex structure Bhat. These
commute exactly. Their Bhat +/- Jhat pure Gaussian branches have one common
known normal-form frame and differ in all k active bits. Undo that learned
frame on fresh copies, estimate the unnormalized 2x2 density block on those
two Fock strings with bounded observables (no free postselection), return
its top pure eigenvector and the frame. All conditional failures retained.

Uniformity plan: if min(p,1-p)<=epsilon²/10000, covariance polar rounding
already approximates the dominant Gaussian branch. Otherwise a is at least
inverse polynomial in epsilon, so fourth-tensor and active-space gaps are
paid; delta=epsilon^10/[10^20(n+1)^10] is an intentionally conservative
polynomial choice, not yet proved sufficient. For k<=4, use the existing
fixed-4 Gaussian-compressible learner (Mele/Herasymenko PRXQuantum6.010319)
or derive its elementary covariance compression argument. Odd total parity
can be detected and toggled with fixed X1, then restored on output.

Generate the Gaussian candidate, the 4-compressible candidate, and the
O(n) rounded spectral candidates. Select by fresh-copy fidelity estimates
(apply each inverse preparation and measure all zero), error O(epsilon²),
paying polynomial copies and confidence. No test is an all-n proof.

Useful elementary stability: for pure Gaussian covariances Gamma,Gamma',
the expected excitation count of one in the other's vacuum frame is
||Gamma-Gamma'||_F²/8, hence their infidelity is at most that quantity.
Approximate both orthogonal branches => target is near their learned span;
fresh two-dimensional tomography then learns the missing global cat phase.
