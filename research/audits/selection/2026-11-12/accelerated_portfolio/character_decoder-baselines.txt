  1. Information-set decoding with pinning and Fourier enumeration  [isd-pinning-fft]  score 0.2
     why: matched syndrome decoding; no nearby note names it
     Fix (pin) a few coordinates of the error, then enumerate a small block of it, or score every
     candidate on that block at once with a Walsh-Hadamard or F_p fast Fourier transform: Stern and
     Dumer information-set decoding, and the transform step of BKW-style LPN solvers. A better
     exponent than plain Prange, so it is the fair comparison when a quantum decoder has only been
     measured against Prange.
     references: research/AGENT_HANDOFF.md

  2. Prange information-set decoding  [prange-isd]  score 0.2
     why: matched syndrome decoding; no nearby note names it
     Choose a random information set, solve the parity checks on it by Gaussian elimination and keep
     the candidate when the remaining coordinates have low weight or satisfy enough checks; repeat.
     The generic decoder any quantum decoding, DQI or Gibbs-sampling route must beat. The referenced
     notes match product-USD quantum decoders with Prange-style classical samplers and measure BPQM
     against the Prange rank ceiling.
     references: research/PARITY_BLOCK_USD_PRANGE.md
