D.1. Graph Cloning
We begin with the subroutine G RAPH -C LONE which produces several independent samples from
a planted subgraph problems given a single sample. This procedure is a simple generalization of
PDS-C LONING in Section 10 of Brennan et al. (2018) and can be viewed as an exact variant of a
simple multivariate rejection kernel to products of Bernoulli random variables.
