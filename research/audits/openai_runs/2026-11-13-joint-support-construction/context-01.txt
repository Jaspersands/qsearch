# Joint norm constraints: a specific support question

13 November2026. NEW proposed constructive route, NOT an admitted algorithm.
The previous arbitrary single relative norm solver is closed and is not
reopened. Independent comparisons rejected new Kronecker LR-sampler work
(existing output-sensitive classical algorithm), generic class-two group
frame alignment (decoder absent), and operator-spreading wrappers (known
simulation without a new operation). A scalar norm recheck found the
existing completed solver. The question here instead concerns TWO norms of
ONE element, a native arithmetic-torus problem with a distinct joint kernel.

Input: explicitly represented linearly disjoint number fields A,B over Q,
L=A tensor_Q B a field, maximal-order tables and embeddings all supplied and
charged, expanded targets a in A*,b in B*. Growing degrees d,e,N=de.
Output NO or a polynomial-size compact power product x in L* with
N_(L/A)x=a AND N_(L/B)x=b. No normal closure, support, unit/class-group,
solution or torus-resolution advice. Norms to Q must agree; that necessary
condition is not assumed sufficient. Integral/short output is not requested.

Mechanism: find a polynomial-size support S that contains a joint solution
whenever one exists, then use the already checked quantum complete S-unit
primitive and exact atom-relation/SNF endpoint for both norm maps together.
Potential clock poly(explicit input) under explicitly charged GRH; not proved.
Existing classical S-unit/index-calculus routines are the matched baseline;
no classical lower bound or Shor-level novelty is inferred.

Prior work: corpus been-tried/baselines on simultaneous norms/tensor support
found no exact new operation, and flagged index calculus and unrelated KLPT.
Actual official Magma handbook text418 SimNEQ was read: its initial S includes
ramification, target support and class generators of L; completeness is
stated for L normal over Q, while arbitrary fields may need more primes.
This is an actual algorithmic motivation, not evidence of hardness or that
no other literature solves the nonnormal case. The general single-norm
support theorem is in RELATIVE_NORM_TENSOR_SUPPORT.md. No already-complete
native scalar norm or an adjacent unit backend is being rebuilt.

## Concrete first proposal and cheapest falsifier

Use E=A tensor A tensor B tensor B (degreeN^2) and its four maps from L.
For each field factor, alternate the four relative norms (+,-,-,+).
Transitivity should make the result have BOTH norms1. If these correspondences
generate the full outside-S joint valuation kernel, the previous auxiliary
class-generator support proof extends with polynomial degree/discriminant.
The crucial word is INTEGRAL, not rational, generation. No normal closure.

Local test at an unramified prime: let X,Y be the Frobenius permutation sets
on A,B embeddings. L primes are orbits Z of X times Y. Norm matrix rows are
orbits in X and Y; a column Z has weights |Z|/|X-orbit| and |Z|/|Y-orbit|.
For every Frobenius orbit of ordered(x0,x1,y0,y1), sum the rectangles
 e_(x0,y0)-e_(x0,y1)-e_(x1,y0)+e_(x1,y1), then take the common per-point
coefficient in each Z-orbit (NOT the total coefficient over that orbit).
This is the integer column from the corresponding local E factor.
Compare its image with the saturated integer kernel by Sage Smith form.

Parent pencil calculation predicts a failure already for X=Y=one 2-cycle:
joint kernel generated by(1,-1), rectangle columns only even multiples.
For equal 3-cycles, an index3 Laplacian obstruction is suspected. These are
falsifiers of the ordered-rectangle support proof only. Galois norm problems
can be solved by other methods; they do not establish nonexistence of joint
solutions or impossibility of a polynomial algorithm.

A possible genuinely new repair uses signed unordered-pair correspondences
and norm-one subtori, rather than silently saturating the lattice. A local
integer vector in the saturation is not a globally available multiplicative
operation; taking an uncharged root is forbidden. Higher cycle correspondences
must retain polynomial total field degree and discriminant, not N! closure.
The next decision depends on the exact local cokernel and whether a paid
uniform repair exists. No quantum implementation before this classical test.

Proof obligations: exact both-norm preservation; full integral local kernel;
polynomial auxiliary total degree/log discriminant; GRH support bound;
complete S-units and full torsion; exact paired norm relations; bounded error,
output bit size, and original-input total clock. Falsifiers: integral index,
missing local types, exponential auxiliary representation, free root/advice,
or an already-applicable classical polynomial solver. No passing finite case
can establish the support theorem or a speedup.
