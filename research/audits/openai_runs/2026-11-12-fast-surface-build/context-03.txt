1 Introduction

and C ⊂ P2 a plane projective absolutely irreducible reduced curve over Fq and X its smooth
projective model and J the jacobian variety of X . Let g be the genus of X and d the degree of
C.
    We assume that we are given the numerator of the zeta function of the function field Fq (X ).
So we know the characteristic polynomial of the Frobenius endomorphism Fq of J . This is a
monic degree 2g polynomial χ(X) with integer coefficients.
    Let ℓ 6= p be a prime integer and let n = ℓk be a power of ℓ. We look for a nice generating
set for the group J [ℓk ](Fq ) of ℓk -torsion points in J (Fq ). By nice we mean that
