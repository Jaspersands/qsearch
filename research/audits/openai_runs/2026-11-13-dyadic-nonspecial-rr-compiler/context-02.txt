## Exact bounded Stoll kernel shape and precision repair

The following parent derivation passed independent mathematical attack;
the detailed addendum is CLOSED and the one new exact control is frozen/PASS.
Let n=deg(a), e=deg(d), epsilon=(-c)^n. Every bounded solution t=(u,v,w)
to Stoll(5.1)-(5.2) satisfies Q(t)=f1*u²-d*v²+epsilon*a1*w²=0.
For any two bounded solutions t,t', their sum is still bounded, so their
polar pairing also vanishes. This nondegenerate ternary form over k(x)
has no totally isotropic2-space: such a space would be contained in its
one-dimensional orthogonal complement. Hence all BOUNDED triples lie on
one rational line. This is NOT a claim about all unbounded solutions of
the congruences, which include independent multiples of their moduli.

Choose a primitive polynomial vector t* on this line. By Bezout, every
polynomial vector on the line is h*t* with h in k[x]. For each congruence
modulus m and residual R(t*), h must be divisible by m/gcd(m,R(t*)).
The lcm of these factors is a principal multiplier condition. Its generator
divides the multiplier of a minimum bounded triple; hence it is itself
bounded, and minimality identifies it with that triple up to a scalar.
Thus every bounded solution is h*t0 for the unique minimal monic t0.

If deg(w0)=j, the leading degrees of f1*u0² and d*v0² have opposite
parity (deg f is odd), so they cannot cancel. With n=2m this gives

    deg(v0)=m+j-e,  deg(u0)<=m+j-g-1;

with n=2m+1 it gives

    deg(u0)=m+j-g,  deg(v0)<=m+j-e.

Zero polynomials have degree minus infinity. Consequently exactly the
multipliers of degree<=g-j respect all original bounds, and

    ker(M)=t0*k[x]_(<=g-j),  dim ker(M)=g-j+1.
    dim ker(M_k)=max(0,k-j+1),  0<=k<=g,

where M_k additionally restricts degw<=k. This settles the report's
unverified kernel-shape question and identifies j from the full exact
rank. It does NOT make that rank decidable from unconditioned finite data.

Accepted Claude lemma: for an integral exact matrix with every nonzero
Smith valuation<=K<N, reduction of ker(M modp^N) modulo p^(N-K) equals
the reduction of the true saturated kernel. Smith coordinates give the
proof: constrained coordinates are multiples of p^(N-e_i), which vanish
modp^(N-K). Conversely every true kernel vector lifts. The bound also
makes all nonzero invariants visible and determines exact rank. Sharpness:
(p^K,0) and(p^K,p^N) have indistinguishable inputs modp^N and primitive
kernel vectors differing first at valuationN-K.

Monic normalization needs a correction to the Claude report. If primitive
v is known modp^P and its w-leading coefficient a has valuationlambda,
then v/a is generally only known to ABSOLUTE precision P-2lambda.
The term delta(a)*v/a² loses twice lambda when a different coordinate is
a unit. Example v=(1,p^lambda),v'=(1,p^lambda+p^P).
A primitive-vector argument boundinglambda by the largest nonzero Smith
valuation of an augmented matrix REQUIRES that matrix to be injective:
B=diag(0,1), v=(1,p^lambda) refutes it without that guard. At minimumj,
source uniqueness gives dimkerM_j=1. Appending the w_j coefficient row
