# New proposed parity-constrained descent (parent construction)
This is a NEW concrete operation, not another review of the same crude support recurrence. Attempt to prove or falsify it and derive a sharp total clock, granting charged relative conics with expanded outputs and explicit log-height bound. The general tower algorithm is still open; this operation specifically aims to avoid expanding the full F(S union E,2) basis at every new support.

For quadratic L=F(sqrt d), define a generalized task U_K(S;J_1,...,J_r): return a complete basis of pairs ([x],c) in K*/K*2 x F2^r with outside-S parity divisor v(x)=sum c_i J_i. J_i are explicitly listed finite parity divisors, fractional valuations taken mod2. Also give the projection to c and coordinate maps. Kernel at c=0 is K(S,2); dimension <=dim K(S,2)+r. Duplicate/dependent J_i are allowed and c must be retained even when x=1. Original squareclass task is r=0.

Proposed descent:
1. Push target divisors J_i in L down by ideal norm parity to F. At outside-S inert primes residue degree2 makes that norm parity zero; at split primes it is the sum of the two parities. Call U_F(S;N J_i), returning (a_k,c_k).
2. Intersect its coefficient space with the Hilbert-symbol norm conditions (a,d)_v=1 at S and real places. Outside S these conditions are automatic: inert valuations of a are even; at split places d is a local square. Let (a_k,c_k) now be a basis of that norm-compatible pair space.
3. For each, solve N beta_k=a_k t_k². The outside-S mismatch parity div(beta_k)+sum_i (c_k)_i J_i is pullback of a base divisor R_k: split parities agree since the norm parity matches, inert single parity arbitrary. Include beta numerator AND denominator support. Call U_F(S;R_1,...,R_s), obtaining pairs (gamma_l,u_l).
4. Map each pair to (alpha_l,c_l)=(gamma_l product beta_k^u_lk, sum u_lk c_k). Proposed image is exactly U_L(S;J_i), and kernel is the single line ([d],0). Pay coordinates of that kernel and remove it.
Converse: for any upper (alpha,c), its norm-tag belongs to first norm-compatible space; subtract a beta product with same norm-tag; square norm quotient is restriction of base gamma by trace identity. Its parity is exactly the mismatch R combination, so the second oracle supplies it.

Critical questions: Is step4 kernel exactly <([d],0)> when first pair space contains pairs (1,c!=0)? Is the whole upper basis complete with no hidden relations or coordinate oracle? At rational base U_Q is elementary signed prime-parity linear algebra. There are TWO lower-U calls, not one per outside prime. Along a branch the target count increases at most dim F(S,2), but source support/height is adaptive and must be charged. Derive the call and representation recurrence assuming polynomial-cost conics with log-height <=C log-height(input)+poly(N,fieldbits) for fixed C, then separately with only generic polynomial bound. Ordinary field-element coefficient BIT height versus absolute/projective Weil height must be distinguished. Do NOT infer a total polynomial algorithm from per-level polynomial bounds. If algebra is correct but conic/batching remains open, give exact reduction and strongest proved recurrence. One precise useful proof, not speculative alternatives.
