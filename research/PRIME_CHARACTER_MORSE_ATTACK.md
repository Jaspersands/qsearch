# Fixed-Input Quadratic OPI: Morse Certificates And An Explicit Classical Bound

2026-10-17. Local theorem application/derivation REVIEW PENDING. Standard
algebraic geometry and factorization, no novelty or quantum algorithm claim.

## What Changed

The [norm attack](PRIME_CHARACTER_NORM_ATTACK.md) solved saved mixed instances
and supplied an IID-population guarantee, but correctly lacked a fixed-input
trial bound. This pass supplies such a bound in an explicit large-field,
degree-slack regime. It does NOT infer it from the successful saved seeds.

The classical mechanism is an arithmetic progression of norm polynomials,
an exact tame Morse certificate, a monodromy argument and an explicit
Galois-closure twist/Weil count. No random input shifts are assumed.

[Bank-Bary-Soroker-Rosenzweig](https://arxiv.org/html/1302.0625), Theorem2.5,
gives prime/factorization statistics in polynomial arithmetic progressions
as the field grows at FIXED degree. That statement alone is not a uniform
growing-degree algorithm. Its Lemmas3.4/3.5 provide the generic critical-point
facts, and Proposition3.1 explains the specialization/twisting count. We use
an explicit one-dimensional genus bound instead of an unspecified c(n).

## Problem, Input And Expected Upside

The same explicit quadratic-coset OPI task: prime p, points1,...,N, degree<K,
public shifts t_i and two power-coset orientations with a verified nonsquare.
Strict and zero-including conventions are both supported. No hidden oracle,
field table, preprocessing advice or hard-state source is supplied.

Choose the MAJORITY orientation first, with group A, multiplier beta, and
opposite group B of size b. Set n=2*floor((K-1)/2). Require

    |A|<K, n>=4, n-b>=3, p>max(n+b-1,2N).

The prospective quantum advantage in this family was polynomial perfect
intersection at high rate. A classical expected-polynomial solver in the
stated large-field regime removes that particular opening. This is not a
dequantization of arbitrary oracle lists, low-rate OPI, all fields, or every
orientation pattern. The classical norm/geometry ingredients are prior art.

## Public Unit Progression

Interpolate T on A and let V_A vanish there. At each B coordinate exactly
one scalar tau would make t_i-T(a_i)-tau*V_A(a_i)=0. Choose tau among0,...,b
outside these at most b forbidden values. This costs polynomial work, not
a field-sized search. Put Q0=T+tau*V_A.

Interpolate f0(a_i)=(t_i-Q0(a_i))/beta on B, and set g=product_B(X-a_i).
Then g is monic squarefree and gcd(f0,g)=1. The base polynomial

    f_base=f0+g*X^(n-b)

is monic of degree n. Adding g*(a1*X+a2*X^2) preserves degree, since n-b>=3.
For a fixed resulting numerator f, every fiber

    F_v=f-v*g

has the same unit residue on B and remains monic degree n. If F_v is
irreducible, it is a constant quadratic-extension norm because n is even.
Write F_v=R^2-nu*Z^2, nu=opposite_multiplier/beta, and output

    Q=Q0+beta*R^2.

This satisfies both groups. Irreducibility means F_v has no Fp root, so the
existing norm-one Cayley rotation supplies nonzero strict roots when p>2N.
The rotation now supports either first orientation; it never changes degree
or the norm. Every final witness is checked by independent native Horner and
membership evaluations.

## Exact Geometry Certificate

For the rational function r=f/g, let

    W=f'*g-f*g',       d=deg W=n+b-1.

The implementation checks monicity, gcd(f,g)=1, squarefreeness of g, the
expected degree of W, gcd(W,g)=1 and squarefreeness of W. It also computes

    C(Y)=Res_X(W(X), f(X)-Y*g(X)).

C has degree d. Its monic normalization must be squarefree. This checks
distinct critical values over the ALGEBRAIC CLOSURE, not merely rational
critical values in Fp. Squarefree W alone is insufficient: the tests retain
an even rational function with simple critical points but colliding values.

C is reconstructed from d+1 exact scalar resultants and an additional
specialization check. The degree bound makes interpolation exact. This is
polynomial-size work in n/log p, not enumeration of p parameter values.

## Why The Monodromy Is S_n

All d finite critical points are simple and have distinct images. Their
inertia permutations are transpositions. The b finite poles are simple;
infinity has one pole of order s=n-b. Characteristic p>n makes the Galois
closure tame, since its group is a subgroup of S_n and p does not divide n!.

The rational cover is connected, hence its monodromy is transitive. Take the
normal subgroup generated by finite inertia. Its quotient cover could be
branched only at infinity. A nontrivial tame cover of P1 branched at just
one point contradicts Riemann-Hurwitz. Thus the finite transpositions generate
the whole group. A transitive group generated by transpositions is S_n.

Geometric S_n also forces arithmetic S_n and constant field Fp: both groups
act faithfully on the same n roots. This is essential for the next count.
No Galois group or factorial-degree field extension is constructed by the code.

## An Explicit Fixed-Input Fiber Count

Write h=n!, d=n+b-1, s=n-b, R_branch=d+1. Riemann-Hurwitz gives the
Galois-closure genus G exactly:

    2G-2=h*(-2+d/2+1-1/s).

Twist that curve by an n-cycle. Over each unramified irreducible fiber the
twist has exactly n rational points, the centralizer size of an n-cycle.
Other unramified fibers give none. Weil bounds its total rational points
below by p+1-2G*sqrt(p). At most R_branch*h geometric points lie above all
branch values, including infinity. Therefore

    Pr_uniform_v[F_v irreducible]
       >= max(0,(p+1-2G*ceil(sqrt(p))-R_branch*h)/(n*p)).

The integer square-root upper bound makes the displayed rational lower bound
conservative and exactly computable. A sufficient condition for >=1/(2n) is

    p>=max(64G^2,4*R_branch*h).

This is a bound for a VERIFIED fixed f/g and fixed input shifts. It is not
the preceding IID-source population bound and uses no source-randomness claim.
Fresh uniform fiber coins can now legitimately be amplified conditional on
this certificate. Seeded replays are still not IID confidence certificates.

## Finding A Morse Map Uniformly

For the public a1,a2 family, W's coefficients have parameter degree<=1,
and C's coefficients degree<=n+d. C's leading coefficient is independent of
the parameters: W modulo g and its leading coefficient do not change.
Consequently the bad discriminant product has total parameter degree at most

    D_bad=(2d-2)*(n+d+1).

The generic critical-point/value lemmas cited above make that polynomial
nonzero under our hypotheses. Schwartz-Zippel bounds the bad-pair probability
by D_bad/p. If p>=2D_bad, a Morse map is found in expected<=2 uniform trials.
The code nevertheless CHECKS each actual map rather than inferring its
geometry from this expectation. Budget failure is UNKNOWN, not impossibility.

Combining conditions gives an explicit promised field regime

    p>=max(64G^2,4*R_branch*h,2D_bad), plus p>max(d,2N).

Then geometry search and fiber search have expected polynomial work in
N/log p. Although G involves n!, that integer has only O(n log n) bits; the
sufficient field threshold also has O(n log n) bits. Neither n! points nor
an n!-degree extension is materialized. Factorization uses standard randomized
expected-polynomial finite-field algorithms, not a hidden norm oracle.

This is a growing-degree LOCAL proof, not a fixed-degree asymptotic relabeled
uniform. Its monodromy, twist, genus and discriminant-degree arguments still
need independent mathematical review before accepted-proof promotion.

## Attempt To Prove It Wrong

- Shared poles, repeated critical points or colliding critical values invalidate
  the S_n/genus argument. Each has an explicit rejection control. Nonrational
  collisions are checked by the resultant, not missed by a rational-root scan.
- Wild characteristic invalidates tame inertia and the genus formula. The
  input characteristic guard is stricter than p>n.
- Arithmetic constant-field obstructions would invalidate the twist density;
  the geometric/arithmetic S_n comparison is part of the local proof.
- The centralizer factor is n, not n!, and branch points must be subtracted.
  The complete quartic control checks the direction of this inequality, but
  finite enumeration does not replace the proof.
- The field threshold is severe. Current61-bit large controls do NOT pass it;
  no numerical success is used to weaken it automatically.
- Fixed input does not mean arbitrary parameters: degree slack and group-size
  conditions are explicit. K39, general higher-power predicates, all field
  sizes and all orientation patterns are not settled.
- Actual code uses bounded budgets and reference libraries. The theorem is
  expected-polynomial under the stated promises; the records are not full
  certified bit-operation budgets. No new quantum or classical novelty claim.
