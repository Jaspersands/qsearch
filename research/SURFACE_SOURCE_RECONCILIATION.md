## Candidate Contract and Conditional Composition

**Problem and input.** Recover the abstract Lagrangian K=ker(phi) in a
specified split presentation End(E^2)/ell=M4(F_ell). Characteristic p>3;
ell is a distinct odd prime. Input includes a full evaluable integral
source quaternion order, original modeled principally polarized B, and a
genuine ell-polarized phi:E^2->B with weak evaluation at the globally chosen

**Mechanism.** For each M in Sp4(F_ell), construct a degree-one source lift
sigma reducing to M. Evenize its elementary coefficients and append a shear
congruent to I modulo ell, forcing t=tr_diag(sigma^dagger sigma) odd. With
S=sigma^dagger sigma, H=phi S phi^-1 is an integral positive symmetric unit
and H^2-tH+I=0. The uniform 157-factor bound supplies T>=t of polynomial
bit length. Choose one odd squarefree N>T^2 supported on polynomially small
primes, away from p*ell. Solve (t+2)X+(t-2)Y=N with X,Y>=0; four squares
and the eight-square identity construct C with C^dagger C=CC^dagger=NH8.
Then F=H8^-1 C^dagger satisfies F^dagger H8 F=NI and FC=CF=NI.
At each r|N, ker(F_r)=im(C_r) is a rank16 maximal isotropic subgroup on
B^8[r]. Weak masks preserve that subgroup. Prime quotients produce Y and
q:B^8->Y with u q=F for a polarized isomorphism u:Y->(B,lambda_B H)^8.
Transport marks by q([N^-1 mod s] C Z), not N^-2.

One common canonical isomorphism and one common automorphism across every
marking level, with independent common-unit minimization per level, gives
label(M)=label(M') iff MK=M'K. Use fixed marks with product R satisfying
R^2>8*16^3*ell^2. Stabilization changes the output dimension, not the acting
group. SYMPLECTIC_FIELD_CHART_REDUCTION.md then supplies the proposed
