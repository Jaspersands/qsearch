# Free Lazard Pullback: No Target Lie-Chart Acquisition

2026-10-22. Constructive input-reduction argument and public word compiler,
UNDER REVIEW. This simplifies a dependency of the proposed Rees algorithm;
it does not independently verify that algorithm or establish novelty.

## 1. The Input Bottleneck

The previous large-prime input conversion learned a complete additive
relation kernel, an actual basis of log G, and coordinates of all brackets.
Those were paid quantum preprocessing tasks. They were not free, and
checking some relations did not certify their completeness.

For the LOCAL EXPONENT-p problem, there is an alternative: do not identify
the unknown target's relations. Keep them inside the hidden preimage of
a larger public free group. The eventual HSP algorithm must recover the
whole preimage, including that kernel, before projecting the answer back.

Contract: known odd prime p, fixed class bound c<p, unique encodings,
identity and coherent public group multiplication, and s supplied
generators of an exponent-p group G of class at most c. Hidden input is
ordinary coherent f, or fresh IID rho_H copies. These group, generation
and source promises are not established by the compiler. Inverses are
public p-1 powers; no inverse of the unknown source is assumed.

## 2. A Public Free Domain

Let F be the free nilpotent Lie algebra of class at most c on s generators
over F_p. Its standard definition and implementation via bracket bases
are described in the [SageMath reference](https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/reference/algebras/sage/algebras/lie_algebras/nilpotent_lie_algebra.html).
Free algebras and their universal property are not new here.

One elementary construction uses the Lie subalgebra generated by the
letters in the truncated free associative algebra with words of length
1 through c. All right-nested bracket words span, by Jacobi. Select
independent original bracket expansions and compute bracket coordinates
using exact field elimination. Truncating words above c is legitimate
here because the intended object is the FREE class-c quotient; it is
not the invalid truncation of nonzero brackets in an arbitrary target.

If d=dim F, then d<=T=sum_(j=1..c) s^j. This is polynomial for fixed c,
not uniformly polynomial when c grows. The compiler enumerates words,
not p^d field vectors or group elements. Examples: d=5 for (s,c)=(2,3),
d=8 for (2,4), and d=30 for (4,3).

By Lazard correspondence, L=log G exists abstractly. The assignment
free generator i -> log(g_i) extends to a Lie homomorphism pi:F->L.
Its image is a Lie subalgebra whose exponential contains every supplied
group generator, so pi is SURJECTIVE under the generator promise.

No target coordinates are needed to evaluate pi. Evaluate each public
nested basis word using the universal BRACKET_c group words; evaluate
linear combinations with ADD_c and ordinary powers. These established
inverse-BCH ingredients are detailed in CLASS_FOUR_BLACK_BOX_INPUT.md
and REES_WHOLE_PROOF_SCOPE_AUDIT.md. The implementation uses the shorter
ClassFourGroupWords on c<=4,p>4, and the exact general compiler in
UNIVERSAL_LAZARD_WORD_COMPILER.md otherwise. The latter has no fixed
table cutoff: its public cost depends on c, possibly very badly. A genuine
opaque class-five matrix pullback is tested. No native quantum circuit
or general HSP receiver is thereby implemented.

The group homomorphism Phi=exp(pi):exp F->G is generally noninjective.
Its unknown kernel is permitted. Plain multiplication of powers of basis
images is NOT the additive Lie evaluation and can destroy this homomorphism.

## 3. Ordinary And Copy-Only Pullback

The ordinary oracle f composed with Phi hides exactly

```
K = Phi^-1(H),    k = pi^-1(log H).
```

It costs one original f query and paid public word evaluation. Compute
Phi(a) reversibly, query f, and uncompute that public workspace while
retaining a and the oracle label. No membership test for H or ker Phi is used.

For a copy-only input, prepare the uniform address over exp F. With one
fresh original rho_H, apply controlled right translation R_Phi(a), clean
Phi's workspace, then discard the target. The address entry at a,b is

```
Tr(rho_H R_(Phi(b)^-1 Phi(a))) / |exp F|
    = 1_H(Phi(b^-1 a)) / |exp F|
    = 1_K(b^-1 a) / |exp F|.
```

This is EXACTLY rho_K. Its trace is one, regardless of the kernel size.
There is no kernel-index success probability, inverse map, image-state
preparation, or many-to-one isometry. The operation deliberately retains
the address and traces a target; it does not erase an unknown index.
Leaving distinct Phi(a) workspaces behind changes the output and is illegal.

## 4. Recovery Still Needs Rees Homogenization

Apply the proposed explicit-Lie Rees theorem to the public F and hidden k.
Given the resulting FULL additive basis v_i of k, output Phi(exp(v_i)),
dropping identity images if desired. Their generated subgroup is H:
the v_i span k, their exponentials generate K by Lazard correspondence,
and surjectivity gives Phi(K)=H. If supplied generators only generate a
proper subgroup, the result is only H intersect image(Phi); that is why
surjectivity is a premise, not an inferred fact.

F is graded, but K need not be. In the Dixmier--Lister target, assigning
a,b,c,d to e1,e2,e3,e4 gives the kernel relation

```
[a,d] + [b,[a,c]] -> e7 - e7 = 0.
```

The two words have degrees two and three. After the natural free-algebra
dilation by s, its image is (s^2-s^3)e7, nonzero for s=2 at p>3.
For H=exp(span(e1,e5,e8)), this is outside H as well. Thus the natural
grading does not preserve even the hidden PREIMAGE. Do not skip the
second, positive Rees homogenization merely because the free domain
already has a grading.

Selecting one lift of each known hidden generator is also insufficient.
The explicit lifts a,[a,b],[b,c] project onto that three-dimensional h,
but their span is not bracket closed in F. The missing relations belong
to the kernel. The full preimage has dimension 25, including a 22-dimensional
kernel, in the 30-dimensional free domain. These dimensions are test
ground truth, not advice given to the public compiler or a recovered answer.

## 5. Resource Change, Not A Free Optimization

Let r_j=dim gamma_j(F) and m=d+ceil(log_p(3/epsilon)). The conservative
original source bound for this route is

```
Q <= c*m*product_(j=2..c) B_j(r_j).
```

Each core source uses c calls to the pulled-back oracle, or c original
copies after composing the controlled translations. No extra source
factor is paid for the pullback. The final Fourier and complete-instrument
precision budgets apply with dimension d, not dim log G.

At fixed c the public map has O(d*W_c*log p) multiplication cost per
evaluation, with polynomial-bit coefficient arithmetic and reversible
cleanup charged. Public basis-image setup uses polynomially many nested
words and no hidden-source reads. The implementation records its exact
setup multiplication count and a per-evaluation upper bound using the
selected backend's word caps. These are not native quantum gate counts.

IMPORTANT: d may be much larger than the target encoding width b. The
previous black-box numerical envelopes replacing all local dimensions by
b belong to the CHART-ACQUISITION route. They are not bounds for this free
route. Use its actual d (or T), and pay the larger tree and register sizes.
For (s,c)=(4,3), the free input has dimension30 and its Rees cover has
dimension76; the direct Dixmier--Lister chart had dimensions8 and14.
This is a simpler dependency structure, not an automatic runtime improvement.

This only removes LOCAL exponent-p target-chart acquisition. General-
exponent reductions, Sylow/normalizer computations, known hidden quotient
construction, small-prime HSMC and its corrected torsion step are separate.
Replacing exponent p by p-power exponent invalidates the free F_p map.
None of those remaining tasks is declared implemented or free here.

## 6. Implementation And Decisive Controls

- `theorems/free_lazard_pullback.py`: standard free-domain construction,
  opaque public word evaluation, one-query wrapper, projection of supplied
  recovered generators, and explicit resource/scope reporting.
- `tests/test_free_lazard_pullback.py`: free dimensions/Jacobi, genuinely
  opaque nonabelian target maps, 127-bit arithmetic, nonhomogeneous kernel,
  incomplete lift failure, redundant/identity generators, class-four matrix
  multiplication, and a wrong-exponent countercontrol.
- One actual bounded density/purification control implements controlled
  translation and target discard for a noninjective free-group map. It
  obtains rho_K with unit trace and exhibits the damage from leaked
  public evaluation workspace. This is not the full Rees receiver.
- `python -m theorems.free_lazard_pullback --write` records the standalone
  artifact in research/reductions/free_lazard_pullback.json.

No target relation kernel is learned by the compiler. Tests compute
reference kernels to verify it, then clearly distinguish those references
from operational inputs. The supplied-generator promise, coherence of the
eventual reversible implementation, and correctness of the separate HSP
receiver remain assumptions. No speedup or novelty flag changes.

The expected contribution is a shorter, less assumption-heavy proof
interface. The strongest baseline remains the old paid chart acquisition,
which can be far smaller and is still valid; already-known abelian/nil-2
algorithms remain preferable on their domains. This does not itself create
a new hard problem, natural application, or classical separation.
