# Subsystem 147: Quantum Wasserstein $W_1$ Distance & Dissipative Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Stabilizer Cooling Bridge

## 1. Mathematical Formulation
For an $m$-qubit syndrome register undergoing dissipative reset $L_j = |0\rangle_j\langle 1|_j$ at rate $\gamma$, the De Palma-Trevisan-Giovannetti Quantum Wasserstein distance $W_1(\rho(t), |0^m\rangle\langle 0^m|)$ contracts exponentially:
$$W_1(\rho(t), |0^m\rangle\langle 0^m|) = m p_0 e^{-\gamma t}, \qquad T(\rho(t), |0^m\rangle\langle 0^m|) \le W_1(\rho(t), |0^m\rangle\langle 0^m|) \le m T(\rho(t), |0^m\rangle\langle 0^m|).$$
