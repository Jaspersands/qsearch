require first computing an O-free basis or a principal ideal generator.

For the rank-eight trace lattice of our full unimodular Hermitian module,
det(G)=p^4 and every nonzero squared length is a positive integer. Apply
exact delta=3/4 LLL. Its Gram--Schmidt squared lengths satisfy

    r_1>=1,  r_i>=r_(i-1)/2>=2^(-(i-1)),  product_i r_i=p^4.

Thus every r_i<=2^28*p^4, and size reduction gives every basis squared
length at most a fixed constant times p^4. Reduce O similarly in rank four,
where its trace determinant is p^2. The quaternion Hermitian Cauchy bound
and fixed-rank dual-basis bounds then give p^O(1) coordinates for the form
and for multiplication by the reduced O-basis on the reduced module basis.
For clarity, inverse trace Gram coefficients are bounded by cofactors divided
by its positive integral determinant, so this assertion does not hide an
arbitrarily ill-conditioned coordinate change.

Use the eight reduced Z-generators as O-generators, with relations expressing
their products by each reduced O-basis element. This is a finite presentation
with O(log p)-bit coefficients: all additive relations are already recorded
by the Z-basis, and the action relations reduce every O-word to that basis.
Remark3.1 supplies the geometric converter's input without retaining the
large ell-dependent coordinates in its small presentation.
