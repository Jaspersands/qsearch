Priority 158

code-equivalence-hard-family-search

self-dual-wreath-growing-width-carrier-decoder

Why it matters: Code equivalence remains one of the plausible hidden-permutation settings where a new collective measurement could matter.

Next experiment obligation: Stop fitting fixed bounded-support separators. Build the average-frame operator in a uniform harmonic basis, then compile its inverse root into the first typed growing-width carrier-sensitive covariant outcome primitive under ordinary mixed coset-state access.

Key Kill Criteria:
  • Support splitting or canonicalization distinguishes every generated family.
  • Coset observable matches a known code invariant.
  • Low-weight codeword support hypergraphs or matroid incidence signatures separate the row.
  • The family has trivial hull and therefore reduces to weighted GI under public-generator access.
Priority 110

dcp-density-one-subset-sum-partial-solver

subset-sum-partial-solver

Why it matters: Density-one dihedral coset states present a critical testing ground for whether quantum walk memory can outperform classical sieves.

Next experiment obligation: Synthesize structural deterministic or explicit-coin randomized partial solvers near density one and demand a uniform inverse-polynomial legal-input coverage theorem, not finite success.

Key Kill Criteria:
  • Legal-input success collapses in the scaling tail or lacks a uniform inverse-polynomial coverage theorem.
  • The method enumerates only polynomially many explicit subset candidates with exponentially small coverage.
  • Distinguishability exists only through exponential subset-sum enumeration.
  • The robust full decoder matches or loses to named Kuperberg/Regev resource frontiers.
Priority 94

nonabelian-coset-collective-observables

surface-reduction-bound

Why it matters: Multi-copy coset states in nonabelian groups isolate the boundary between quantum Fourier sampling and classical commutant moments.

Next experiment obligation: Complete asymptotic growing-width bounds for weighted frame presentations outside the genus-two seed, followed by interleaved-leaf multi-boundary analysis.

Key Kill Criteria:
  • Commutant projection weight decays superpolynomially in target group dimension.
  • Racah recoupling coefficients concentrate around trivial scalar representations.
  • Observable distribution is efficiently sampleable via Weisfeiler-Leman invariants.
