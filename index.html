Featured Exhibit 01 • Commutant Geometry

Anatomy of an invariant: resolving degeneracy for $S_8$ and $S_9$

A concrete walkthrough of how Q-Search generated a non-trivial algebraic invariant, proved it against known collisions, and immediately mapped its physical falsification boundary.

Spectral Degeneracy & Natural-Access Obstruction

In non-abelian hidden subgroup problems on $S_n$, candidate quantum algorithms measure collective invariants of multiple coset states. The central question: Can an algebraic invariant separate all hidden conjugate classes without exponentially decoding the full copy register?

1. Commutant Observable Hypothesis

Hypothesis

Construct central invariants $T \in \operatorname{End}_{S_n \wr S_k}(\mathcal{H}^{\otimes k})$ to distinguish hidden conjugate involutions with an inverse-polynomial spectral gap.

2. The Two-Generator Collision

Collision

Standard two-generator invariants ($T_{C2} / T_{T1}$) failed at $n=8$, collapsing into exact spectral degeneracies on 2 known graph targets of multiplicity $\le 4$.

3. Machine Synthesis: Third Generator

Verified Invariant

Q-Search synthesized an independent third generator $T_{T1} + c T_{C1}$. Fourth moments proved simple spectrum for all $c \neq 0$: separating 20/20 targets at $n=8$ and 27/27 targets at $n=9$.

4. Natural-Access Obstruction

Dequantized

The reduction gate computed the exact natural-access sampling probability: $$P_{\text{natural}} = \frac{d_W^3 d_\xi \operatorname{Tr}(F)}{(n!)^3} \le \frac{25}{3}\frac{n^9}{(n!)^3}$$ Postselecting this branch from raw cosets decays factorially, ruling out generic amplification.

5. Epistemic Verdict

Gated Boundary

The finite algebraic separation is mathematically certified, but fixed-sector postselection cannot yield a speedup. A viable algorithm must adapt to typical high-dimensional partitions.

What Q-Search successfully killed

Negative results are first-class scientific assets. Here are three representative candidate shortcuts that Q-Search rigorously disproved with automated attack suites.

Dihedral HSP DHS-NEAREST-PLANE

Lattice Nearest-Plane Sieve Collapse

The Hypothesis

Nearest-plane lattice decoding on Gowers-sieve coset states recovers dihedral reflections in polynomial time.

The Falsifier

All-target censuses across $16.7\times 10^6$ assignments proved fixed-depth lists collapse exponentially at scale. Small-$n$ success was an artifact.

Code Equivalence CODE-GOPPA-SYZYGY

Goppa Code Syzygy Collisions

The Hypothesis

High-weight syzygies of alternant codes provide a quantum invariant that resists classical permutation attacks.

The Falsifier

An automated classical hull-projector reduction resolved the syzygy collisions in polynomial classical time, dequantizing the advantage.

Coset States COSET-NATURAL-ACCESS

Fixed-Sector Frame Postselection

The Hypothesis

Postselecting stable 3-copy coupling trees provides efficient binary coset state discrimination.

The Falsifier

Exact weak-Fourier trace proved natural sampling mass decays factorially as $O(n^9 / (n!)^3)$, ruling out generic amplification.
